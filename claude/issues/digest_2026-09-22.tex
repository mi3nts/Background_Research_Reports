\section*{\sffamily\bfseries\color{Deep}Signal of the day}
\vspace{-1.5mm}{\color{Amber}\rule{\linewidth}{1.1pt}}\vspace{2.5mm}

\begin{keybox}
{\sffamily\bfseries\small\color{Deep}Four hundred and five kitchens in Bukavu, one
PurpleAir each, and no stated correction: for the second day running, an African
maternal-health cohort rests its exposure on a low-cost optical sensor in biomass smoke.}\\[1.4mm]
\footnotesize
Zigabe et al.\ (\textit{Environ Pollut}) report the household-air-pollution sub-study of
the \textbf{MUTOTO} pregnancy cohort in \textbf{Bukavu, eastern DR Congo}: \textbf{405
households}, each with a \textbf{24-hour household PM$_{2.5}$ measurement by a PurpleAir
Zen} and \textbf{24-hour personal CO by a Lascar EL-USB-CO logger}, March 2025 to January
2026. Mean household PM$_{2.5}$ was \textbf{53.8\,$\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$} (SD 34.2), the highest
household mean \textbf{244.3}; personal CO averaged \textbf{5.3\,ppm} (SD 8.7), maximum
65.1. Biomass fuel and cooking frequency drove both, biomass use rose from \textbf{56\,\%
to 87\,\%} across CO exposure groups, and \textbf{96\,\% of kitchens had no chimney}.
\textbf{The exposure picture is credible and the determinants are the expected ones.}
What makes this the signal is the pairing with yesterday's tier-A record: the Butajira
fetal-growth cohort (21 September) ran on an \textbf{Atmotube Pro} wearable, and this one
runs on a \textbf{PurpleAir} --- \textbf{two consecutive issues, two sub-Saharan pregnancy
cohorts, two consumer-grade optical instruments, and neither abstract reports a correction
or a gravimetric co-location.} Fresh biomass smoke is the case where Plantower-class
sensors are least reliable: the factory calibration aerosol differs in size distribution
and refractive index, and high humidity near a cookstove inflates light scattering. The
direction of the error is not even fixed --- US-derived PurpleAir corrections
\textit{lower} readings in smoke, while mass above the sensor's effective cut is missed ---
so the 53.8 cannot be bounded from the abstract alone. \textbf{The CO leg is the useful
internal check}: electrochemical CO is far less aerosol-dependent, and the CO--PM$_{2.5}$
ratio across 405 homes is exactly the dataset that could calibrate the PurpleAir against
the combustion it is measuring. \textbf{A pattern worth tracking, not yet a trend}: low-cost
sensing is now the default exposure instrument for LMIC maternal cohorts, and the
calibration step is not surviving into the abstract.\par
\end{keybox}

% -------------------------------------------------------------- what changed
\section*{\sffamily\bfseries\color{Deep}What changed today}
\vspace{-1.5mm}{\color{Amber}\rule{\linewidth}{1.1pt}}\vspace{2.5mm}

\footnotesize
\begin{itemize}

\item \textbf{A Canadian multi-pollutant framework returns a coefficient no burden model
can use.} Huang, Brown and Shin (\textit{Int J Epidemiol}) fit a \textbf{nonlinear Bayesian
case-crossover} to PM$_{2.5}$, NO$_2$ and O$_3$ jointly across \textbf{20 Census Divisions}
(mortality 2001--2015, hospitalisation 2001--2018), \textbf{propagating the uncertainty of
imputed monitor data} rather than treating infilled days as observed. The clearest signal
is on the \textbf{combined PM$_{2.5}$+NO$_2$ metric}: \textbf{+1.5\,\%} non-accidental
mortality (95\,\% CrI 0.3--2.2) and \textbf{+2.7\,\%} senior respiratory hospitalisation
(0.9--4.5) per 10-unit contrast. The modelling is the advance; the summed index is the
cost, because \textbf{it has no single-pollutant counterpart} and so cannot be dropped into
a PM$_{2.5}$-only health-impact function. Imputed monitor gaps entering as exposure error is
the entry point for dense networks.

\item \textbf{A causal estimate of what PM$_{2.5}$ costs cognitive labour.} Dong, Qiao
and Zhou (\textit{Nat Hum Behav}) instrument annual city PM$_{2.5}$ (mean
\textbf{45.9\,$\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$}) against individual Chinese scholars' output:
\textbf{--0.23\,\% publications per 1\,$\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$} (95\,\% CI --0.28 to --0.17\,\%),
larger among younger and more productive scholars, with further analyses pointing to fewer
working hours, more hospital visits and weaker collaboration. The identifying assumption --- the
instrument's exclusion restriction --- is \textbf{not named in the abstract}, and annual
city means sit a long way from the indoor office where the work is done.

\item \textbf{Two instrument papers with full abstracts, both on the watch's own axis.}
A \textbf{Parallel Electro-inertial Particle Sizer} (\textit{Meas Sci Technol}) --- corona
charger plus six non-bouncing impactor/electrometer channels, 10--560\,nm --- tracked an
SMPS at \textbf{slope 1.023, $r$ 0.995} over a 16-day rooftop co-location, with sub-20\,nm
and complex multimodal structure the stated weak points and no drift data. And a
\textbf{PAX + integrating-sphere hybrid} (\textit{ACP}) apportions absorption in Beijing to
BC, BrC (\textbf{$\sim$10\,\%}) and \textbf{lensing, which averaged 40\,\% of total
absorption but swung 4.6--52.0\,\% by season}. A seasonally varying lensing term of that
size is precisely what a filter photometer's fixed mass-absorption cross-section absorbs
without comment.

\item \textbf{Individual-level mitigation reaches outcome trials --- and the first result
in the window is null on its primary contrast.} The \textbf{COATED-AIR} design paper
(\textit{Eur Heart J Cardiovasc Pharmacother}) factorially randomises ASCVD patients to a
\textbf{bundled mitigation package} --- SMS air-quality alerts, outdoor-activity advice,
KN95 masks, citrus --- against usual care, alongside an aspirin-formulation question. A
bundle cannot attribute benefit to exposure reduction unless personal exposure and
adherence are measured, which the abstract does not describe. Separately, an
8-week synbiotic RCT in adolescents (\textit{Int J Prev Med}) found \textbf{no between-arm
difference in malondialdehyde} (P\,=\,0.75) against an omega-3 comparator; the positive
reading appears only after covariate adjustment.

\item \textbf{The PubMed connector added what the harvester missed.} The harvester's
PubMed legs returned \textbf{10 health and 0 sensing} (the sensing leg's \textbf{third}
consecutive zero). Separate connector queries on the same \texttt{[EDAT]} day returned
\textbf{11 PMIDs the harvester's PubMed legs had not}; nine of them were new to every leg, and \textbf{3 were admitted}, including the
\textit{Thorax} APEAL record and the COATED-AIR design paper. Metadata-only fell to
\textbf{2 of 23 (9\,\%)}, from 28\,\% on 21 September, again with no change on the
retrieval side --- ACS, IOP, Copernicus and MDPI deposited abstracts; the two bare records
are one ACS and one Elsevier deposit. \textbf{The retry list is now 30.}

\item \textbf{Outcome metrology can erase an exposure contrast.} The APEAL cohort baseline
(\textit{Thorax}, 4,175 adolescents in four Indian cities) finds GLI-2012 places
\textbf{74.6\,\%} of healthy 11--14-year-olds below the lower limit of normal for FEV$_1$
(mean $z$ --2.44). Every South Asian PM--lung-function study using those equations is
therefore comparing groups on a scale where most of both groups read as abnormal. The
counter-risk runs the other way: a local reference derived from the same polluted cities
may absorb part of the effect it will later be used to detect.

\end{itemize}

{\fontsize{7.4}{9}\selectfont\color{Slate}Ordered by cluster. Each entry gives the
methodological core and the finding that matters, not an abstract paraphrase. Records
marked \textit{metadata only} carry no recoverable abstract and assert no finding.\par}

\band{Neuro / mental health\hfill 1 record}{Deep}

\paperentry{India-Aldana et al. 2026}
{The chemical exposome during peri-pregnancy, maternal sleep outcomes post-pregnancy, and the potential mediating role of body mass index.}
{Environ Pollut}
{About 393 PROGRESS mothers with 52 exposure markers (estimated air pollutants, metals, phthalates, pesticides, PFAS) in pregnancy or one month postpartum, and actigraphy plus NIH-Toolbox sleep outcomes eight years later. BWQS regression: a quartile rise in the air-pollutant mixture associated with about 8 minutes shorter sleep (beta -0.14 h, 95\% CrI -0.29 to 0.00); non-essential metals lowered sleep efficiency (-0.95\%, -1.81 to -0.17). No BMI mediation. The air-pollution result sits on the null boundary, the mixture's PM$_{2.5}$ share is not separable in the abstract, and a single pregnancy exposure window is asked to explain sleep eight years later, over which residential and occupational exposure will have moved. Hypothesis-generating. The long lag is where repeated personal-sensor exposure histories would add the most.}
{10.1016/j.envpol.2026.129220}

\band{Cardiovascular \& metabolic\hfill 2 records}{Teal}

\paperentry{Bikdeli et al. 2026}
{Coated or chewable Aspirin and a hybrid air pollution mitigation strategy in patients with atherosclerotic disease: Rationale and Design of the COATED-AIR Randomized Trial.}
{Eur Heart J Cardiovasc Pharmacother}
{Design paper for a 2x2 factorial randomised trial in patients with established ASCVD on low-dose aspirin: double-blind enteric-coated versus chewable aspirin 81 mg, and a hybrid air-pollution mitigation package versus usual care. The package combines an educational flashcard, SMS air-quality alerts, and on high-pollution days advice to limit outdoor activity or wear a KN95 mask and to raise citrus intake. No results. This is among the first outcome-powered trials of individual-level pollution mitigation, which is why it is tier B despite having no data. The weakness is built in: a bundled behavioural intervention cannot attribute any benefit to exposure reduction unless adherence and personal exposure are measured, which the abstract does not describe. The alert signal presumably comes from a monitoring network, so alert latency and spatial resolution are unreported design parameters.}
{10.1093/ehjcvp/pvag071}

\paperentry{Lin et al. 2026}
{Joint association of PM$_{2.5}$ and AIP with advanced Cardiovascular-Kidney-Metabolic syndrome in middle-aged and older Chinese adults: a national cohort study.}
{Research Square (preprint)}
{CHARLS 2011-2015 participants classified by median PM$_{2.5}$ and median atherogenic index of plasma, logistic regression for advanced (stage 3-4) cardiovascular-kidney-metabolic syndrome. High AIP was strongly associated; PM$_{2.5}$ alone showed no positive association; joint high-PM$_{2.5}$ and high-AIP exposure raised risk, with no additive or multiplicative interaction. Effect sizes and n are not in the abstract. The analysis is a median split of both exposures, so the 'joint' effect is almost entirely the AIP effect restated, and absence of interaction contradicts the framing. Preprint, not peer-reviewed. Carried for the null PM$_{2.5}$ main effect, which is informative in a cohort with a wide exposure range, and as a caution against dichotomising a continuous exposure that sensor and satellite products now resolve finely.}
{10.21203/rs.3.rs-10585989/v1}

\band{Respiratory \& allergic\hfill 2 records}{Sky}

\paperentry{Samdarshi et al. 2026}
{Systematic misclassification of lung function in Indian adolescents using GLI-2012 reference equations: evidence from the multicity APEAL cohort.}
{Thorax}
{Baseline of the Air Pollution Exposure on Adolescents' Lungs cohort: 4,175 healthy non-smoking 11-14-year-olds with ATS/ERS spirometry in four cities. Against GLI-2012 (Asian category), mean z-scores were -2.44 for FEV1, -1.34 for FVC and -0.78 for FEV1/FVC; 74.6\% fell below the LLN for FEV1 against an expected 5\%. The shift was uniform across sex, city, age, height and BMI. No PM analysis yet. It is carried because it bears directly on the outcome metrology of every South Asian PM-lung study: using GLI-2012 would label most exposed and unexposed adolescents abnormal, compressing contrasts and inflating 'impairment' prevalence. The counter-risk is that part of the deficit is itself pollution-driven, so a local reference set derived from these same cities may absorb the very effect later analyses seek.}
{10.1136/thorax-2026-225309}

\paperentry{Dou et al. 2026}
{Risk factors for allergic rhinitis in children: an umbrella review of systematic review and meta-analysis.}
{Front Immunol}
{PROSPERO-registered umbrella review of meta-analyses to May 2025, AMSTAR and GRADE appraisal. Of 75 candidate risk factors, 22 raised paediatric allergic-rhinitis risk, 7 were protective and 46 were indeterminate; air pollution sits among the risk-raising factors with antibiotics, caesarean delivery and prenatal smoking. Evidence quality was low or very low overall, and only early-life food sensitisation reached moderate certainty - so air pollution is on the list at low certainty. Pollutant, metric and pooled effect size are not given in the abstract. Umbrella reviews inherit the heterogeneity of their inputs, and ambient-pollution meta-analyses in children pool incomparable exposure assignments. A calibration of how weak the paediatric AR evidence is, not a new estimate.}
{10.3389/fimmu.2026.1849271}

\band{Reproductive \& developmental\hfill 1 record}{Sage}

\paperentry{Thirumalazhagan et al. 2026}
{A Comprehensive Meta-Analysis on the Impact of airborne Particulate Matter on Trophoblast Cell Function In Vitro.}
{Reprod Toxicol}
{Twelve in vitro studies from 1,506 records to September 2025. PM$_{2.5}$ reduced trophoblast viability by a pooled 20.7\%, disrupted steroidogenic enzymes (CYP11A1, 3beta-HSD1, FDXR), uncoupled hCG from progesterone synthesis, induced oxidative DNA damage with ATR-mediated p53-independent apoptosis, suppressed VEGF-VEGFR2-MEK-ERK-mTOR signalling and impaired invasion via TIMP, RhoA and collagen I. The central problem is that pooling relative risks of gene expression across studies with different particle sources, extraction methods, doses and cell lines produces a number with no exposure meaning; the dose axis is absent from the abstract, and cell-line trophoblasts are not placenta. Google Scholar as a search source compromises reproducibility. Useful as a pathway catalogue for placental mechanisms behind birth-weight associations, not as quantitative evidence.}
{10.1016/j.reprotox.2026.109364}

\band{Mechanistic toxicology\hfill 2 records}{Violet}

\paperentry{Wang et al. 2026}
{Ozone and PM$_{2.5}$ co-exposure exacerbates allergic rhinitis and is associated with PPAR/TRPV1-related barrier damage: Insights from nasal transcriptomic and microbial analyses.}
{Environ Pollut}
{C57BL/6J allergic-rhinitis mice in five groups (control, AR, AR+O$_3$, AR+PM$_{2.5}$, AR+PM$_{2.5}$+O$_3$). Co-exposure markedly aggravated sneezing, disrupted nasal mucosa and lowered ZO-1 and occludin; nasal transcriptomics implicated PPAR/TRPV1 signalling and 16S sequencing showed microbial dysbiosis correlated with inflammation and barrier injury. The authors state the doses were acute and high and not designed to model environmental exposure, which is the limitation - interaction at high dose need not persist at ambient levels, and group sizes and PM dose are not reported in the abstract. Associations between omics layers are correlational. Carried for the co-exposure design: O$_3$ and PM$_{2.5}$ peak in different seasons in most airsheds, so their joint effect is a question sensor networks measuring both can actually address.}
{10.1016/j.envpol.2026.129207}

\paperentry{Ye et al. 2026}
{Mechanistic Study of SRSF1-Mediated Inhibition of Treg Cell Function via the mTORC1/ULK1 Signalling Axis Through Mitophagy in Asthma.}
{Clin Exp Pharmacol Physiol}
{SRSF1 was elevated in BALF cells from asthma patients and correlated negatively with Treg FOXP3 and FEV1 \%pred. In OVA-asthma mice, SRSF1 upregulation in Tregs increased mTOR and ULK1-Ser757 phosphorylation and impaired mitophagy and metabolic reprogramming; SRSF1 knockdown or rapamycin restored Treg function and reduced airway inflammation, while an mTORC1 activator reversed the rescue. A PM$_{2.5}$ arm exaggerated these changes in an SRSF1-dependent way. PM is secondary to the paper's question: dose, particle source and exposure route are absent from the abstract, and patient numbers are not given. The value is mechanistic - a Treg mitophagy axis through which PM could worsen established asthma - and it adds nothing to exposure-response. Tier C.}
{10.1111/1440-1681.70157}

\band{Exposure assessment \& modelling\hfill 3 records}{Clay}

\paperentry{Broda et al. 2026}
{Vertical and Zonal Characteristics of Transatlantic Biomass Burning Aerosol Transport During the Extreme 2023 Canadian Wildfire Season.}
{Atmosphere}
{May-September 2023 smoke transport from Canada to Europe against a 2003-2022 baseline. Source-region smoke AOD was the highest on record in every month except August. At source the 0-2 km layer held 78-93\% of smoke over western Canada; during transport most smoke rose above 2 km, the 2-5 km layer dominated over the Atlantic and the extinction-weighted mean altitude climbed from 1-2 km to 2.5-4.3 km. Monthly transport efficiency to Europe was low, and satellite AOD did not reproduce the reanalysis zonal pattern. Monthly means mask episodes, and a reanalysis is not an observation. The surface-exposure implication is the useful part: when smoke is elevated, column AOD overstates surface PM$_{2.5}$, so AOD-to-surface products need vertical-profile correction, and surface sensors are the check.}
{10.3390/atmos17100913}

\paperentry{Zahid \& Morris 2026}
{Demand or Congestion? Disentangling Event-Related Emissions Using a Directional Control on I-15.}
{Preprints.org (preprint)}
{One-minute freeway detector data from the LATTICE platform during the 2026 Electric Daisy Carnival. Northbound (arrival) lost 9.5 mph over a five-hour window; southbound lost nothing, yet CO$_2$ rose almost equally in both directions (+11.6\% vs +11.8\%), so the headline increase is weekend demand, not congestion. Using the uncongested direction as control in a difference-in-differences, only PM$_{2.5}$ kept a substantial congestion residual (+8.9 percentage points), and residuals ranked by low-speed emission sensitivity (Spearman rho 1.00, n = 4). Emissions are modelled from speed and volume, not measured, so the PM$_{2.5}$ result inherits the emission model's low-speed factors; n = 4 pollutants makes rho uninformative. Preprint. The control-corridor logic transfers directly to near-road sensor pairs.}
{10.20944/preprints202609.1747.v1}

\paperentry{Rana \& Guzman 2026}
{Heterogeneous Nitrate Radical Oxidation of Catechol Produces Nitrocatechols, Oligomers, and Brown Carbon.}
{ACS Earth Space Chem}
{Flagged backfill: Crossref created this DOI on 20 Aug; it reached this window via a 22 Sep Europe PMC deposit and had not been carried or rejected. Catechol thin films oxidised by NO$_3$ under dry conditions produced nitroaromatic IR bands near 1590 and 1350 cm-1, a broad UV-vis absorption centred near 349 nm characteristic of brown carbon, and 4-nitrocatechol, dinitrocatechols, dimers and trimers; the proposed route runs through semiquinone radicals, then nitration and radical coupling. Thin films under dry conditions are far from real humid particles, and no yields or mass absorption efficiencies are given, so atmospheric significance is asserted rather than shown. Paired with the PAX-ISS record in this issue: nighttime BrC formation is one source of the seasonal BrC term that record apportions.}
{10.1021/acsearthspacechem.6c00226}

\band{Sensing, forecasting \& instrumentation\hfill 3 records}{Amber}

\paperentry{Le et al. 2026}
{Real-time monitoring of ultrafine aerosol size distributions using parallel non-bouncing impactors coupled with corona charger.}
{Meas Sci Technol}
{A Parallel Electro-inertial Particle Sizer: unipolar corona charger feeding six parallel non-bouncing impactor/Faraday-cup electrometer channels with kernel inversion, 10-560 nm. Impactor cut-offs landed within 5\% of design; against an SMPS, unimodal and bimodal distributions over 1e4-1e7 /cm3 were reproduced with under 10\% deviation in total PNC, NMAD and GSD. In a 16-day rooftop co-location, total PNC and UFP gave slope 1.023 and r 0.995, and nucleation, growth and plume events were resolved. Weak points are stated: sub-20 nm and complex multimodal structure deviate, and one site over 16 days says nothing about drift, humidity or long-term electrometer zero stability.
