\begin{keybox}
{\sffamily\bfseries\small\color{Deep}Toronto: the published four-station community AQHI missed other city sites by more than 2 AQHI units in both directions, across eight continuous sites in six weeks.}\\[1.4mm]
\footnotesize
SWAPIT, January--March 2024. NO$_2$ dominated short-term AQHI risk and rose under calm winds; O$_3$ rose with wind speed; PM$_{2.5}$ carried both local and regional features. Against the NAPS record from 1997, median AQHI has fallen almost twice as fast in summer as in winter, leaving winter higher since 2013.
\textbf{Where it is weak:} one six-week winter at eight sites, no CI, and the abstract gives neither instrument class nor site typology, so the deviation cannot be split into siting and method.
\textbf{Why it matters here:} it puts a number on the gap between a sparse index and the intra-urban field. NO$_2$ rather than PM$_{2.5}$ drives the index here, so the sensor case rests on the multi-pollutant node, not on an optical PM node alone.\par
\end{keybox}

\section*{\sffamily\bfseries\color{Deep}What changed today}
\vspace{-1.5mm}{\color{Amber}\rule{\linewidth}{1.1pt}}\vspace{2.5mm}

\footnotesize
\begin{itemize}

\item \textbf{Aged smoke is not fresh smoke.} Chamber aging raised ROS and oxidative DNA damage in BEAS-2B cells, most for smoldering emissions, while attenuating inflammatory signalling; cytotoxicity barely moved. No fold-changes in the abstract, and extracts on a submerged line, so dose relevance is open.

\item \textbf{Low-cost optical sensors meeting vape aerosol, twice.} AirBeam3 in a bar: PM$_{2.5}$ mean 28.8, peak 84.7\,$\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$ (pilot, no reference). Vendor-led school deployment, 37 sites, 18 months: over 300 incidents, no confusion matrix, alert fatigue and a smoke false positive reported. \textsc{backfill} for the second, entry 4 May.

\item \textbf{Inductive ferrous-aerosol sensing.} Micro-3D-printed impactor with liquid-metal coils: cut-offs 5.28 and 0.99\,$\mu$m, nRMSE 6.72--8.95\,\%. Laboratory aerosol only; no humidity, drift or unit-to-unit data.

\item \textbf{Hygroscopicity shifts with chemistry.} CMAQ, US summers 2013--2022: isoprene organosulfates move $\kappa$ by $-$5.6\,\% net. A spatially structured term that a single humidity correction cannot carry.

\item \textbf{Joint burden accounting.} 825 European cities: 213,889 deaths (89,224--344,223) jointly attributable to PM$_{2.5}$ and NO$_2$ in 2018, about a quarter below the sum of the single-pollutant figures. Abstract states a 76,833 gap; its printed numbers give 75,504. \textsc{backfill}, Crossref 26 October.

\item \textbf{Reproductive signals on thin exposure.} PMOS PR 1.22 (1.09--1.35) per SD PM$_{2.5}$, IDW from three stations; offspring hernia or hydrocele OR 1.07 (1.01--1.14) per $\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$, 53 events (\textsc{backfill}, entry 4 May); a pregnancy-loss classifier with AUC 0.935 on internal validation only. All three are hypothesis-grade.

\item \textbf{Exposure basis (x1).} Of 19 records, 2 rest on low-cost sensors (AirBeam3 bar pilot; school vape nodes), neither with a reference co-location in the abstract, and 4 on reference monitors. Health side with a recoverable basis (n=8): 50\,\% measured, 25\,\% modelled, 25\,\% proxy, but the measured share includes two station-interpolated residential estimates (three-station IDW; kriging), not personal measurement.

\end{itemize}

\paperentry{Cho exercise under particulate matter exposure BDNF neurotrophic narrative review 2026}
{Exercise Under Particulate Matter Exposure: Neurotrophic Responses and Functional Brain Outcomes}
{Atmosphere}
{Focused narrative review, PubMed and Web of Science, 37 reports (33 main, four indirect). Circulating BDNF responses to exercise under PM were heterogeneous; VEGF and IGF-1 gave complementary peripheral evidence. Human studies mixed attenuation in selected cognitive, dementia and imaging contrasts with retained activity benefit and possible buffering. Central molecular evidence from inhalation was limited; some animal improvements came from intravenous or instillation models. The authors find support for neither a uniform loss of exercise benefit nor a general reversal threshold. No pooling. Limitation: non-systematic, overlapping cohorts possible. Relevance: low-moderate - the stated gap is activity-specific exposure, which is what wearable PM sensing alongside exercise intensity could supply.}
{10.3390/atmos17111077}
