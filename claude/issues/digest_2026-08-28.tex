\section*{\sffamily\bfseries\color{Deep}Signal of the day}
\vspace{-1.5mm}{\color{Amber}\rule{\linewidth}{1.1pt}}\vspace{2.5mm}

\begin{keybox}
{\sffamily\bfseries\small\color{Deep}Sulfate carries three-quarters of the PM$_{2.5}$
mixture effect on infarct mortality --- and, separately, coating chemistry moves black
carbon's mass absorption cross section by $\sim$1\,m$^2$/g overnight. Both results
penalise mass-only instruments.}\\[1.4mm]
\footnotesize
Li et al.\ (\textit{Environ Sci Technol Lett}) fit a causal mixture model to county-level
US acute myocardial infarction mortality against PM$_{2.5}$ and its EC / OC / SO$_4^{2-}$
/ NO$_3^-$ constituents in a difference-in-differences design. Total mass gives
\textbf{RR 1.019 (95\% CI 1.013--1.025) per IQR}; the constituent mixture gives
\textbf{RR 1.116 (1.101--1.130) per quartile}, with \textbf{SO$_4^{2-}$ weighted 0.762} of
that total and an east--west split (SO$_4^{2-}$ 0.707 in the east, OC 0.485 in the west).
The mass estimate is not wrong, it is simply a diluted average over a mixture whose
components are not interchangeable. Zhang et al.\ (\textit{ACP}) supply the
instrumentation-side counterpart from Shenzhen: SP2 + SPAMS single-particle measurement
shows nighttime heterogeneous and daytime photochemical aging proceed by different
chemistry (nitrate-first vs.\ nitrate-then-sulfate) yet converge on nearly the same
\textbf{MAC enhancement at 532\,nm --- $\sim$0.8\,m$^2$/g overnight, $\sim$1.0\,m$^2$/g
by day} on a 1.2\,$\mu$g/m$^3$ rBC background with 155--170\,nm core MMD. For a
distributed low-cost network this is the operative number: an optical channel calibrated
against a fixed mass-scattering assumption absorbs the whole diurnal aging cycle as
apparent concentration drift.
\end{keybox}

% -------------------------------------------------------------- exec brief
\section*{\sffamily\bfseries\color{Deep}What changed today}
\vspace{-1.5mm}{\color{Amber}\rule{\linewidth}{1.1pt}}\vspace{2.5mm}

\footnotesize
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Exposure assessment is the largest cluster (5 records), and for once it is
not all epidemiology.} It holds a satellite--surface fusion product, a new summary
statistic, a filter-composition campaign, a wave-channel source-process experiment and a
bioaerosol survey. The common thread is that all five are about \emph{how the exposure
number is constructed}, not what it does to people.

\item \textbf{A 1\,km daily PM$_{2.5}$ surface for Bangladesh now exists.} Cui et al.\
gap-fill MAIAC AOD --- more than 60\% of retrievals are missing --- with LightGBM, then
push the complete AOD through an attention-based TabNet to ground level. Held-out
$R^2=0.95$, RMSE 17.41\,$\mu$g/m$^3$ over a national mean of 68--75\,$\mu$g/m$^3$. The
RMSE is roughly a quarter of the mean; that is defensible at these concentrations but it
is not a monitoring-grade product, and the paper's own DHS linkage is ecological.

\item \textbf{Citizen-science monitoring gets its first proper critical review in this
watch.} Marcon et al.\ (\textit{Curr Environ Health Rep}) is explicit that AI-assisted
calibration and distributed sensor networks are the live methodological frontier, and
equally explicit that representativeness, not sensor accuracy, is the binding constraint
on policy uptake --- an ordering worth taking seriously.

\item \textbf{A threshold-free concentration-frequency metric.} Hopke et al.\ define the
PM$_{2.5}$ Magnitude--Rank Index (largest $k$ with $\geq k$ days at $\geq k$\,$\mu$g/m$^3$),
applied to 918 US MSA-years. It correlates 0.83--0.96 with the annual mean, the 98th
percentile and exceedance counts --- which is both the selling point and the problem: a
metric that correlates 0.96 with days $\geq15$\,$\mu$g/m$^3$ has to earn its place on
interpretability, not information content.

\item \textbf{Low-cost sensors as the primary instrument in a health-risk paper.}
Wongsasuluk et al.\ run five DustBoy stations for a year in a Chiang Mai road underpass
(385{,}622 records) and build a stepwise MLR forecast. No co-location against a reference
monitor is reported in the abstract, so the risk estimates inherit whatever bias the
DustBoy units carry in a high-humidity, high-loading tunnel --- exactly the regime where
nephelometric error is largest.

\item \textbf{Policy-scale co-benefit modelling.} Zhang et al.\ (\textit{Nat Commun})
put hydrogen shipping at $\sim$129{,}500 (68{,}300--202{,}200) averted premature deaths
annually and US\$306\,bn (146--488) saved --- but \textbf{75\% of that is ozone}, only
25\% PM$_{2.5}$. Read as a PM result it is much weaker than the headline suggests.

\item \textbf{Empty clusters.} \textit{Respiratory \& allergic} and \textit{Reproductive
\& developmental} are both unoccupied; respiratory was last empty on 23 August.
\end{itemize}

\section*{\sffamily\bfseries\color{Deep}Paper-level digest}
\vspace{-1.5mm}{\color{Amber}\rule{\linewidth}{1.1pt}}\vspace{1mm}

{\fontsize{7.4}{9}\selectfont\color{Slate}Ordered by cluster; the 28 August entry window
only.\par}

\band{Sensing, forecasting \& instrumentation\hfill 4 records}{Amber}

\paperentry{Zhang et al.\ 2026h}
{Contrasting nighttime heterogeneous and daytime photochemical aging drive the optical
evolution of black carbon}
{Atmos Chem Phys}
{Integrated SP2 and single-particle aerosol mass spectrometry over an urban Shenzhen
campaign, mean refractory BC \textbf{1.2\,$\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$}, core mass median diameter
155--170\,nm. The coating-to-core mass ratio rises continuously through the diurnal
cycle by two mechanistically distinct routes --- nighttime heterogeneous coating growth
with enhanced nitrate signal, and daytime photochemical aging with rapid nitrate
accumulation followed by sulfate and oxidised organics. Both converge on similar mass
absorption cross section enhancement at 532\,nm: \textbf{$\sim$0.8\,m$^2$\,g$^{-1}$
overnight, $\sim$1.0\,m$^2$\,g$^{-1}$ by day}. No uncertainty is given on the MAC
enhancements and it is a single city over one campaign, so the magnitudes should not be
transported. Directly relevant to distributed sensing: any optical channel with a fixed
absorption or scattering assumption re-expresses this aging cycle as concentration drift
with a diurnal period.}
{10.5194/acp-26-12197-2026}

\paperentry{Marcon et al.\ 2026}
{Citizen science in air pollution monitoring: methodological approaches, effectiveness
and integration with health research}
{Curr Environ Health Rep}
{Critical review of participatory air-quality monitoring --- low-cost sensors, wearables,
community co-creation --- and its uptake into environmental health research. The review
identifies AI-assisted calibration and distributed sensor networks as the methodological
frontier and argues that hyperlocal citizen data materially improves spatial resolution
and risk communication. Its useful contribution is the ordering of the barriers:
\textbf{representativeness and policy uptake, not sensor accuracy}, are named as the
binding constraints. No quantitative synthesis, no pooled performance figures and no
inclusion criteria are reported in the abstract, so it is a framing document rather than
an evidence base. For a distributed low-cost network this is the argument that siting
design deserves as much engineering attention as the calibration pipeline.}
{10.1007/s40572-026-00559-1}

\paperentry{Wongsasuluk et al.\ 2026}
{PM$_{2.5}$ exposure and respiratory health risk for vulnerable populations in an urban
underpass: a predictive MLR approach, Chiang Mai}
{Environ Sci Pollut Res Int}
{Five DustBoy low-cost stations along the Don Kaew road tunnel, August 2022 to July 2023,
yielding \textbf{385{,}622 records} across PM$_{2.5}$, PM$_{10}$, CO, CO$_2$, NO$_2$,
SO$_2$, O$_3$, TVOC, CH$_2$O and meteorology. A stepwise multiple linear regression
stratified by weekday/weekend and day/night gives a high reported goodness of fit, and a
comparative risk assessment converts predicted concentrations into inhaled dose and
relative risk against Thai and WHO exposure factors. The inference-threatening gap is
that \textbf{no co-location against a reference monitor is reported}: a semi-enclosed
tunnel is a high-humidity, high-loading environment, precisely where nephelometric
low-cost sensors over-read most, and every downstream risk number inherits that bias
uncorrected.}
{10.1007/s11356-026-38171-3}

\paperentry{Xiong et al.\ 2026}
{In situ real-time determination of SO$_2$ photochemical oxidation in nanoscale sea salt
aerosols based on dark-field microscopy}
{Atmos Chem Phys}
{Planar-waveguide dark-field microscopy tracks hygroscopic growth factors of single NaCl
nanoparticles under UV irradiation and SO$_2$, with the Zdanovskii--Stokes--Robinson rule
converting growth to composition and hence to reaction rate. Reported first-order rate
constant \textbf{0.6523\,h$^{-1}$ for 100\,nm NaCl}, with a \textbf{non-monotonic size
dependence} --- rising from 50 to 200\,nm, falling above 200\,nm --- attributed to
competition between surface curvature and specific surface area, and cross-checked
against transition-state theory and a double-film mass-transfer treatment. Single
salt system under controlled UV; the extrapolation to ambient mixed-composition marine
aerosol is not demonstrated. Methodologically this is the interesting part: an optical
single-particle technique that yields kinetics without collection, at sizes below what
most PM instrumentation resolves at all.}
{10.5194/acp-26-12183-2026}

\band{Exposure assessment \& modelling\hfill 5 records}{Amber}

\paperentry{Cui et al.\ 2026}
{Daily 1\,km satellite-based PM$_{2.5}$ exposure estimates in Bangladesh, 2020--2025}
{Environ Pollut}
{Two-stage machine learning over one of the most monitoring-sparse high-burden regions in
the world. Stage one reconstructs MAIAC aerosol optical depth, of which
\textbf{more than 60\% of potential retrievals are missing}, with LightGBM; stage two
feeds the gap-filled AOD plus meteorological, composition and geospatial predictors into
an attention-based TabNet to estimate ground PM$_{2.5}$. Independent test performance
\textbf{$R^2 = 0.95$, RMSE 17.41\,$\mu$g/m$^3$, MAE 9.38}, against a national annual mean
of \textbf{68--75\,$\mu$g/m$^3$} and winter peaks above 130. The limitation is structural:
with almost no ground reference network, the held-out set is small and drawn from the
same sparse monitors used in training, so $R^2 = 0.95$ describes interpolation skill more
than transferability, and the DHS linkage is division-level and ecological.}
{10.1016/j.envpol.2026.129051}

\paperentry{Hopke et al.\ 2026}
{A PM$_{2.5}$ magnitude-rank index for summarizing annual concentration-frequency
structure}
{Environ Monit Assess}
{Introduces the PM$_{2.5}$ Magnitude--Rank Index --- the largest integer $k$ such that at
least $k$ days in a year have daily mean PM$_{2.5}$ $\geq k$\,$\mu$g/m$^3$ --- as a
threshold-free joint summary of magnitude and frequency. Applied to \textbf{918
qualifying MSA-years across 96 US metropolitan areas, 2013--2022}, PMRI ranges 6--38
(median 16) and correlates with the annual mean at Spearman $\rho = 0.83$, the annual
98th percentile at 0.95, and days $\geq15$\,$\mu$g/m$^3$ at 0.96. That last figure is the
honest problem with the metric: at $\rho = 0.96$ against an existing exceedance count it
adds very little information, so its case rests on being policy-threshold-free and
intuitively readable rather than on discriminating power. No health-outcome validation is
attempted.}
{10.1007/s10661-026-15858-1}

\paperentry{Reyna-Bello et al.\ 2026}
{Seasonal variability of PM$_{2.5}$-bound metals and meteorology in an urban-industrial
area of northern Mexico}
{Environ Monit Assess}
{MiniVol low-volume sampling across four seasons in Saltillo during 2023, with ten metals
(Al, Cd, Cu, Fe, K, Mn, Na, Ni, Pb, Zn) by flame atomic absorption and PCA against
temperature, humidity, wind, precipitation and pressure. Mass is strongly cold-season
weighted --- \textbf{152.9$\pm$23.6\,$\mu$g/m$^3$ in autumn and 115.3$\pm$37.2 in winter
against 40.7$\pm$6.8 in spring} --- consistent with suppressed dispersion, while several
metals invert that pattern and peak in spring and summer. The reported metal
concentrations are implausibly high for PM$_{2}$$_{.5}$-bound species (Cd at
3.13\,$\mu$g/m$^3$ is orders of magnitude above typical urban values), which points to a
units or digestion-blank problem; treat the metal magnitudes as unverified and the
seasonal contrast as the usable result.}
{10.1007/s10661-026-15856-3}

\paperentry{Leibensperger et al.\ 2026}
{Sea surface warming suppresses primary sea spray aerosol number production}
{Atmos Chem Phys}
{Controlled wave-breaking experiments in the Scripps Ocean-Atmosphere Research Simulator,
which reproduces wind, waves and sea surface temperature together. Raising SST from 2 to
23\,$^\circ$C \textbf{suppressed subsurface bubble concentration by 1.5$\times$, sea
spray aerosol number by 3$\times$ and SSA number flux by 3$\times$}, with a
non-monotonic SST dependence in the supermicron fraction. This is a single simulator with
one seawater matrix and no biological aging, so absolute fluxes are not transferable ---
the value is the sign and rough magnitude of the SST dependence, which the field has not
agreed on. Relevance here is indirect but real: marine background number concentration is
the baseline against which coastal low-cost network anomalies are read.}
{10.5194/acp-26-12211-2026}

\paperentry{Agpaoa et al.\ 2026}
{Occurrence, temporal variation and meteorological drivers of airborne fungal spores in
Laoag City, northern Philippines}
{Environ Monit Assess}
{First aeromycological record for a monsoon-influenced coastal site in northern Luzon:
216 sampling days, March to November 2022, cumulative index \textbf{190{,}096
spores\,m$^{-3}$}, dominated by \textit{Cladosporium} and Fusaroid-like types. Wet-season
sampling carries most of the cumulative load but the highest daily concentrations fall in
the dry season, and diurnal peaks sit at 00:00--06:00. Positive associations with relative
humidity and rainfall, negative with temperature, and no significant wind association.
Single site, single year, morphology-based identification only, and no PM co-measurement
--- so the bioaerosol fraction cannot be placed within a mass-based PM budget. Included
because overnight spore peaks are a documented confounder for optical particle counters
in humid climates.}
{10.1007/s10661-026-15814-z}

\band{Burden, policy \& mitigation\hfill 4 records}{Coral}

\paperentry{Zhang et al.\ 2026g}
{Health and economic impacts of improved air quality from transitioning global shipping
energy to hydrogen}
{Nat Commun}
{Integrated emission--air quality--health framework over feasible hydrogen-transition
scenarios for global shipping. Full adoption averts approximately \textbf{129{,}500
(95\% CI 68{,}300--202{,}200) premature deaths annually}, valued at \textbf{US\$306\,bn
(146--488)}, with India, Japan and the United States taking the largest economic
benefit. The number that matters for a PM watch is the split: \textbf{75\% of the averted
mortality is ozone, only 25\% PM$_{2.5}$}, with the O$_3$ gains concentrated in Southern
Asia and the PM$_{2.5}$ gains in Eastern Asia. The confidence interval spans a factor of
three and propagates concentration--response uncertainty only, not scenario or
inventory uncertainty, so the true spread is wider than stated.}
{10.1038/s41467-026-76134-x}

\paperentry{Nilsson et al.\ 2026}
{Urban environment, health and equity: symptoms and annoyances related to air pollution,
traffic noise and greenness}
{Environ Epidemiol}
{\textbf{46{,}282 adults aged 18--84} in Stockholm County responding to the 2023 Swedish
National Environmental Health Survey, with geocoded residential PM$_{2.5}$, PM$_{10}$,
NO$_2$, road/rail/aircraft noise and 300\,m NDVI, dichotomised and stratified by
socioeconomic status and building type. Apartment residents are more exposed to pollution
and noise and have less greenness; SES-stratified patterns are exposure-specific with no
consistent overall gradient. The strong associations are with \emph{perception} --- NO$_2$
with poor perceived outdoor air quality (\textbf{OR 3.22; 95\% CI 2.75--3.79}), road noise
with high annoyance (OR 4.59; 4.02--5.26). Both exposure and outcome are cross-sectional
and the outcome is self-reported, so these are perception--exposure concordance
estimates, not health effects, and the odds ratios should not be read as such.}
{10.1097/EE9.0000000000000516}

\paperentry{Sirikul et al.\ 2026}
{Knowledge, attitudes and practices of caregivers regarding PM$_{2.5}$ exposure among
children in northern Thailand}
{J Clim Chang Health}
{Cross-sectional KAP survey of caregivers recruited at Chiang Mai Child Development
Centres. Knowledge is moderate (10.44$\pm$3.80 of 22), attitudes positive
(30.74$\pm$3.57 of 40), practices inconsistent (17.33$\pm$5.22 of 32). The specific gaps
are the useful part: \textbf{97.0\% recognise the health risk to children, but only
41.8\% know surgical masks are inadequate for PM$_{2.5}$ and only 9.7\% can correctly
interpret an AQI value}; air purifier ownership is 15.3\% and monitoring-app use 28.2\%,
both with a clear education and income gradient. The abstract does not report the sample
size, response rate or instrument validation, and centre-based recruitment excludes
non-attending households, so absolute prevalences are not generalisable. The AQI-literacy
figure is the one that should worry anyone designing a public-facing sensor dashboard.}
{10.1016/j.joclim.2026.100734}

\paperentry{Sharma et al.\ 2026b}
{Geospatial linkage of environmental exposures, socioeconomic profile and health outcomes
among women of reproductive age, Victoria}
{Health Promot J Austr}
{Postcode-level linkage of 2021 Australian Census sociodemographic and health variables to
PM$_{2.5}$, NO$_2$, CO, SO$_2$, formaldehyde, ozone, land surface temperature and
greenness, with Moran's I, Wagstaff concentration indices and geospatial regression.
Non-communicable disease burden in women of reproductive age is strongly socioeconomically
patterned (\textbf{323.0 vs.\ 227.3 per 1000} in most- versus least-disadvantaged
postcodes; steepest for diabetes, Wagstaff $-0.16$), \textbf{but environmental exposures
show only weak associations with outcomes} despite clear spatial clustering. Inner
Melbourne has lower NCD burden despite elevated NO$_2$ and PM$_{2.5}$. This is
ecological, cross-sectional and confounded by the same SES gradient it measures; the
null environmental finding is uninformative rather than reassuring.}
{10.1002/hpja.70241}

\band{Neuro / mental health\hfill 2 records}{Deep}

\paperentry{Wang et al.\ 2026e}
{Speciated fine particulate matter and self-reported sleep time in middle-aged and older
Chinese adults}
{Sleep Med}
{\textbf{19{,}505 CHARLS participants aged 45+}, 2011--2018, with 6-month and 2-year
PM$_{2.5}$ and five constituents --- BC, organic matter, SO$_4^{2-}$, NO$_3^-$,
NH$_4^+$ --- from the Tracking Air Pollution in China product, against total sleep time
(nocturnal plus napping) via linear mixed models and quantile g-computation. The
secondary inorganic ions NO$_3^-$, SO$_4^{2-}$ and NH$_4^+$ show the most consistent
associations, and the direction is the surprise: highest versus lowest quartile of
intermediate-term exposure \textbf{increases} total sleep time (PM$_{2.5}$ +0.16\,h and
comparable for the constituents). Self-reported duration, no actigraphy, and an
increase in sleep duration is not obviously a harm --- it may index fatigue, reduced
outdoor activity on high-pollution days, or reverse behavioural adaptation. Mechanism is
not addressed and the finding should be treated as hypothesis-generating.}
{10.1016/j.sleep.2026.109229}

\paperentry{Chen et al.\ 2026i}
{Gender and season disparities in the influence of short-term PM$_{2.5}$ exposure on
emergency room visits for mood disorders in Taiwan}
{J Occup Environ Med}
{Time-stratified case-crossover with distributed lag non-linear models over
\textbf{2{,}172 emergency visits} for bipolar or depressive disorder in the Chang Gung
Research Database, 2010--2020, linked to Ministry of Environment monitoring. Associations
appear \textbf{exclusively in the hot season and predominantly in women}, with delayed
effects at lags 3--5 and cumulative effects over lags 0--1 and 0--3 to 0--5. No effect
estimates, confidence intervals or exposure contrasts appear in the abstract, which makes
the result unquantifiable as reported. With 2{,}172 events split by sex and season the
subgroup cells are small, and a hot-season-only, women-only finding from a design with no
pre-registered subgroup plan is the classic shape of a multiplicity artefact. Carried at
tier B on design quality, not on the strength of the reported signal.}
{10.1097/JOM.0000000000003848}

\band{Cardiovascular \& metabolic\hfill 1 record}{Teal}

\paperentry{Li et al.\ 2026e}
