\section*{\sffamily\bfseries\color{Deep}Signal of the day}
\vspace{-1.5mm}{\color{Amber}\rule{\linewidth}{1.1pt}}\vspace{2.5mm}

\begin{keybox}
{\sffamily\bfseries\small\color{Deep}A commodity indoor-monitor particle sensor, flown on a
weather balloon, reproduced research-grade aerosol scattering profiles to 27\,km --- and the paper is honest
about the two retrievals that did not work.}\\[1.4mm]
\footnotesize
Broda and colleagues (\textit{Atmos Meas Tech}) fly \textbf{Sensirion SPS30} sensors ---
the same commodity class used in indoor monitors --- on a balloon-borne \textbf{AeroSonde},
calibrated against an \textbf{Aurora 4000 nephelometer} and an \textbf{LAS 3340}
spectrometer. Correlation with the reference instruments exceeded \textbf{$r$ 0.88}, with
the \textbf{aerosol scattering coefficient} the best-retrieved quantity; the
\textbf{scattering \r{A}ngstr\"om exponent and effective radius carried substantially
larger uncertainty} and the authors restrict them to \textbf{qualitative fine-versus-coarse
discrimination} rather than reporting them as measurements. Soundings over southeastern
Poland in June and August 2025 reached \textbf{10--27\,km at better than 10\,m vertical
resolution} and caught \textbf{North American biomass-burning plumes and Saharan dust};
layer structure and extensive properties tracked active remote sensing at \textbf{$r$
0.74--0.81}. \textbf{Why this is the signal and not just a nice instrument paper:} the
vertical dimension is exactly where surface low-cost networks are blind and where the
satellite-AOD-to-surface-PM$_{2.5}$ inversion fails. The transatlantic smoke record carried
on 22 September made the same point from the other side --- when smoke is elevated, column
AOD overstates surface concentration --- and a sensor of this class that can profile the
layer structure directly is a cheaper answer than another retrieval correction. \textbf{The
honest failure is the valuable part:} intensive optical properties (SAE, effective radius)
are \textbf{not} recoverable at this sensor's signal-to-noise, which is the same conclusion
surface calibration studies keep reaching about inferring composition from a
Plantower-class scattering signal. \textbf{Two campaigns, one region, and no independent
in-situ profile} leave the intensive retrievals unvalidated aloft; this is a demonstration,
not a validated product.\par
\end{keybox}

\section*{\sffamily\bfseries\color{Deep}What changed today}
\vspace{-1.5mm}{\color{Amber}\rule{\linewidth}{1.1pt}}\vspace{2.5mm}

\footnotesize
\begin{itemize}

\item \textbf{Metadata-only jumped to 7 of 27 (26\,\%), and it took the day's most relevant
title with it.} On 22 September the figure was 2 of 23 (9\,\%). The regression is entirely
a publisher-deposit artefact, not a retrieval change: \textbf{five \textit{Atmospheric
Environment} deposits}, one \textit{Environment International} and one \textit{Aerosol
Science and Technology} arrived as title-plus-DOI with no abstract in Crossref, Europe PMC
or PubMed. The T\&F record is the one that matters --- \textbf{a head-to-head evaluation of
real-time elemental-carbon monitors against diesel particulate matter}, the reference
comparison behind occupational black-carbon sensing --- and \texttt{doi.org} returned
\textbf{403} to an automated fetch, so not even the landing page was readable. It is listed
and not summarised. \textbf{The retry list is now 37.}

\item \textbf{Two tier-A cohorts, two exposure resolutions, and a sign flip that reads as
misclassification.} Oh et al.\ (\textit{Mov Disord}) follow \textbf{5.3 million Koreans aged
65+} to \textbf{143,557 incident Parkinson's cases} and report \textbf{HR 1.018 (95\,\% CI
1.008--1.028)} per IQR (4.3\,$\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$) of PM$_{2.5}$ --- with \textbf{NO$_2$
inverse after full adjustment}. Exposure is CMAQ output assigned \textbf{by administrative
district}, so within-district contrast is zero and urban structure is absorbed into the
pollutant. Yang et al.\ (\textit{Thorax}), on \textbf{172,845 Beijing lung-cancer
registrants}, get \textbf{HR 1.111 (1.083--1.140)} per 10\,$\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$ from a 1\,km
reanalysis --- \textbf{six times the per-unit effect} on a sicker population, and a
\textbf{significant PM$_{2.5}$--O$_3$ interaction above $\sim$105\,$\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$ O$_3$}
that offsets about \textbf{40\,\%} of the modelled 13,229 avoided deaths.

\item \textbf{The composition axis had its best day of the month.} Hua et al.\
(\textit{Environ Health}) cross \textbf{41 components and six source factors against nine
size fractions below 10\,$\mu$m} for \textbf{58,763 ischaemic and 2,588 haemorrhagic}
stroke admissions: ischaemic risk attaches to fractions \textbf{below 1.1 and above
3.3\,$\mu$m}, haemorrhagic to \textbf{As, Cu, Fe, Mn, Ni, Pb and PAHs}, with coal
combustion \textbf{RR 1.05 (1.01--1.10)} and industry \textbf{RR 1.18 (1.00--1.41)} ---
the latter sitting on the null. Separately, Jiang et al.\ (\textit{ES\&T}) quantify
\textbf{$\sim$1,000 nitrated aromatics} on personal PM$_{2.5}$ in Beijing and find
\textbf{secondary NACs carry over 70\,\% of summer exposure}, with IL-6 associations
\textbf{2.1--232 times} those of PAHs or gaseous NO$_x$. \textbf{Both findings are
invisible to a mass-only network.}

\item \textbf{A within-year exposure-structure metric beats the annual mean.} A medRxiv
preprint (Zhang) defines \textbf{PMRI} --- the largest $k$ for which at least $k$ days
reached $k$\,$\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$ --- and adds it to annual-mean models of age-adjusted
mortality over \textbf{27,289 county-years}. County-held-out $R^2$ rises
\textbf{0.0802\,$\to$\,0.1391} ($\Delta R^2$ 0.0589, 95\,\% CI 0.0499--0.0677), holding
across geographic, temporal and all six cause-specific splits. Ecological and still weak
prediction in absolute terms, but it is a direct argument that \textbf{daily-resolution
networks carry mortality-relevant information that annual means discard} --- which is the
case for dense sensing stated as a statistic rather than an aspiration.

\item \textbf{The PubMed sensing leg returned zero for the fourth consecutive day, and the
DOI key earned its keep again.} The harvester's instrumentation query has now produced
\textbf{0 records on 20, 21, 22 and 23 September}; the entire sensing block came from
\textbf{Crossref-by-ISSN (65 deposits)}, which is also why five of the seven metadata-only
records exist. Three separate connector queries returned \textbf{9 PMIDs the harvester
missed}: six were appended as new records and \textbf{two were merged onto Crossref records
that carried no PMID at all} --- caught on the DOI key, exactly the near-miss logged on
18 September. \textbf{PMID-first dedup alone would have double-admitted both.}

\item \textbf{A publisher landing page recovered an abstract that every structured route
had refused.} The \textit{Particle and Fibre Toxicology} inhalation record returned nothing
from Crossref, Europe PMC or PubMed \texttt{efetch}, but the Springer article page carried
the full abstract --- rats inhaling \textbf{alumina nanoparticles at 1--20\,mg\,m$^{-3}$
with 5\,ppm HCl}, where \textbf{four consecutive days resolved by day 28} while
\textbf{once-weekly for eight weeks left higher retained burden, impaired clearance and
persistent neutrophilia}. Equal cumulative dose delivered intermittently is not equivalent
--- which is an assumption every time-averaged occupational exposure metric makes.

\end{itemize}

\section*{\sffamily\bfseries\color{Deep}Paper-level digest}
\vspace{-1.5mm}{\color{Amber}\rule{\linewidth}{1.1pt}}\vspace{1mm}

{\fontsize{7.4}{9}\selectfont\color{Slate}Ordered by cluster. Each entry gives the
methodological core and the finding that matters, not an abstract paraphrase. Records
marked \textit{metadata only} carry no recoverable abstract and assert no finding.\par}

\band{Neuro / mental health\hfill 1 record}{Deep}

\paperentry{Oh et al. 2026}
{Association Between Long-Term Exposure to Air Pollution and Parkinson's Disease: Administrative Cohort of Older Adults in South Korea.}
{Mov Disord}
{5.3 million adults aged 65 and over in the Korean National Health Insurance database, 2010-2019, with 143,557 incident Parkinson's disease cases ascertained from claims requiring hospitalisation or repeated outpatient diagnoses. Per interquartile increase, PM$_{2.5}$ (4.3 $\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$) gave HR 1.018 (95\% CI 1.008-1.028) and O$_3$ (7.3 ppb) HR 1.019 (1.006-1.033); NO$_2$ was inverse after full adjustment, and no demographic or clinical effect modification reached significance. The inverse NO$_2$ is the tell: exposure is CMAQ output assigned by administrative district, so within-district contrast is zero and urban socioeconomic structure is absorbed into the pollutant. Claims also date diagnosis, not onset. District-resolution assignment at this cohort size is precisely what a dense sensor network would replace.}
{10.1002/mds.70518}

\band{Cardiovascular \& metabolic\hfill 4 records}{Teal}

\paperentry{Hua et al. 2026}
{Heterogeneous Risks of Chemical Components and Sources in Size-Resolved Particulate Matter on Ischemic and Hemorrhagic Stroke.}
{Environ Health (Wash)}
{Time-series analysis of 58,763 ischaemic and 2,588 haemorrhagic stroke hospitalisations against 41 PM components and six source factors resolved across nine size fractions below 10 um - the finest joint size-composition-source contrast this watch has carried. Ischaemic stroke tracked components below 1.1 um and above 3.3 um; haemorrhagic stroke tracked As, Cu, Fe, Mn, Ni, Pb and PAHs. Coal combustion gave RR 1.05 (95\% CI 1.01-1.10) per IQR for ischaemic stroke and industrial emissions RR 1.18 (1.00-1.41) for haemorrhagic, whose lower bound sits on the null with 2,588 events. Source factors from receptor modelling are collinear by construction, so component-specific attribution is softer than the size resolution implies. Size-resolved sensing, not PM$_{2.5}$ mass, is what this design needs at scale.}
{10.1021/envhealth.6c00019}

\paperentry{Campailla et al. 2026}
{Ambient particulate matter exposure and acute type A aortic dissection: A retrospective time-stratified case-crossover study in northern Italy.}
{Int J Cardiol Cardiovasc Risk Prev}
{Time-stratified case-crossover of 40 Stanford type A acute aortic dissections at a single northern-Italian centre, 2018-2022, referent days matched on weekday within month and year, temperature-adjusted. A doubling of same-day PM$_{2.5}$ gave adjusted OR 4.78 (95\% CI 1.61-14.29); 30-day PM$_{2.5}$ and both PM$_{10}$ windows pointed the same way. Forty cases is the whole story - the interval spans nearly an order of magnitude, the authors label the estimates exploratory, and a single urban-background monitor stands in for everyone in the catchment. Reported here because it is a rare European estimate for a high-lethality outcome, not because the magnitude is credible. This is exactly the low-count, high-misclassification setting where a dense network changes the variance more than the point estimate.}
{10.1016/j.ijcrp.2026.200708}

\paperentry{Zhang et al. 2026}
{Air Pollution Exposure Induces Vascular Endothelial Cell Dysfunction via Elevated Circulating F2: Multiomics Insights from an Ex Vivo Biosensor Assay.}
{Environ Health (Wash)}
{Travel-panel design: eight college students moved from Qingdao to the substantially more polluted Shijiazhuang, with serial serum sampling, proteomics, metabolomics and an ex vivo endothelial biosensor. Prothrombin (F2) emerged as the hub node correlated with exposure and was nominated again by weighted gene coexpression analysis; metabolomics showed bile-acid and arginine-proline perturbation, and endothelial transcriptomes showed activation. n = 8 with no counter-travel arm is the ceiling on all of it - city is confounded with diet, water, sleep and stress, and the omics search space dwarfs the sample. Hypothesis-generating only. The design itself is cheap to strengthen: a personal-sensor-instrumented reciprocal travel panel would give an exposure axis instead of a city label.}
{10.1021/envhealth.5c00833}

\paperentry{Jeong et al. 2026}
{Joint effects of air pollution and temperature on glycemia: An exposome analysis in European cohorts.}
{Environ Int}
{\textsc{metadata only.} Metadata-only record: Elsevier deposited title, authors (Jeong, Edstorp, Lovison, Pickford and colleagues) and DOI on 23 Sep with no abstract, and Crossref, Europe PMC and PubMed all returned nothing. The title states a joint air-pollution-temperature exposome analysis of glycaemia across European cohorts, which is a design this watch tracks - temperature is the covariate most likely to be doing the work in short-term PM cardiometabolic associations. Nothing about the pollutants, exposure model, cohorts, sample size or estimates can be stated from the deposit, and inferring a method from a title is not something this watch does. On the retry list; re-screen on its merits when the abstract appears.}
{10.1016/j.envint.2026.110538}

\band{Respiratory \& allergic\hfill 1 record}{Sky}

\paperentry{Dinh et al. 2026}
{Association of air pollution with chronic obstructive pulmonary disease: anemia-related outcomes and exploratory genetic susceptibility analyses.}
{Front Med (Lausanne)}
{Taiwan Biobank case-control of 7,566 COPD cases and 22,698 age- and sex-matched controls. Each 10 $\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$ of PM$_{2.5}$ carried OR 1.252 (95\% CI 1.222-1.283) at a one-month averaging window and 1.642 (1.588-1.699) at twelve months; the within-COPD anaemia analysis showed no consistent association, and a haemoglobin polygenic score interacted with SO$_2$ only nominally. Those confidence intervals are implausibly narrow for 30,000 participants under correlated multi-pollutant exposure, which usually means unmodelled spatial correlation rather than precision, and COPD is defined partly by self-reported emphysema or chronic bronchitis. The monotone rise with averaging window is as consistent with confounding by place as with cumulative dose - a distinction only within-person exposure variation can settle.}
{10.3389/fmed.2026.1925502}

\band{Reproductive \& developmental\hfill 2 records}{Sage}

\paperentry{Hu et al. 2026}
{Real-world PM$_{2.5}$ exposure triggers early testicular epigenetic responses in the absence of significant changes in measured reproductive endpoints.}
{Reprod Toxicol}
{Male mice breathed real-world ambient PM$_{2.5}$ in Hangzhou through a whole-body inhalation system for 4, 8 or 12 weeks - roughly one to three spermatogenic cycles. Sperm quality, reproductive hormones, histopathology and oxidative DNA damage were all unchanged, while global testicular 5mC fell at 4 weeks and rose at 12 weeks relative to control (P < 0.05), and 5hmC rose significantly at 12 weeks. The claim is that epigenetic remodelling precedes measurable injury. Two limits: the record gives no exposure concentration, so dose is unquantified, and global 5mC/5hmC has no locus resolution, so a bidirectional time course is as compatible with adaptation as with damage. Real-ambient inhalation studies live or die on continuous characterisation of what the animals actually breathe - an instrumentation problem.}
{10.1016/j.reprotox.2026.109366}

\paperentry{Li et al. 2026a}
{Cohort profile: the Shenzhen Birth Cohort (SZBC) study.}
{BMJ Open}
{Cohort profile rather than a result: 4,208 women recruited at 19 weeks' gestation or earlier from a single Shenzhen maternity hospital since March 2018, 3,962 with complete baseline data, 3,150 deliveries, up to 20 follow-up points through age seven, with questionnaires, examinations and blood, urine and faecal samples. Findings cited to date include prenatal greenness associated with better motor development and postnatal PM$_{2.5}$ - nitrate in particular - with delayed motor and problem-solving development. No effect sizes, intervals, exposure model or adjustment set are given, and single-hospital recruitment in a migrant-dense city limits transportability. Carried as infrastructure: a component-resolved prenatal and postnatal cohort with biosamples is the design that could eventually separate nitrate from mass.}
{10.1136/bmjopen-2026-121066}

\band{Mechanistic toxicology\hfill 4 records}{Violet}

\paperentry{Jiang et al. 2026}
{Nitration of Urban PM$_{2.5}$: A Hidden Driver of Enhanced Human Inflammation.}
{Environ Sci Technol}
{Longitudinal population exposomics in Beijing resolved roughly 1,000 nitrated aromatic compounds (NACs) on personal PM$_{2.5}$, at median concentrations above the traditional polycyclic aromatics measured alongside them. Secondary NACs formed by aqueous-phase nitration, represented by 3- and 5-nitrosalicylic acid, carried over 70\% of summer NAC exposure, and their associations with lung and systemic IL-6 were 2.1 to 232 times stronger than those of PAHs or gaseous NOx. The upper end of that ratio range is doing a lot of work and no CIs are given; associations are cross-sectional within a panel and NAC exposure covaries with everything else in summer photochemistry. Still the strongest argument this quarter that NOx health effects are partly particle-phase - which means mass-only sensing misses the mechanism entirely.}
{10.1021/acs.est.6c06494}

\paperentry{Cherriere et al. 2026}
{Influence of lung overload and exposure scenarios on recovery following inhalation of an alumina nanoparticle and HCl gas mixture.}
{Part Fibre Toxicol}
{Rats inhaled alumina nanoparticles at 1-20 mg/m3 with 5 ppm HCl under two temporal patterns - four consecutive days, or once weekly for eight weeks - with assessment at 1 and 28 days post-exposure. The short scenario produced dose-dependent neutrophilic inflammation that resolved by day 28 with partial clearance recovery; the intermittent long scenario left significantly higher retained lung burdens, reduced clearance and persistent neutrophilia at day 28, with the top dose exceeding accepted overload thresholds but no remodelling or genotoxicity. The abstract gives no burden values or dispersion, so the overload claim cannot be checked against the usual volumetric criterion. The transferable point for exposure science: equal cumulative dose delivered intermittently is not equivalent, which time-averaged occupational metrics assume.}
{10.1186/s12989-026-00703-7}

\paperentry{Yang et al. 2026b}
{Molecular Mechanisms of Indoor Environmental Pollution tt-DDE-Induced Blood Pressure Elevation and the Protective Role of Human-Specific Gene CHRFAM7A.}
{Environ Health (Wash)}
{Cooking-oil fumes are stated here as roughly 90\% of indoor particulate emissions, and tt-DDE is their dominant aldehyde. Inhaled tt-DDE raised blood pressure in mice through endothelial damage; CHRFAM7A-transgenic mice resisted the systolic rise and the endothelial dysfunction, with protection traced to STAT3/HO-1 activation, and in vitro tt-DDE drove KLF5 inflammatory signalling via METTL3-dependent m6A modification, reversible by CHRFAM7A overexpression and abolished by HO-1 inhibition. No exposure concentration, duration or blood-pressure magnitude appears in the record, and CHRFAM7A is human-specific, so the transgenic model has no wild-type counterpart. Relevance: kitchen PM sensing conflates mass with a gas-phase aldehyde that may carry the cardiovascular signal.}
{10.1021/envhealth.5c00703}

\paperentry{Yang et al. 2026a}
{The Human Microbiome in Airborne Particulate Matter-Associated Aging-Related Chronic Diseases: An Evidence-Stratified Review.}
{Environ Health (Wash)}
{Evidence-stratified review proposing a PM exposure-microbiome-aging framework across respiratory, gut and skin compartments, with the gut microbiota argued to be the most mechanistically developed interface. The stratification is the contribution: the authors grade epidemiological, animal and multiomics evidence separately rather than pooling them into a single causal story. What it cannot do is quantify anything - no pooled estimates, no dose-response, and the reviewed studies use incompatible PM metrics, so the framework is a research agenda rather than a finding. Carried because microbiome mediation keeps being invoked to explain PM associations that survive adjustment, and because a graded map of where the evidence actually is saves the field repeated litigation of the same weak studies.}
{10.1021/envhealth.5c00852}

\band{Exposure assessment \& modelling\hfill 7 records}{Clay}

\paperentry{Zhang 2026}
{Within-Year PM$_{2.5}$ Exposure Structure Provides Mortality-Predictive Information Beyond the Annual Mean.}
{medRxiv (preprint)}
