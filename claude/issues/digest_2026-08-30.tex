\section*{\sffamily\bfseries\color{Deep}Signal of the day}
\vspace{-1.5mm}{\color{Amber}\rule{\linewidth}{1.1pt}}\vspace{2.5mm}

\begin{keybox}
{\sffamily\bfseries\small\color{Deep}A city-scale low-cost sensor network is used as the
\emph{instrument of record} for a mesoscale meteorology question --- and the calibration
literature published alongside it says why that is still risky.}\\[1.4mm]
\footnotesize
Chen et al.\ (\textit{Environ Sci Technol}) composite \textbf{42 lake-breeze events} over
Chicago in 2022 using one year of the Microsoft Eclipse low-cost sensor network plus
meteorological stations. Weekday lake breezes \emph{reduce} O$_3$ by up to
$\sim$6\,ppb along the northeast shoreline --- the opposite of the sea-breeze ozone
exceedances reported for most coastal regions --- while \emph{raising} PM$_{2.5}$ by up to
$\sim$3\,$\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$ through accumulation in a shallower boundary layer and enhanced
semivolatile partitioning at lower temperature. A 3\,$\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$ signal is precisely
the magnitude that the three calibration papers carried alongside it show is
\emph{recoverable but not free}: Blaga et al.\ report Pearson $r$ moving from
$-0.28$ to $0.89$ in the humid season after a two-step temperature/RH correction, and
from 0.06 to 0.65 in the dry season --- and the uncorrected Bucharest network flagged
exceedances at 8 of 44 sites where the corrected one flagged all 44. The methodological
reading for distributed PM sensing is blunt: an LCS network is now good enough to resolve
a few-$\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$ meteorological forcing, but only after a seasonally stratified,
site-specific correction, and Senarathna et al.\ show a wet-season model applied to
dry-season data carries \textbf{26.57\% MAPE} and does not transfer 115\,km between
Colombo and Kandy.
\end{keybox}

\section*{\sffamily\bfseries\color{Deep}What changed today}
\vspace{-1.5mm}{\color{Amber}\rule{\linewidth}{1.1pt}}\vspace{2.5mm}

\footnotesize
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Calibration transferability is the binding constraint, stated three ways.}
Blaga (Bucharest, 44 Clarity Node-S), Senarathna (Sri Lanka, TSI BlueSky, two climatic
zones) and Sengupta (Oxford OxAria, LSTM) independently report the same failure mode:
a correction fitted at one site and season degrades elsewhere. Sengupta's contribution is
that a sequence model reaches \textbf{9.1\% expanded uncertainty for PM$_{2.5}$} against
the Equivalence Spreadsheet Tool --- inside the EU indicative-monitoring threshold --- by
learning time-lagged environmental dependence a Random Forest treating observations
independently cannot.

\item \textbf{The regulatory consequence of \emph{not} calibrating is now quantified.}
Bucharest's manufacturer pre-calibration understated PM$_{2.5}$ enough that only 18\% of
sensors appeared to breach the EU 25\,$\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$ daily limit; after correction every
site did, averaging $>$60 exceedance days against 35 permitted. This is the strongest
argument in the day's harvest for treating uncalibrated LCS output as unfit for
compliance framing.

\item \textbf{An operational null worth keeping.} Zhang \& Yu's Wuhan time-series
(906 days, 391{,}344 ED visits) recovers textbook associations --- PM$_{2.5}$ AQI
RR \textbf{1.028 (1.015--1.042)} per IQR at lag 0--2, PM$_{10}$ 1.030, NO$_2$ 1.049 ---
and then shows that \emph{adding those variables did not improve} day-ahead demand
forecasting (MAE 33.2 with meteorology, unchanged with pollution). Statistical detectability
and operational value are dissociable, and papers rarely test the second.

\item \textbf{Detection method, not environment, dominated airborne-microplastic
heterogeneity.} Rahmani et al.\ pool 27 studies across 16 countries at
$I^2>99\%$ and find the largest moderator is \emph{instrument} --- FTIR consistently
reporting higher counts than microscopy or Raman. Their indoor/outdoor contrast
(1{,}380 vs.\ 102\,MP\,m$^{-3}$) should not be read as an exposure ratio until sampling
and detection protocols are harmonised, and they say so.

\item \textbf{Two records ship on metadata alone.} The \textit{Aerosol Air Qual Res}
oil-mist ventilation study and the \textit{Environ Int} solid-fuel-PM nitrogen-organics
paper deposited no abstract to Crossref and are absent from Europe PMC, PubMed and
Semantic Scholar. They are listed, not summarised, and carry no extracted numbers.

\item \textbf{Health-side yield was low and skewed to synthesis.} Of 7 PubMed records
entering on 30 August, one was off-topic (flotation process water); the remainder are two
reviews, one animal study, one epigenetics cohort where air pollution is 1 of 25 mixture
components, one composition campaign, and one cross-sectional performance study.
No cardiovascular, respiratory or neuro-epidemiological record entered the window at all.
\end{itemize}

\band{Sensing, forecasting \& instrumentation\hfill 4 records}{Amber}

\paperentry{Chen et al.\ 2026k}
{Lake-breeze effects on urban air quality resolved by a city-wide low-cost sensor network}
{Environ Sci Technol}
{One year (2022) of the Microsoft Eclipse LCS network plus meteorological stations over
Chicago, composited into \textbf{42 lake-breeze events} and 51 weak events confined within a
few kilometres of the shore. Weekday events reduce O$_3$ by up to $\sim$6\,ppb along the
northeast shoreline --- attributed to NO titration in a shallow boundary layer, inflow of
cleaner lake air, and suppressed photochemistry at lower temperature --- while
\emph{raising} PM$_{2.5}$ by up to $\sim$3\,$\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$ via accumulation of local
emissions and semivolatile partitioning. The limitation that matters: event-composited
means over an LCS network inherit that network's calibration bias, and no
reference-monitor validation of the PM$_{2.5}$ increment is reported, so the
3\,$\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$ figure is a network-relative anomaly rather than a traceable
concentration. Directly relevant precedent for using a dense LCS deployment as the primary
instrument in a mesoscale study.}
{10.1021/acs.est.6c09290}

\paperentry{Blaga et al.\ 2026 \textnormal{[backfill]}}
{City-scale calibration of a 44-node PM$_{2.5}$ network for regulatory-compliant assessment}
{PLOS Clim}
{44 Clarity Node-S sensors across Bucharest against National Environmental Protection Agency
reference stations; nine co-located spatial clusters, one used to fit and eight held out.
Manufacturer pre-calibration \textbf{systematically underestimated} PM$_{2.5}$, worst in
summer when secondary aerosol formation dominates. A two-step seasonal model on temperature
and relative humidity alone moved Pearson $r$ from \textbf{0.06 to 0.65} (dry season) and
from \textbf{$-$0.28 to 0.89} (humid season). Applied network-wide, calibrated sensors
average \textbf{$>$60 days/yr} above the EU 25\,$\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$ daily limit against 35
permitted; pre-calibration, only 8 of 44 (18\%) exceeded. Weakness: calibration degrades in
hot dry low-concentration conditions and inter-sensor variability in calibration performance
remains large, so a single network-wide model is not defensible. The 1:8 train/validate
split is the design detail worth copying.}
{10.1371/journal.pclm.0000780}

\paperentry{Senarathna et al.\ 2026 \textnormal{[backfill]}}
{Seasonal and spatial non-transferability of LCS calibration across two tropical climatic zones}
{Environ Monit Assess}
{TSI BlueSky sensors in a harmonised network across Colombo (wet-zone) and Kandy, compared
under linear regression, multiple linear regression and an echo-state network. Harmonised
sensors agreed within \textbf{10\%} of one another. Performance improves monotonically with
averaging window: at 24\,h, wet season $R^2=0.60$, MAE 0.93\,$\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$, MAPE 7.81\%;
dry season $R^2=0.85$, MAE 1.98, MAPE 9.17\%. The two transfer results are the point ---
applying a wet-season model to dry-season data yields \textbf{26.57\% MAPE}, and a
Colombo-fitted model degrades in Kandy, while a Kandy-specific model with temperature and RH
reaches $R^2=0.84$--$0.92$. Limitation: no independent reference co-location is reported for
the second zone at the same duration, so the spatial-transfer claim rests partly on
inter-sensor agreement rather than reference truth.}
{10.1007/s10661-026-15623-4}

\paperentry{Sengupta et al.\ 2026 \textnormal{[backfill]}}
{LSTM calibration of low-cost PM and NO$_2$ sensors against a regulatory equivalence test}
{arXiv (preprint)}
{Sequence model trained on co-located OxAria reference data in Oxford for PM$_{2.5}$,
PM$_{10}$ and NO$_2$, benchmarked against a Random Forest baseline. The framing argument is
that RF treats each hourly observation independently and therefore cannot represent sensor
lag or delayed environmental response; the LSTM with time-lagged features, harmonic time
encodings and interaction terms beats it on $R^2$ across train, validation and test.
The result that carries weight is the external check: validation against Equivalence
Spreadsheet Tool 3.1 gives expanded uncertainties of \textbf{9.1\% (PM$_{2.5}$),
12.42\% (PM$_{10}$) and 22.11\% (NO$_2$)} --- i.e.\ PM meets, and NO$_2$ misses, the
indicative-monitoring bar. Unrefereed, single network, and no drift analysis over a
multi-year horizon, which is where sequence models are most likely to fail.}
{10.48550/arXiv.2604.21527}

\band{Exposure assessment \& modelling\hfill 2 records}{Amber}

\paperentry{Rahmani et al.\ 2026}
{Airborne microplastics indoors and outdoors: pooled concentrations and the instrument problem}
{Rev Environ Health}
{PRISMA systematic review to October 2023: 27 eligible studies across 16 countries, 21
pooled under a random-effects model. Indoor concentrations exceed outdoor --- moderately
polluted indoor environments 1{,}380.28 vs.\ 102.45\,MP\,m$^{-3}$ outdoors --- with indoor
healthcare settings and outdoor urban areas the most contaminated sub-environments.
The authors present the pooled figures as a descriptive summary rather than an estimate,
correctly: heterogeneity is $\mathbf{I^2>99\%}$ and the dominant moderator is
\textbf{detection instrument}, with FTIR reporting consistently higher counts than
microscopy or Raman spectroscopy; sampling duration also matters. Sensitivity analysis
excludes single-outlier dominance. For instrumentation work this is a clean external
demonstration that method-driven variance can exceed environmental variance, which is
exactly the risk in heterogeneous LCS fleets.}
{10.1515/reveh-2025-0059}

\paperentry{Ren et al.\ 2026}
{Vertical contrast in PM$_{2.5}$ composition and secondary formation, Shiyan, central China}
{iScience}
{One year across three urban sites and one mountain site. Annual mountain PM$_{2.5}$ is
\textbf{70\% of urban} levels, but sulfate--nitrate--ammonium is a \emph{larger} fraction
there (49.3\%). SNA is vertically near-homogeneous in spring, summer and autumn
(coefficient of divergence $<$0.2) and stratifies only in winter, when most components are
urban-enriched. Higher nitrogen oxidation ratio (0.27) and SOC/OC (0.65) at altitude
indicate a more aged aerosol. Limitation: a single mountain site cannot separate altitude
from the site's own upwind exposure, and no vertical profiling instrument is used --- the
"vertical distribution" claim rests on a two-point contrast. Relevant to siting logic for
distributed networks: composition, not mass, is what varies with height here, and a
mass-only node would report the mountain site as simply cleaner.}
{10.1016/j.isci.2026.117090}

\paperentry{Gu et al.\ 2026b}
{Nitrogen-containing organics in household solid-fuel combustion PM vs.\ urinary inflammatory

\paperentry{Li et al.\ 2026f}
{Non-uniform oil-mist distribution and local ventilation in a machining workshop}

\band{Mechanistic toxicology\hfill 2 records}{Violet}

\paperentry{Jerome et al.\ 2026}
{World Trade Center dust: neuroinflammation and myelin response at 3 and 12 months}
{J Immunotoxicol}
{Twelve-week-old male spontaneously hypertensive rats given intratracheal WTC dust at
\textbf{2.43 $\pm$ 0.78\,mg/day on two consecutive days}, then euthanised at 3 and 12 months.
At 3 months, increased Black-Gold II myelin staining without change in myelin basic protein
or CNPase; at 12 months, microglial activation (Iba1) and elevated brain
\textbf{IL-1$\beta$}. The authors read the discrepant myelin markers as a compensatory
response preceding degeneration. Three limitations bound the inference: a two-day bolus
instillation is not a chronic inhalation exposure and bypasses the upper airway; the SHR
strain is hypertensive by design, so the neuroinflammatory phenotype is confounded with a
vascular one; and males only. Useful as a mechanistic anchor for the First-Responder
cognitive literature, not as a dose--response.}
{10.1080/1547691X.2026.2722654}

\paperentry{Luo et al.\ 2026b}
{Environmental pollutants and innate immunity via group 2 innate lymphoid cells}
{Clin Rev Allergy Immunol}
{Narrative review covering O$_3$, PM, diesel exhaust particles and cigarette smoke alongside
nanomaterials and microplastics, organised around ILC2 biology. The proposed common route is
epithelial barrier compromise followed by alarmin release --- IL-33, IL-25, TSLP --- with
ILC2s as the sensor coupling environmental detection to type-2 inflammation, plus
non-canonical regulation of ILC2 plasticity and functional adaptation. No new data, no
quantitative synthesis, and PM is not separated from the other exposures, so nothing here
constrains a PM-specific dose or particle-property relationship. Carried as a citation
anchor for the epithelial-alarmin axis in respiratory PM work; tier C.}
{10.1007/s12016-026-09193-z}

\band{Reproductive \& developmental\hfill 1 record}{Sky}

\paperentry{Tanner et al.\ 2026}
{Prenatal exposure mixture and aromatase brain-promoter methylation in cord blood}
{Environ Epigenet}
{Barwon Infant Study birth cohort, \textbf{n = 906}, testing 25 prenatal exposures across
five classes --- manufactured chemicals, air pollution, pharmacological, nutritional and
sunlight-related --- against methylation of the brain-specific \textit{CYP19A1} P1.f promoter
using weighted quantile sum regression. The mixture index was associated with higher P1.f
methylation, \textbf{adjusted mean difference 0.71 (95\% CI 0.11--1.32), $p=0.021$},
interpreted as reduced brain aromatase activity. Bisphenols, reduced sunlight, household
mould, phthalates, low folate and air pollution carried the largest weights. The
inference-threatening limitation is structural: WQS assigns weights within a single index,
so air pollution's individual contribution is neither estimated nor separable, and cord
blood is a proxy for a brain-specific promoter. Tier C for this watch --- PM is one component
of a 25-exposure mixture.}
{10.1093/eep/dvag027}

\band{Burden, policy \& mitigation\hfill 1 record}{Coral}

\paperentry{Simion et al.\ 2026}
{Climate-related health attribution in Southeast Asia: what is and is not being studied}
{Lancet Reg Health West Pac}
{Scoping review, \textbf{5{,}294 records screened, 77 included}. Air pollution is the subject
of \textbf{70\% of studies}, linked to up to 31\% of all-cause mortality and an estimated
\textbf{296{,}000 annual deaths from wildfire smoke during El Ni\~no events}; heat is second,
associated with up to 8.61\% of mortality in Bangkok. Floods, droughts, extreme rainfall,
storms and sea-level rise are near-absent. The paper's own framing --- that the research
portfolio is misaligned with the regional hazard profile --- is the transferable finding; the
attributable fractions themselves are heterogeneously derived across 77 studies with no
standardised attribution framework, which the authors name as the central methodological gap.
For a monitoring programme the operative point is that the region's dominant PM source is
episodic biomass burning, which mass-only fixed networks under-sample in both space and time.}
{10.1016/j.lanwpc.2026.101952}

\band{Other clinical endpoints\hfill 2 records}{Slate}

\paperentry{Donaldson et al.\ 2026}
{Race-day air pollution and marathon finishing time across 2.76 million finishes}
{Sports Med Open}
{\textbf{2{,}756{,}553} net finishing times from six World Marathon Majors (Berlin, Boston,
Chicago, London, New York, Tokyo), 75 marathon-year events, 2010--2024, with race-day NO$_2$
and PM$_{2.5}$ from CAMS global reanalysis and linear mixed models adjusting heat index, wind
and temporal trend. Each 1\,$\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$ NO$_2$ was associated with
\textbf{+1.43\,min (95\% CI 0.66--2.21) in men and +1.56\,min (0.63--2.48) in women}, with a
steep gradient by ability --- recreational 2.12 vs.\ elite 0.34\,min per $\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$,
persisting as a percentage of mean time (0.67\% vs.\ 0.18\%) --- and by age
(2.17\,min in 18--29-year-olds vs.\ 1.32 in $\geq$60). \textbf{PM$_{2.5}$ was null}
($-$0.13\,min, $-$0.67 to 0.40 in men). The exposure is reanalysis at city scale, not
course-level or personal, and NO$_2$ at that resolution is a traffic-proximity proxy; the
NO$_2$/PM$_{2.5}$ divergence is therefore at least partly a measurement-error contrast
rather than a toxicological one. Still the largest exercise-performance exposure dataset
assembled.}
{10.1186/s40798-026-01099-6}

\paperentry{Zhang \& Yu 2026 (preprint)}
{Air pollution is detectable in ED demand and useless for forecasting it}
{Res Sq (preprint)}
{Single-centre daily time-series at Tongji Hospital Guanggu Branch, Wuhan, 1 Jan 2024 to
26 Jun 2026: \textbf{906 dates, 391{,}344 visits}, mean 431.9/day (SD 59.2), falling to
319.3/day over Spring Festival. Quasi-Poisson models give, per IQR of lag 0--2
pollutant-specific AQI, RR \textbf{1.028 (1.015--1.042)} for PM$_{2.5}$,
1.030 (1.017--1.043) for PM$_{10}$, 1.049 (1.034--1.064) for NO$_2$ and an inverse
0.980 (0.965--0.996) for O$_3$; each 1\,$^\circ$C is +0.85\% visits. The contribution is the
temporally-ordered validation: a history-plus-calendar model achieves MAE 33.6 visits/day,
meteorology reduces it to 33.2, and \textbf{adding air-pollution AQI does not improve it at
all}. Single centre, city-level AQI rather than catchment exposure, unrefereed. The inverse
O$_3$ term is almost certainly seasonal confounding. Read as a warning that a statistically
robust exposure--response can carry no incremental predictive information.}
{10.21203/rs.3.rs-10496137/v1}
