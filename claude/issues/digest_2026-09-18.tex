\section*{\sffamily\bfseries\color{Deep}Signal of the day}
\vspace{-1.5mm}{\color{Amber}\rule{\linewidth}{1.1pt}}\vspace{2.5mm}

\begin{keybox}
{\sffamily\bfseries\small\color{Deep}Three independent records today attack the same
assumption from three directions: that a surface-sited, mass-reporting monitor measures
the exposure that matters. Source, height and composition each break it.}\\[1.4mm]
\footnotesize
\textbf{Source.} Zhang et al.\ (\textit{Nat Commun}) separated wildfire-specific from
non-wildfire PM$_{2.5}$ across \textbf{20 US states, 2006--19}, in a self-controlled
design. Per 1\,$\mu$g/m$^3$, wildfire PM$_{2.5}$ raised cardiopulmonary hospitalisation
risk \textbf{10\% (heart failure) to 16\% (asthma)}; non-wildfire PM$_{2.5}$ raised it
\textbf{4.7\% (COPD) to 8.5\% (hypertension)}. Equal mass, unequal hazard, by roughly a
factor of two.
\textbf{Height.} Bai et al.\ (\textit{Atmos Meas Tech}) used Doppler wind lidar over
Hefei to show two dust episodes with opposite surface signatures: in one, a descending
low-level jet drove aerosol down and surface PM$_{10}$ peaked at
\textbf{410\,$\mu$g/m$^3$}; in the other, a stable jet core held the layer aloft and the
surface peak arrived \textbf{$\sim$8\,h} after frontal onset. A surface network reads
clean air while a loaded layer sits overhead.
\textbf{Composition.} Yu et al.\ (\textit{Atmos Chem Phys}) found marine organic-aerosol
light absorption over the Yellow and Bohai Seas rising with both organic-nitrogen
concentration \emph{and} ON/OC ratio --- absorption capability per unit carbon changes
with continental outflow, which is precisely the term a fixed optical calibration assumes
constant.
None of the three is a sensor paper. Together they are the strongest argument this watch
has carried for why the next increment in exposure assessment is source- and
height-resolved, not denser mass.
\end{keybox}

\section*{\sffamily\bfseries\color{Deep}What changed today}
\vspace{-1.5mm}{\color{Amber}\rule{\linewidth}{1.1pt}}\vspace{2.5mm}

\footnotesize
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{A large indoor-monitoring design paper, with a deployment protocol worth
stealing.} Kim et al.\ (\textit{Environ Health}) report the design and early findings of
a residential indoor PM$_{2.5}$ campaign in \textbf{130 older adults with idiopathic
pulmonary fibrosis} in Seoul, 2021--23: MicroPEM runs of five days in each of four
seasons, matched outdoor sampling, 36 chemical components, and infiltration factors of
\textbf{51--70\%} from sulfur, nickel and iron indoor/outdoor ratios. The operational
contribution is non-contact redeployment by postal mail --- the unglamorous problem that
decides whether an unattended network survives a year.

\item \textbf{The issue's evidence base is unusually thin on quantitative precision.}
Six effect estimates were extractable and \textbf{only two carry a confidence interval}.
Both of those are hazard ratios below the null from one UK Biobank analysis; the four
estimates above the null --- all from the wildfire paper --- are point values with no
interval reported in the abstract. Read the forest plot as direction only.

\item \textbf{Four records are carried on metadata alone, and for the first time the gap
is not purely Elsevier's.} A BMC deposit (\textit{Part Fibre Toxicol}, PM and human
embryonic stem cell osteogenic differentiation) and an ACS deposit
(\textit{Environ Sci Technol}) joined the usual two Elsevier same-day deposits with no
abstract recoverable from Crossref, Europe PMC or PubMed. No finding is asserted for any
of the four.

\item \textbf{A useful null, and a reason to distrust it.} Temporao et al.\ found
\emph{no} robust day-to-night association for PM$_{2.5}$ or NO$_2$ with EEG-measured
sleep in a Budapest panel, while day-1 ozone predicted lower day-2 sleep efficiency. But
exposure is city-aggregated from fixed stations: within-city contrast is near zero and
misclassification is far worse for spatially heterogeneous PM$_{2.5}$ than for ozone. The
pollutant that survived is the one the exposure model measures best.

\item \textbf{Two \textit{Environ Sci Technol} Perspectives in one issue argue air into
the exposome programme.} Sunyer-Cald\'u proposes a multi-matrix framework with a unified,
toxicokinetically normalised exposure scale; Godri Pollitt's companion piece is titled
``Air as an Integrative Matrix''. Two of these on the same day is an editorial position,
not a coincidence --- though the first has no data and the second has no recoverable
abstract.

\item \textbf{Consensus returned nothing admissible for the second consecutive issue.}
Ten sensor-calibration papers came back; five were already in the archive on a title
match and all five survivors failed a Crossref \texttt{created}-date check, dating from
January to June 2026. Scholar Gateway, queried for an abstract, again returned only
off-topic Wiley content.
\end{itemize}

\section*{\sffamily\bfseries\color{Deep}Paper-level digest}
\vspace{-1.5mm}{\color{Amber}\rule{\linewidth}{1.1pt}}\vspace{1mm}

{\fontsize{7.4}{9}\selectfont\color{Slate}Ordered by cluster. Each entry gives the
methodological core and the finding that matters, not an abstract paraphrase. Records
marked \textit{metadata only} carry no recoverable abstract and assert no finding.\par}

\band{Neuro, cognition \& mental health\hfill 1 record}{Deep}

\paperentry{Temporao et al.\ 2026}
{Daily associations between outdoor air pollution and sleep quality}
{medRxiv (preprint)}
{Budapest Sleep, Traits and Experiences Study --- seven days per participant of daily
activity and sleep-quality reports plus home EEG giving sleep efficiency, \%REM, onset
latency and total sleep time. Linear mixed models with participant random intercepts,
adjusted for age, sex, BMI, education, weekday, season, temperature, physical activity,
screen exposure and travel. \textbf{Day-1 ozone predicted lower day-2 sleep efficiency
and poorer perceived quality}, surviving multiple-comparison correction, multi-pollutant
models and sensitivity analyses for depression, insomnia and chronotype;
\textbf{no robust association for PM$_{2.5}$ or NO$_2$}. The null is confounded with
exposure-model quality --- city-aggregated station data give near-zero within-city
contrast, and misclassification is worse for PM$_{2.5}$ than for the more spatially
homogeneous ozone. Sample size is not stated in the abstract.}
{10.64898/2026.09.15.26363130}

\band{Cardiovascular \& metabolic\hfill 1 record}{Teal}

\paperentry{Zhang et al.\ 2026}
{Cardiopulmonary hospitalisation risks from wildfire-specific and non-wildfire
PM$_{2.5}$ in 20 US states}
{Nat Commun}
{Self-controlled design over 20 states, 2006--19, contrasting two-year average
wildfire-specific against non-wildfire PM$_{2.5}$. Per 1\,$\mu$g/m$^3$, wildfire
PM$_{2.5}$ carried \textbf{10\% (heart failure) to 16\% (asthma)} higher hospitalisation
risk; non-wildfire PM$_{2.5}$ \textbf{4.7\% (COPD) to 8.5\% (hypertension)}. Associations
were stronger among minorities, metropolitan residents, the less educated and more
deprived communities. The toxicity contrast at equal mass is the contribution, and it
undercuts the mass-only metric every regulatory network and low-cost sensor reports. Two
limits: no confidence interval is given for any of the eight headline risks, so precision
cannot be judged; and wildfire-specific PM$_{2.5}$ is itself modelled, so the toxicity
difference is conditional on a smoke-attribution step whose error is not propagated.}
{10.1038/s41467-026-76974-7}

\band{Respiratory \& allergic\hfill 2 records}{Sage}

\paperentry{San Juan-Reyes et al.\ 2026}
{Short-term ambient air pollution and respiratory hospitalisation in children under five}
{Environ Sci Pollut Res Int}
{266 children under five admitted to one regional IMSS hospital in the Toluca Valley
Metropolitan Zone during 2023; matched case-crossover with conditional logistic
regression, primary window lag 1--7, adjusted for temperature and humidity. PM$_{2.5}$
was associated with admission for viral pneumonia, bacterial pneumonia and asthma, CO
with bronchiolitis, with heterogeneous municipality-level associations. \textbf{The
abstract reports direction only --- not one odds ratio, interval or unit appears} --- so
the effect size is unverifiable from the published summary. 266 events split across four
diagnoses, five pollutants, several lag windows and three municipalities is thin, and
exposure is entirely fixed-station with no personal or indoor component in a setting
where indoor sources plausibly dominate a young child's dose.}
{10.1007/s11356-026-38237-2}

\paperentry{Chen et al.\ 2026}
{Residential green and blue space, genetic susceptibility and incident COPD}
{Int J Chron Obstruct Pulmon Dis}
{385{,}502 UK Biobank participants, Cox models, greenness at 300\,m and 1000\,m buffers
against a COPD polygenic risk score. At 1000\,m: \textbf{green space HR 0.87
(95\% CI 0.83--0.92)}, \textbf{natural environment HR 0.83 (0.78--0.87)}; blue space weak
and inconsistent. No multiplicative or additive interaction with genetic risk, and the
natural-environment association held in every genetic stratum --- so the protective signal
is not running through a susceptible subgroup. The inferential problem is what is absent:
\textbf{no air-pollution term is reported}. Residential greenness is inversely correlated
with traffic PM$_{2.5}$ and NO$_2$ nearly everywhere, so an unadjusted greenness--COPD
association may simply restate a pollution--COPD association.}
{10.2147/copd.s627458}



\band{Mechanistic toxicology\hfill 2 records}{Violet}

\paperentry{Gao et al.\ 2026}
{CCL22-associated immune signatures in air pollution-related atopic dermatitis}
{Front Public Health}
{Shared pollutant--atopic dermatitis targets enriched in cytokine and chemokine
signalling, pattern-recognition receptor activity, IL-17 and Toll-like receptor pathways.
A framework over 15 algorithms and 175 combinations selected plsRglm\,+\,AdaBoost, with
AUC 0.963 in training and 1.000, 0.909 and 0.966 across three validation cohorts, giving
a ten-gene signature (CCL22, CCL5, CSF2, F2RL1, HRH4, ICAM1, IFNG, IL10, IL17A, IL18).
CCL22 was upregulated in lesional samples, localised to dendritic cells and macrophages,
and confirmed in DNFB-induced murine dermatitis. \textbf{The exposure link is the weak
joint: no particulate matter was measured or administered anywhere}, so air pollution
enters only as a database annotation. An AUC of exactly 1.000 indicates leakage or
overfitting rather than performance, and DNFB is a contact hapten, not a particle.}
{10.3389/fpubh.2026.1906298}

\paperentry{Vera-Col\'on et al.\ 2026 \textnormal{---} metadata only}
{Airborne fine particulate matter and human embryonic stem cell osteogenic
differentiation}
{Part Fibre Toxicol}
{BMC deposited this DOI on 18 September with no abstract; Crossref, Europe PMC core,
Europe PMC \texttt{fullTextXML} and the publisher landing page all returned nothing on
per-DOI retry --- \textbf{the first time this gap has appeared on a BMC rather than an
Elsevier deposit}. Carried on title because skeletal development is an endpoint the PM
literature has barely touched, and a human-embryonic-stem-cell osteogenic model would be
a new mechanistic line rather than another airway or endothelial assay. No finding is
asserted: neither particle source, dose, duration nor the direction of the reported
disruption is verifiable from metadata. On the retry list.}
{10.1186/s12989-026-00701-9}

\band{Occupational \& indoor exposure\hfill 2 records}{Clay}

\paperentry{Khan et al.\ 2026}
{Cement industry emissions and respiratory health: global systematic review and
meta-analysis, 2000--2025}
{Front Public Health}
{Eighteen observational studies, PROSPERO CRD420251268913. Random-effects pooling gave
standardised mean differences of \textbf{$-$0.71 for FEV$_1$, $-$0.56 for FVC and $-$0.57
for FEV$_1$/FVC} in exposed groups; chronic cough was significantly more common while
other symptoms were elevated but not significant. Occupational exposure produced greater
FEV$_1$ impairment than residential --- the expected dose ordering and the most reassuring
internal check available. Reporting is the weakness: \textbf{no confidence interval, no
$I^2$ and no prediction interval accompanies any pooled estimate}, so neither precision
nor heterogeneity can be assessed, and SMDs pooled across studies using different
spirometry reference equations with no common dust metric cannot be read as a
dose--response.}
{10.3389/fpubh.2026.1887654}

\paperentry{Assan et al.\ 2026}
{Ambient particulate matter, urinary arsenic and resident health in two Ghanaian mining
communities}
{Health Sci Rep}
{65 residents each from an artisanal and an incorporated mining community, all resident
at least five years; PM by Modular Air Quality System, urinary arsenic by AAS, kidney
function by albumin--creatinine ratio. More artisanal residents fell in the at-risk band
for kidney disease ($p = 0.006$); the incorporated community showed poorer general health
status. Descriptive only: $n = 130$, chi-square on dichotomised uACR with no confounder
adjustment and no exposure--response, and \textbf{neither PM concentrations nor arsenic
levels are numerically reported} despite being the two measured exposures. The 35 and
70\,$\mu$g/m$^3$ figures quoted as WHO standards are the 2005-era interim targets, not
the 2021 guideline values of 15 and 45\,$\mu$g/m$^3$ for 24-hour PM$_{2.5}$ and
PM$_{10}$ --- so the exceedance framing understates the gap.}
{10.1002/hsr2.73246}



\band{Sensing, forecasting \& instrumentation\hfill 4 records}{Amber}

\paperentry{Bai et al.\ 2026}
{Low-level jet evolution and vertical aerosol redistribution: a Doppler wind lidar study}
{Atmos Meas Tech}
{Two dust episodes over Hefei, East China. April 2021: the northwesterly jet core
descended from 2.5\,km to 0.3\,km then re-ascended to 1.0\,km while surface PM$_{10}$
peaked at \textbf{410\,$\mu$g/m$^3$}, with shear above \textbf{0.04\,s$^{-1}$} and
increased spectral width at the lower jet interface and a downward shift of enhanced
backscatter. March 2022: a stable jet core and persistent lower-tropospheric stability
held aerosol aloft, the PM$_{10}$ peak arriving at 16:00\,LT, \textbf{$\sim$8\,h after
inferred frontal onset}. Two regimes --- rapid downward mixing during jet descent versus
delayed surface impact from a decoupled layer. Two cases is mechanism illustration, not
climatology, and the inertial-oscillation attribution rests on hodograph rotation alone.
The clearest statement yet of why a surface-only network can read clean for hours.}
{10.5194/amt-19-5951-2026}

\paperentry{Huang et al.\ 2026}
{Interpretable spatiotemporal coupling network for emission prediction in sludge
gasification}
{Environ Sci Technol}
{PISTC-Net couples a directed process topology with per-feature optimal time lags,
reaching $R^2$ of \textbf{91.18\% for SO$_2$, 80.37\% for NO$_x$ and 78.09\% for PM} on
industrial-scale data. Under simulated abrupt sensor failure it continued tracking
emission trajectories via learned process inertia, and hierarchical attention shifted from
upstream thermochemical zones for SO$_2$ to whole-process attention for PM, flagging
disturbance propagation \textbf{5--8 minutes} before emission peaks. That PM is the
worst-predicted species is the informative result: particle formation runs through
nucleation, condensation and agglomeration paths a topology-plus-lag representation
captures least well, whereas gas-phase sulfur is nearly a mass balance. Single plant, no
external validation site, and interpretability is inferred from attention maps, which are
a saliency artefact rather than a causal readout.}
{10.1021/acs.est.6c09135}

\paperentry{Kalluri et al.\ 2026 \textnormal{---} metadata only}
{Long-term (2008--2024) black carbon over a semi-arid rain-shadow region in southern
peninsular India}
{Atmos Environ}
{The expected same-day Elsevier deposit gap: no abstract in Crossref, Europe PMC or
PubMed on per-DOI retry. Carried on title because a 17-year single-site black-carbon
record in a semi-arid rain-shadow setting is the kind of long baseline trend detection
needs and South Asian networks mostly lack, and because absorption-coefficient records
are what constrain the optical-to-mass conversion every filter- and scattering-based
instrument depends on. No trend, magnitude or source attribution is asserted --- the title
names methods, not results. On the retry list.}
{10.1016/j.atmosenv.2026.122366}

\paperentry{Krajnc Poga\v{c}nik et al.\ 2026 \textnormal{---} metadata only}
{Selective real-time monitoring of respiratory droplets for indoor air safety in
educational environments}
{Build Environ}
{Elsevier same-day deposit, abstract absent from Crossref, Europe PMC and PubMed on
per-DOI retry. Retained because selective real-time discrimination of respiratory
droplets against a background of ambient particles is the hard problem for any optical
counter deployed indoors: a scattering sensor reports total number in a size bin and
cannot by itself separate exhaled aerosol from resuspended dust, so any claimed
selectivity mechanism is worth reading. Whether that selectivity is optical, chemical or
inferential is not recoverable from the title, and no performance claim is asserted.
On the retry list.}
{10.1016/j.buildenv.2026.115281}

\band{Exposure assessment \& modelling\hfill 4 records}{Amber}

\paperentry{Kim et al.\ 2026}
{Residential indoor air monitoring for long-term exposure assessment in susceptible
populations}
{Environ Health}
{130 older adults with idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis, Seoul Metropolitan Area, 2021--23.
Indoor hourly PM$_{2.5}$ averaged \textbf{14.90\,$\mu$g/m$^3$ (SD 36.12)} against
\textbf{20.44 (61.63)} outdoors, higher in winter (20.17) than summer (11.19) and by day
than night, with hourly patterns that contrast rather than track outdoors. Infiltration
factors from median indoor/outdoor ratios of sulfur, nickel and iron were
\textbf{51--70\%}. Source contrasts were large: pets 24.09 vs 13.35, humidifiers 19.19 vs
13.16, smokers 16.76 vs 11.79, cooking 15.14 vs 10.96\,$\mu$g/m$^3$. Every standard
deviation exceeds its mean, so the distribution is episodic and a seasonal mean is a poor
summary; five days per season is thin support for a long-term claim and no health estimate
is reported yet. The transferable piece is the non-contact protocol --- instruments
redeployed by postal mail, which is how an unattended network is actually serviced.}
{10.1186/s12940-026-01334-3}

\paperentry{Yu et al.\ 2026}
{Continental pollutants modulate organic nitrogen and light absorption of marine organic
aerosols}
{Atmos Chem Phys}
{Shipboard sampling over the Yellow and Bohai Seas across three seasons. Aerosol organic
nitrogen averaged \textbf{0.35\,$\pm$\,0.25\,$\mu$g\,N\,m$^{-3}$}, 4--60\% of total
nitrogen, highest in autumn. Anthropogenic secondary pollutants --- aged biomass burning
and secondary nitrate --- supplied \textbf{36--76\% of ON}, 46--83\% of the water-soluble
and 39--89\% of the water-insoluble fraction; in spring dust accounted for 55\% of ON and
above 80\% during a dust episode, while summer carried a marine biogenic component.
Organic-aerosol light absorption rose with both ON concentration \emph{and} ON/OC ratio,
so absorption capability per unit carbon increased with nitrogen enrichment rather than
tracking mass. Three cruises give no annual coverage and apportionment is receptor
modelling on a small campaign. The sensing consequence is direct: an optical instrument
calibrated on one absorption regime is misapplied when composition shifts.}
{10.5194/acp-26-13175-2026}

\paperentry{Sunyer-Cald\'u 2026}
{Beyond blood and urine: decoding the human chemical exposome across matrices and time}
{Environ Sci Technol}
{A Perspective arguing that high-resolution mass spectrometry can profile thousands of
chemical features per sample while study design has not kept pace --- no investigation has
combined external source monitoring, multi-matrix internal biomonitoring and toxicokinetic
normalisation in one longitudinal framework. Three elements are proposed: multi-matrix
sampling extending beyond blood and urine to accumulation archives, developmental matrices
and external media; a Unified Exposure Scale placing heterogeneous matrices on a common
toxicokinetically normalised dosimetric axis; and a two-track hazard-prioritisation
pipeline. No data, no validation, and the Unified Exposure Scale is circular where it
matters --- it needs toxicokinetic parameters that do not exist for the molecular dark
matter it is meant to rank. Logged for how air monitoring is being positioned inside the
Global Human Exposome Project.}
{10.1021/acs.est.6c06509}

\paperentry{Godri Pollitt 2026 \textnormal{---} metadata only}
{Beyond inhalation: air as an integrative matrix for exposome research}
{Environ Sci Technol}
{ACS deposited this DOI on 18 September without an abstract and no text was recoverable
from Crossref, Europe PMC or PubMed on per-DOI retry. Carried because it is the
\textbf{second air-as-exposome-matrix Perspective in this same issue}, alongside
Sunyer-Cald\'u, which makes the pair an editorial signal rather than a coincidence: air
sampling is being argued into the exposome programme as an integrative matrix rather than
merely an inhalation route. No argument or claim is asserted beyond the title.
On the retry list.}
{10.1021/acs.est.6c05073}

\band{Burden, policy \& mitigation\hfill 1 record}{Coral}

\paperentry{Massimi et al.\ 2026}
{Urban green walls under traffic stress: elemental composition of PM deposition and plant
response}
{Environ Sci Technol}
{A nine-species green wall at a high-traffic urban site, followed for a year. PCA on the
insoluble fraction of Al, Cr, Cs, Fe, Mn, Mo, Rb, Sb, Sn, Ti and Zn identified
\textit{Photinia} $\times$ \textit{fraseri} as the highest and most stable retainer of
non-exhaust traffic particles and \textit{Pteris vittata} as the lowest, with SEM--EDS
showing \textbf{up to 100-fold higher retention} in the former. The methodological finding
travels furthest: \textbf{only insoluble elements traced deposition reliably}, because
