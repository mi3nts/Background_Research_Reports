\section*{\sffamily\bfseries\color{Deep}Signal of the day}
\vspace{-1.5mm}{\color{Amber}\rule{\linewidth}{1.1pt}}\vspace{2.5mm}

\begin{keybox}
{\sffamily\bfseries\small\color{Deep}China's 2026 PM$_{2.5}$ standard (GB 3095-2026, 35 to 25\,$\mu$g/m$^3$ annual) would, at full compliance in 2024, avert 4.3 times the deaths that compliance with the old limit would, lifting PM$_{2.5}$-specific progress toward the WHO 2040 halving target from 66.2\,\% to 83.4\,\%.}\\[1.4mm]
\footnotesize
Peking University, \textit{Environmental Science and Ecotechnology}; comparative risk assessment on gridded PM$_{2.5}$ and population, 2013--2024, cause-specific exposure--response functions. Gains concentrate in COPD, stroke, lung cancer, lower respiratory infection and IHD; dementia and type 2 diabetes barely move.
\textbf{Where it is weak:} a counterfactual, not an observed mortality change --- the 4.3$\times$ ratio is set by the shape of the exposure--response functions between 25 and 35\,$\mu$g/m$^3$, and the abstract gives neither absolute deaths nor uncertainty intervals.
\textbf{Why it matters here:} enforcement at 25\,$\mu$g/m$^3$ moves the regulatory margin into the range where uncorrected low-cost sensor bias (humidity, composition) is a large fraction of the exceedance being judged.\par
\end{keybox}

\paperentry{Peking University China GB 3095-2026 NAAQS 25 $\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$ halving target PM$_{2.5}$ attributable mortality comparative risk assessment 2013-2024}
{Halving PM$_{2.5}$ mortality by 2040: China's tightened air quality standard and residual disease burdens.}
{Environ Sci Ecotechnol}
{Comparative risk assessment for mainland China, 2013-2024, combining gridded PM$_{2.5}$, gridded population and cause-specific exposure-response functions, scored against the WHO 2040 halving target and China's 2026 revision of the annual PM$_{2.5}$ standard (GB 3095-2026) from 35 to 25 $\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$. In 2024, full compliance with 25 $\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$ would avert 4.3 times as many deaths as compliance with 35 $\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$, and PM$_{2.5}$-specific progress toward halving rises from 66.2\% to 83.4\%. Gains concentrate in COPD, stroke, lung cancer, LRI and IHD; dementia and type 2 diabetes respond little, and the dementia function is weakly supported. The abstract gives no absolute deaths or uncertainty intervals; the result is a counterfactual inherited from the chosen exposure-response shapes, not observed mortality change. Relevance: compliance below 25 $\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$ is a range where low-cost sensor bias and humidity error become a large fraction of the margin being regulated.}
{10.1016/j.ese.2026.100781}

\paperentry{Arba Minch Bule Hora GBD 2023 Eastern Sub-Saharan Africa stroke attributable burden household air pollution 1990-2023}
{Burden of stroke attributable to environmental, behavioral, and metabolic risk factors in Eastern Sub-Saharan Africa, from 1990 to 2023.}
{J Med Access}
{Secondary analysis of GBD 2023 for Eastern Sub-Saharan Africa. In 2023, stroke attributable to all risk factors accounted for 110,013 deaths (359 per 100,000; 95\% UI 275-449) and 7,702 DALYs per 100,000 (6,043-9,495). Environmental risks contributed 4,146 DALYs per 100,000 (2,621-5,668), behind metabolic (6,286) and ahead of behavioural (2,505); household air pollution ranked third among single risks after systolic blood pressure and diet. No significant annualised decline in any risk category from 1990 to 2023. The abstract does not separate household from ambient PM within the environmental category, and every number is a GBD model output with sparse local monitoring or cohort input for this region. Relevance: the household-fuel PM term in East Africa rests on fuel-use proxies; kitchen-level sensor data are what would calibrate it.}
{10.1177/27550834261493111}
