\section*{\sffamily\bfseries\color{Deep}Signal of the day}
\vspace{-1.5mm}{\color{Amber}\rule{\linewidth}{1.1pt}}\vspace{2.5mm}

\begin{keybox}
{\sffamily\bfseries\small\color{Deep}Plantower's new PMSX003N is better than the PMS5003T
at dust and worse at humidity --- and the two failure modes are the same failure mode.}\\[1.4mm]
\footnotesize
Kaur, Mangin and Kelly (\textit{Aerosol Sci.\ Technol.}) co-located three PMSX003N, three
PMS5003T and three OPC-N3 against FEM monitors at a Utah DEQ site for five months
(Dec 2025--Apr 2026), through dust events, winter inversions, freezing fog, mist, snow and
rain. During dust the PMSX003N tracked the reference where its predecessor did not
(\textbf{PM$_{2.5}$ slope 1.85--1.91, $R^2>0.7$} against \textbf{slope 0.15, $R^2<0.37$}
for the PMS5003T; PM$_{10}$ \textbf{slope 0.91--1.07} against \textbf{$\sim$0.02}), and
under high humidity it \textbf{substantially overestimated both fractions}. Laboratory
size-binning explains both: the sensor \textbf{preferentially assigns particles $>$1\,$\mu$m
to the 2.5--5\,$\mu$m bin}. Coarse mineral dust is thereby recovered rather than lost, and
hygroscopically grown droplets are recruited into the same oversized bin. The design change
that fixes coarse-PM response is the design change that makes RH correction mandatory ---
so a humidity-uncorrected PMSX003N network is not a drop-in upgrade over a PMS5003T
network, it is a different bias. Note the PM$_{2.5}$ slope: even at its best the sensor
reads roughly \textbf{1.9$\times$} the reference during dust.
\end{keybox}

\section*{\sffamily\bfseries\color{Deep}What changed today}
\vspace{-1.5mm}{\color{Amber}\rule{\linewidth}{1.1pt}}\vspace{2.5mm}

\footnotesize
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{The largest single-day issue this watch has shipped, and it is instrument-led.}
38 records against a previous single-day maximum of 36 (27 August); only the retrospective
July back-scan is bigger. \textbf{Eleven} sit in \textit{Sensing, forecasting \&
instrumentation} and \textbf{twelve} in \textit{Exposure assessment \& modelling}: 23 of
38 before a single health endpoint. Volume is a one-day artefact of a fat \textit{ES\&T}
by-ISSN deposit, but the sensing half is substantive rather than incidental.

\item \textbf{Two independent low-cost-sensor papers, and they disagree about what LCS are for.}
Kaur et al.\ treat the sensor as an instrument to be characterised against a reference;
Guth et al.\ (\textit{Atmosphere}) treat it as the only practical way to get
\emph{time-resolved} emission factors across \textbf{19 prescribed burns}, where CO EFs
swing \textbf{+3260\%} with combustion phase. The second use case tolerates a bias the
first would reject, because within-burn contrast is the measurand.

\item \textbf{Component-resolved epidemiology produced two effect sizes that cannot both be
read the same way.} Chen K et al.\ report per-IQR frailty HRs of
\textbf{1.142 (1.055--1.236)} for organic matter and \textbf{1.131 (1.041--1.229)} for
black carbon in CHARLS ($n$=9{,}603) --- component ordering above bulk PM$_{2.5}$
(1.100), and biologically legible. Chen Y et al.\ report \textbf{OR 2.23 (2.19--2.27)}
for sulfate on congenital heart disease across \textbf{2.6 million} Guangdong births,
with the effect peaking in \emph{pregnancy week 1}. A doubling of CHD odds with a CI of
width 0.08 is not a plausible causal contrast; it is what a spatially smooth
satellite exposure does against a very large register. Cite the first, quarantine the second.

\item \textbf{A perspective worth reading against your own measurement plan.}
Gao et al.\ (\textit{ES\&T}) argue that the recurring \emph{inverse} O$_3$--health
associations are an artefact of measuring O$_3$ as a proxy for an unmeasured oxidant
field: low O$_3$ can mark active oxidative chemistry producing toxic secondary products
nobody instruments for. The same argument applies verbatim to PM mass as a proxy for
particle oxidative potential.

\item \textbf{Radon methodology, transferable wholesale.} Giang et al.\ co-located
\textbf{45 consumer radon monitors (15 models $\times$ 3 units)} against a reference for
98 days in an occupied home: 8 of 15 models within $\pm$25\% relative percent error,
worst-case \textbf{$-$47\% to $+$220\%}, intra-model CV mostly under 15\%. Different
analyte, identical QA problem to a consumer PM network --- and a rare published estimate
of \emph{between-model} spread at consumer price points.

\item \textbf{Africa, twice, and both times as a gap statement.} Wright et al.\ find only
13 African case-control studies in a decade (indoor PM$_{2.5}$ 96--177\,$\mu$g/m$^3$,
ambient to 259) and \textbf{none} examining pollution--heat interaction; Hulme et al.\
link home and school exposures for 919 Accra schoolchildren. A third African record
(Lagos speciation, Hopke a co-author) ships on metadata only.

\item \textbf{Nil legs.} Consensus nil for the fifth run; arXiv answered without a 429 but
its newest submission is \textbf{2026-08-20}; OpenAlex 429; trial registry a true zero.
\end{itemize}

\section*{\sffamily\bfseries\color{Deep}Paper-level digest}
\vspace{-1.5mm}{\color{Amber}\rule{\linewidth}{1.1pt}}\vspace{1mm}

{\fontsize{7.4}{9}\selectfont\color{Slate}Ordered by cluster. Each entry gives the
methodological core and the finding that matters, not an abstract paraphrase.\par}

\band{Reproductive \& developmental\hfill 2 records}{Sky}

\paperentry{Xu ZL et al.\ 2026}
{Component-specific PM$_{2.5}$ windows and oocyte yield in assisted reproduction}
{Biomed Environ Sci}
{\textbf{51{,}122 ART cycles} with individual PM$_{2.5}$ and its sulfate, nitrate,
ammonium, organic-matter and black-carbon fractions assigned over recent (0--3 months),
distal (4--12 months) and cumulative windows; negative binomial regression on total,
mature and normally fertilised oocyte counts, DLNM for window identification, mixture
models for joint effect. Higher exposure tracked poorer oocyte outcomes throughout, with
\textbf{stronger effects for the distal window} and two sensitive periods --- 1 month and
6--11 months before retrieval. Sulfate and ammonium dominated the mixture. The 6--11 month
window is biologically coherent with primordial-to-antral follicle transit, which is the
paper's strongest argument. Its weakness is that ART patients are a selected population and
the exposure model is residential, so a distal window is also the window over which
residential misclassification is largest.}
{10.3967/bes2026.072}

\paperentry{Chen Y et al.\ 2026}
{PM$_{2.5}$ components in early pregnancy and congenital heart disease}
{Sci Rep}
{\textbf{2{,}601{,}300 live births and stillbirths}, Guangdong 2014--2017, with
satellite-derived component exposures resolved to the week of gestation and quantile
g-computation for mixture weights. Effects peak in \textbf{pregnancy week 1}: sulfate
\textbf{OR 2.23 (2.19--2.27)}, black carbon \textbf{1.95 (1.91--1.98)}, joint weights
86.8\% and 13.2\%. Read the intervals before the point estimates. A near-doubling of CHD
odds with a 95\% CI of width 0.08 implies a precision no exposure model at this spatial
scale supports, and a week-1 peak in a satellite product with month-scale effective
resolution is more consistent with seasonal and spatial confounding than with a
window-specific teratogenic mechanism. The scale of the register is real; the causal
contrast is not established.}
{10.1038/s41598-026-59940-7}

\band{Respiratory \& allergic\hfill 2 records}{Sage}

\paperentry{Wright et al.\ 2026}
{Air pollution and respiratory outcomes in Africa --- a review that mostly documents absence}
{Curr Environ Health Rep}
{PRISMA search of PubMed, ScienceDirect and Elicit for African case-control studies
2015--2025 returned \textbf{13 studies from 10 countries}. Reported concentrations are the
finding: indoor PM$_{2.5}$ \textbf{96--177\,$\mu$g/m$^3$} in biomass-using homes and
ambient PM$_{2.5}$ to \textbf{259\,$\mu$g/m$^3$}, an order of magnitude above WHO guidance.
Nitrogen oxides tracked reduced lung function in children; household air pollution tracked
under-five mortality and low birthweight; associations with acute respiratory infection
were inconsistent across settings. \textbf{Only three studies measured temperature and none
examined heat--pollution interaction}, which is the stated motivation for the review and
which it therefore cannot deliver. Restricting to case-control designs also excludes the
cohort and panel literature, so this is a floor on the African evidence base, not a census.}
{10.1007/s40572-026-00561-7}

\paperentry{Cui L et al.\ 2026}
{Epidemic thunderstorm asthma in China as an \textit{Artemisia} phenotype}
{Clin Rev Allergy Immunol}
{54 primary studies (43 international, 11 Chinese --- Yulin, Hohhot, Chongqing, Lanzhou,
Shenzhen) from 436 records, risk of bias by Newcastle--Ottawa and Murad, certainty by
GRADE. Three patterns recur: Chinese events are associated with \textbf{mugwort
(\textit{Artemisia})} rather than ryegrass sensitisation; \textbf{40--60\% of Chinese
cases and up to 78\% of Australian and UK cases had no prior asthma diagnosis}; and
individual Chinese localities report recurrence. No meta-analysis, and GRADE certainty for
direct pollen rupture, pollution-mediated severity and pre-emptive treatment is
\textbf{low to very low}. The measurement gap is explicit and is the actionable part:
Chinese reports identify sensitisation only to genus level and \textbf{do not speciate the
airborne pollen}, so no aerobiological trigger threshold can be set. The authors' first
research priority is field aerosol sampling.}
{10.1007/s12016-026-09194-y}

\band{Mechanistic toxicology\hfill 3 records}{Violet}

\paperentry{Na HW et al.\ 2026}
{Airborne metals reconstructed from worker sebum, then dosed onto skin}
{J Toxicol}
{ICP-MS on sebum from \textbf{62 indoor and 40 outdoor workers} in Shanghai across 24
metals, with large outdoor--indoor contrasts (cobalt 925$\times$, arsenic 135$\times$,
barium 29$\times$, manganese 17$\times$). Sebum-mimicking mixtures were then formulated at
the measured profiles and applied to keratinocytes, sebocytes and ex vivo scalp skin: the
outdoor mixture reduced viability, raised lipid peroxidation, induced $\gamma$H2AX foci,
upregulated \textit{IL1A}, \textit{IL1B}, \textit{TNFA}, \textit{IL6} and \textit{PTGS2},
and activated NF-$\kappa$B and MAPK. The design is the interesting part --- a biomonitoring
matrix used to specify the in vitro dose rather than an arbitrary slurry concentration.
The weakness is that sebum metal load is a cumulative deposition proxy of unknown
relation to inhaled or dermally absorbed dose, and indoor/outdoor is a crude exposure
contrast with occupation confounded into it.}
{10.1155/jt/7925949}

\paperentry{Wang Y et al.\ 2026}
{$\alpha$-Dicarbonyl aqueous products modulate cellular ROS in opposite directions}
{Environ Sci Technol Lett}
{Glyoxal or methylglyoxal reacted with ammonium sulfate or glycine in the aqueous phase;
HRMS shows ammonium routes yield imidazole-type nitrogen heterocycles and glycine routes
yield more pyrrole-related species. Alone, neither product set induces much ROS in A549
cells. Co-exposed with 1,4-naphthoquinone, Cu$^{2+}$ or both, \textbf{imidazole-dominated
mixtures enhance ROS and pyrrole-rich mixtures suppress it}, with acellular assays
attributing the split to promotion versus suppression of quinone redox cycling and
metal-associated pathways. This is a direct challenge to any oxidative-potential assay
read as a scalar: the same secondary organic aerosol aging pathway can raise or lower
measured ROS depending on the nitrogen source. Cell-line work at unstated
atmospheric relevance of concentration, so treat as mechanism, not as an exposure-response.}
{10.1021/acs.estlett.6c00722}

\paperentry{Cao et al.\ 2026}
{Black carbon interfacial chemistry and multiscale aging}
{Environ Sci Atmos}
{A review that puts BC \emph{particle state} --- surface reactivity, coating thickness,
morphology, aging timescale --- rather than BC mass at the centre, and follows it through
to optics, cloud activation, removal, lifetime and toxicity. The framing matters for
instrumentation because every one of those downstream properties is a function of a
variable that filter-based absorption and single-particle soot photometry constrain only
partially, and that no low-cost optical sensor constrains at all. No primary data and no
quantitative synthesis; the Crossref deposit carries only a graphical-abstract blurb, so
the parameterisation recommendations were not verifiable at digest time. Listed as a
reference framework rather than as evidence.}
{10.1039/d6ea00093b}

\band{Occupational \& indoor\hfill 3 records}{Clay}

\paperentry{Srinivasan et al.\ 2026}
{Occupational wood dust and lung function --- pooled, and heterogeneous}
{Natl Med J India}
{PRISMA review of PubMed and Google Scholar; 55 studies qualitatively, 16/14/9 pooled by
outcome. Random-effects pooled mean differences in percent predicted:
\textbf{FEV$_1$ $-$8.57 (95\% CI $-$12.07 to $-$5.08)}, \textbf{FVC $-$7.43
($-$11.35 to $-$3.51)}, \textbf{PEFR $-$13.43 ($-$25.14 to $-$1.72)}. Sawmill workers
trended obstructive, furniture workers restrictive. Three things limit it:
\textbf{I$^2$ exceeds 95\%} on every pooled outcome, the funnel plot indicates publication
bias, and exposure is binary --- no study contributed a dust concentration, so there is no
exposure--response and no basis for an occupational limit. Searching only PubMed and
Google Scholar is thin for an occupational literature that sits substantially in
non-indexed regional journals.}
{10.25259/nmji\_668\_2024}

\paperentry{Feng R et al.\ 2026}
{Clean heating renovation cuts nitro-PAHs but not nitrated phenols}
{Environ Sci Technol Lett}
{Size-resolved \emph{personal} exposure to particulate nitrated phenols (NPs) and
nitro-PAHs among rural users of clean coal, raw coal chunk and biomass, paired with A549
cytotoxicity and NF-$\kappa$B reporter assays. Biomass users carried up to
\textbf{11.5$\times$} the PM$_{2.5}$-bound n-PAH exposure of clean-coal users, while NPs
varied \textbf{$\leq$1.3-fold} across fuels (clean coal 64.0$\pm$12.1 vs raw coal
57.4$\pm$25.8\,ng/m$^3$, $p$=0.545). Toxicologically the two classes diverge as well:
n-PAHs were more cytotoxic by IC$_{50}$, NPs activated NF-$\kappa$B more strongly via
p-I$\kappa$B-$\alpha$ and COX-2. The policy reading is sharp --- a fuel switch evaluated on
PM mass or on PAHs alone will score a benefit that the NP fraction does not deliver.
Group sizes are not stated in the abstract and the in vitro work uses representative
compounds, not the collected particles.}
{10.1021/acs.estlett.6c00629}

\paperentry{Jamali et al.\ 2026}
{All-cellulose HEPA nanofilter in a wooden frame}
{Int J Biol Macromol}
{Fog-sprayed, freeze-dried bacterial-cellulose nanofibres on a wood-derived
cellulose-microfibre tissue substrate, in a modular wooden frame with a starch-adhesive
gasket. Removal efficiency rises with BCNF grammage to \textbf{2\,g/m$^2$} and falls above
it --- consistent with pore closure raising velocity through the remaining paths. Optimum
gives \textbf{99.998\% PM$_{0.3}$ at 150\,Pa}, inside the EN~1822 H13--H14 band, and
PM$_{0.3}$ capture \emph{peaks at high RH} under cyclic humidity, the opposite of the
usual electret degradation. The claim being made is biodegradability at H13 performance,
and the tests reported are single-pass efficiency and pressure drop; there is no loading
lifetime, no microbial growth assessment on a cellulose substrate held at high RH, and no
mechanical durability data. Promising material, incomplete filter.}
{10.1016/j.ijbiomac.2026.154178}

\band{Sensing, forecasting \& instrumentation\hfill 11 records}{Amber}

\paperentry{Kaur et al.\ 2026}
{Plantower PMSX003N against FEM through a Utah winter}
{Aerosol Sci Technol}
{Three PMSX003N, three PMS5003T and three OPC-N3 co-located with FEM monitors at a Utah
DEQ station for five months, plus laboratory size-binning against monodisperse DOS
aerosol. The laboratory result drives everything: the PMSX003N
\textbf{preferentially assigns particles $>$1\,$\mu$m to the 2.5--5\,$\mu$m bin}. In dust
events this recovers coarse mass its predecessor loses --- \textbf{PM$_{2.5}$ slope
1.85--1.91, $R^2>0.7$} vs \textbf{0.15, $R^2<0.37$}; \textbf{PM$_{10}$ slope 0.91--1.07}
vs \textbf{$\sim$0.02} --- and under freezing fog, mist, snow and rain it substantially
overestimates both fractions. Three units per model over one site-season is a small basis
for a between-unit claim, and a slope near 1.9 is a large residual bias even where the
correlation is good. Humidity-dependent correction is not optional for this part.}
{10.1080/02786826.2026.2716649}

\paperentry{Guth et al.\ 2026}
{Time-resolved emission factors for 19 prescribed burns, measured with low-cost sensors}
{Atmosphere}
{Emission factors for CO, PM$_{2.5}$, EC, OC and speciated organics across \textbf{19
prescribed fires} in Colorado and southeastern Georgia, using multiple low-cost monitors
per burn to get temporal resolution that filter-based EF campaigns cannot reach. CO EFs
are driven by combustion phase --- \textbf{+3260\%} at low modified combustion efficiency,
i.e.\ smouldering. Broadcast and pile burns are compared directly, which is rare. The
methodological point for a distributed network is that the LCS bias that disqualifies a
sensor for regulatory-equivalent concentration is tolerable here because the measurand is a
ratio within a plume; the risk is that any RH- or size-dependent bias in the PM channel
propagates straight into the PM$_{2.5}$/CO EF ratio, and the abstract does not report the
sensor correction applied.}
{10.3390/atmos17090867}

\paperentry{Xing et al.\ 2026}
{DeepAQM --- observation-constrained ML surrogate for the NOAA forecast system}
{Environ Sci Technol}
{A ConvLSTM--ResNet fusion reconstructs spatially continuous surface PM$_{2.5}$ and O$_3$
from VIIRS AOD, TEMPO NO$_2$ and EPA AirNow, and supplies them as initial conditions for
72\,h UFS-AQM forecasts over the continental US. It reproduces UFS-AQM spatial patterns
($R^2>0.9$ for O$_3$, to 0.80 for PM$_{2.5}$, normalised mean bias within $\pm$10\%).
The result that matters: observation-updated initial conditions lift site-level $R^2$ in
the first 10 forecast hours from \textbf{$\sim$0.10--0.15 to 0.40--0.50 for PM$_{2.5}$},
and observation-based fine-tuning carries overall $R^2$ to \textbf{0.41 for PM$_{2.5}$}
across the full 72\,h. Note what that number is: a fourfold gain at short lead, decaying
to a forecast that still explains under half the variance. TEMPO makes this a
current-generation result rather than a reanalysis exercise.}
{10.1021/acs.est.6c04600}

\paperentry{Veselovskii et al.\ 2026}
{Five-channel fluorescence lidar retrieves smoke mass inside the boundary layer}
{Atmos Meas Tech}
{Smoke and urban aerosol have distinct fluorescence spectra, and a multi-channel
fluorescence lidar exploits that to separate them and retrieve smoke mass concentration
with an estimated detection threshold of \textbf{$\sim$0.1\,$\mu$g/m$^3$}. Over Moscow,
smoke was detected in \textbf{59 of 67 sessions between April and October 2024}, mostly
from long-range Atlantic transport; only 12 episodes traced to southern Russian fires.
Within the PBL, transported North American wildfire smoke contributed
\textbf{$\sim$1\,$\mu$g/m$^3$} against \textbf{up to 50\,$\mu$g/m$^3$} for regional fires.
This is the source-resolved column-to-surface capability that mass-only networks lack, and
0.1\,$\mu$g/m$^3$ is a genuinely low threshold. Single site, single instrument, and mass
retrieval requires an assumed fluorescence-to-mass conversion whose uncertainty is the
main open term.}
{10.5194/amt-19-5587-2026}

\paperentry{Zhang Z et al.\ 2026}
{Microfluidic multispectral diffraction for black carbon quantification}
{Anal Chem}
{A microfluidic chip does inertial separation, low-velocity sheath-flow regulation and
high-voltage electrostatic capture to enrich submicron BC in the imaging region
(\textbf{92\% enrichment efficiency}); a lensless diffraction system then acquires
hyperspectral fingerprints over 400--800\,nm, which a graph model maps to concentration.
Over \textbf{0.01--0.20\,mg/m$^3$}, $R^2$=0.98. The discriminating claim is more
interesting than the calibration: the spectral fingerprint separates BC from
