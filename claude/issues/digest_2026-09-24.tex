\section*{\sffamily\bfseries\color{Deep}Signal of the day}
\vspace{-1.5mm}{\color{Amber}\rule{\linewidth}{1.1pt}}\vspace{2.5mm}

\begin{keybox}
{\sffamily\bfseries\small\color{Deep}Humidity turned up on both sides of the ledger today ---
as a stroke risk factor in a 17,000-person cohort, and as the known bias term in the optical
sensors that increasingly supply the PM$_{2.5}$ those cohorts are built on.}\\[1.4mm]
\footnotesize
Sun and colleagues (\textit{Front Public Health}) split \textbf{CHARLS} (n = 17,197) at the
median of annual PM$_{2.5}$ and of \textbf{ambient relative humidity}: high PM$_{2.5}$ gives
\textbf{HR 1.184 (95\,\% CI 1.083--1.295)} for incident stroke, high humidity
\textbf{HR 1.112}, and the joint high--high group \textbf{HR 1.361 (1.171--1.582)}, with frailty
mediating \textbf{18.1\,\%} of the PM$_{2.5}$ association. Taken alone this is a modest cohort
result with a crude exposure contrast. \textbf{Why it is the signal:} light-scattering sensors
overread PM$_{2.5}$ as particles take up water, and that error is \textbf{positively correlated
with RH by construction}. If exposure surfaces are increasingly fused with or derived from
low-cost networks, an uncorrected humidity bias will manufacture exactly this pattern --- a
PM$_{2.5}$ effect that grows where it is humid, and a ``humidity effect'' that is partly
mis-measured particle mass. This study used reanalysis-grade inputs, so it is not itself
contaminated; the point is forward-looking. \textbf{Three other records today lean on the same
axis:} Contini et al.\ show fine and coarse fractions decoupling under Saharan dust over a
decade in Lecce (the regime where Plantower-class sensors undercount); the metadata-only
Lawson et al.\ deposit (\textit{AS\&T}) is titled on \textbf{humidity-dependent bias in photoacoustic
absorption} --- a reference method for black carbon --- and Zhang et al.\ find satellite and
monitor PM$_{2.5}$ giving near-identical preterm-birth ORs (\textbf{1.089 vs 1.091} per
10\,$\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$), which is the benchmark any sensor-derived exposure should have to meet.
\textbf{The operational rule:} RH correction belongs upstream of the health model, and a joint
PM\,$\times$\,humidity term estimated from sensor-derived exposure should be treated as
suspect until shown otherwise.\par
\end{keybox}

% -------------------------------------------------------------- what changed
\section*{\sffamily\bfseries\color{Deep}What changed today}
\vspace{-1.5mm}{\color{Amber}\rule{\linewidth}{1.1pt}}\vspace{2.5mm}

\footnotesize
\begin{itemize}

\item \textbf{Black carbon carried the cerebrovascular signal.} Wang et al.\ (CHARLS, 8,724
adults, 610 incident strokes over a median 5.0 years) assign TAP-reanalysis PM$_{2.5}$ and five
constituents: per IQR, \textbf{PM$_{2.5}$ HR 1.55 (1.35--1.79)} and \textbf{black carbon HR
1.77 (1.54--2.03)}, with BC taking the largest quantile g-computation weight (\textbf{28.6\,\%}).
Constituents are collinear model output and IQRs differ, so the ranking is weaker than the HRs
suggest --- but it follows the 23 September size- and source-resolved stroke result in pointing
at \textbf{carbonaceous, combustion-derived particles}, the fraction a scattering-only sensor
does not resolve.

\item \textbf{The instrumentation axis came in through long records and hybrid networks, not
new devices.} Contini et al.\ (\textit{Environ Pollut}, tier A) report ten years at the Lecce
observatory: PM$_{2.5}$ \textbf{$-$2.58\,\%/yr}, eBC \textbf{$-$3.17\,\%/yr}, total particle
number \textbf{$-$2.99\,\%/yr}, coarse mass flat, and a \textbf{mass absorption cross-section
stable at 12.4\,$\pm$\,2.4\,m$^2$\,g$^{-1}$} across the decade. A Kathmandu preprint combines
regulatory monitors with a \textbf{low-cost sensor network} to resolve a core/periphery
gradient (\textbf{40.71 vs 27.22\,$\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$} annual PM$_{2.5}$) --- but reports no sensor
model, count or co-location statistics, so the gradient cannot yet be separated from sensor
bias.

\item \textbf{Grid resolution biases environmental-justice estimates, quantified.} Zhou et al.\
(\textit{GeoHealth}) run CMAQ at 1 and 12\,km for 30\,\% EV adoption in Atlanta: the headline
PM$_{2.5}$ benefit is a negligible \textbf{0.06\,$\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$}, but \textbf{12-km grids
underestimated minority-group exposure by up to 25\,\% for NO$_2$ and 10\,\% for PM$_{2.5}$}.
That resolution penalty is a number, not an argument, for dense monitoring in disparity work.

\item \textbf{Indoor PM got five records, and two of them show what goes wrong without
measurement.} An Anyang rural-retrofit study finds envelope tightening cut thermal load
\textbf{52.2\,\%} while \textbf{raising winter indoor PM$_{2.5}$ 25.8\,\%} from solid fuel; an
\textit{Indoor Air} review says the retrofit evidence base mostly lacks controls and duration.
A randomised crossover of true vs sham purification (\textbf{38 adults, 47.45\,\% indoor
PM$_{2.5}$ reduction}) and an older-adult home-hazard tool (\textbf{61\,\% of 41 homes above
9\,$\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$} on consumer monitors) both rest on personal or consumer sensors whose
calibration the abstracts do not report.

\item \textbf{The PubMed sensing leg returned one record after four zero days, and the connector
again supplied the difference.} The harvester's instrumentation query produced \textbf{1} hit
(a proteomics study, not a sensing paper). The PubMed connector on the same \texttt{[EDAT]} day
returned \textbf{11 PMIDs} worth screening that no harvester leg had, of which \textbf{6 were
admitted} --- including the tier-A \textit{ATVB} Los Angeles--Beijing metabolomics panel and
the \textit{J Hazard Mater} welding nanoparticle study. The harvester's PubMed legs saw neither;
without the connector the mechanistic block would have shipped thinner.

\item \textbf{Metadata-only held at 7, now 20\,\% of a larger issue.} Six Elsevier deposits
(\textit{Atmos Pollut Res} $\times$3, \textit{Atmos Environ}, \textit{Atmos Res}, \textit{Sci
Total Environ}) and one Taylor \& Francis (\textit{AS\&T}) arrived without abstracts; Semantic
Scholar was tried as a fourth route and \textbf{404'd on all seven} (not yet indexed). The
Dhaka heavy-metals record (\textit{Environ Sci Atmos}) was admitted on its deposited graphical
summary alone and says so. \textbf{Retry list: 44.}

\end{itemize}

\band{Neuro / mental health\hfill 3 records}{Deep}

\paperentry{Rodulfo-Cardenas et al. 2026}
{APOE genotype shapes persistent behavioral and molecular alterations following early postnatal particulate matter exposure in humanized apoE3 and apoE4 mice.}
{Neurotoxicology}
{Humanized apoE3 and apoE4 mice of both sexes inhaled NIST fine PM (SRM 2787) or saline for 1 h/day across postnatal days 4-14, then underwent behavioural testing to PND96 and hippocampal/striatal expression profiling. Genotype, not PM, was the main determinant: apoE4 animals were more anxiety-like with hippocampal reorganisation of synaptic, glutamatergic, glial and redox pathways. PM produced only subtle social-behaviour effects and suggestive sex- and region-dependent marker changes. The authors state the chamber concentration was a nominal theoretical maximum of 3789 $\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$ and was never measured - an unquantified dose, which makes the null-leaning PM result uninterpretable in dose-response terms. Relevance: a clean example of why exposure chambers need the same real-time mass verification as field sensors.}
{10.1016/j.neuro.2026.103580}

\paperentry{Zou et al. 2026}
{Association of preconception to early childhood residential greenness with child neurodevelopmental outcomes and the mediating role of air pollution: a prospective cohort study.}
{Environ Res}
{5,284 mother-child pairs in the Jiangsu Birth Cohort, with satellite NDVI from preconception to early childhood and Bayley-III screening at age three. Per 0.1 NDVI, preconception and third-trimester greenness lowered risk of the emerging/at-risk cognition category (RR 0.85, 95\% CI 0.74-0.98; 0.82, 0.71-0.95) and gross motor (0.87, 0.77-0.98; 0.77, 0.68-0.88), surviving FDR at 0.10. Single-pollutant mediation assigned 68.0\% (PM$_{10}$) and 63.5\% (NO$_2$) of the preconception-cognition association, and 59.1\% via PM$_{2.5}$ for third-trimester gross motor. Separate single-pollutant mediators summing well past 100\% show the pollutants are proxies for one another and for urbanicity; mediation of that size in a greenness model needs sequential or joint mediation to be credible. Relevance: greenness-PM trade-offs are measurable only with sub-kilometre PM surfaces.}
{10.1016/j.envres.2026.125746}

\paperentry{Falkenstein et al. 2026}
{Investigating effects of day-to-day variations in environmental exposure on the human brain: study protocol for the Day2Day Environment project.}
{BMC Neurosci}
{Protocol and dataset description for a dense longitudinal study: 30 participants, 25 testing sessions each, with wearable devices, GPS-based geospatial tracking and geographic ecological momentary assessment capturing air quality, noise, light, activity, stress, cognition and affect, paired with repeated MRI (T1/T2*, quantitative multi-parameter mapping, hippocampal proton density, DTI). No results. The abstract does not name the air-quality sensor, its calibration or which pollutants are logged, which is the component this watch would need to judge. n=30 is small but the within-person repeated design is where short-term neuroplasticity signals are detectable. Relevance: one of few designs coupling personal low-cost PM tracks to imaging; worth following when the sensor methods paper appears.}
{10.1186/s12868-026-01052-z}

\band{Cardiovascular \& metabolic\hfill 5 records}{Teal}

\paperentry{Aysin et al. 2026}
{Effects of Traffic-Related Diesel Exhaust Exposure and Moderate Exercise on Obesity, Metabolic Dysfunction, and Inflammatory Responses in Rats.}
{Metabolites}
{48 female Wistar rats randomised to six groups: diesel exhaust at a mean 300 $\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$ PM$_{2.5}$ for 2 or 4 h/day over eight weeks, with or without 30 min/day moderate treadmill exercise. Exhaust raised body weight, BMI, Lee index, visceral adiposity index, LDL, triglycerides, glucose, leptin, CRP, IL-1b, IL-6 and TNF-a and lowered HDL and insulin (all p<0.001), most strongly at 4 h/day; exercise attenuated but did not abolish these changes. Only p-values are reported - no effect sizes - and whole exhaust cannot separate particles from NO$_2$ and CO. 300 $\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$ is a high-traffic peak, not a typical mean. Relevance: the exposure-by-activity interaction argues for pairing wearable PM with accelerometry, since ventilation rate rises with exercise.}
{10.3390/metabo16090682}

\paperentry{Wang et al. 2026}
{Long-term exposure to fine particulate matter constituents and incident stroke risk among middle-aged and older adults in China: a cohort study.}
{Front Public Health}
{CHARLS 2013-2018: 8,724 adults, 610 incident strokes over a median 5.0 years, with PM$_{2.5}$ and five constituents from the TAP reanalysis. Per IQR, HRs were 1.55 (95\% CI 1.35-1.79) for PM$_{2.5}$, 1.55 for sulfate, 1.37 for nitrate, 1.42 for ammonium, 1.59 for organic matter and 1.77 (1.54-2.03) for black carbon; quantile g-computation gave BC the largest mixture weight (28.6\%). Susceptibility was higher above 65 and in southern China. Stroke is self-reported physician diagnosis, constituents are strongly collinear model outputs, and IQRs differ by constituent, so per-IQR HRs are not directly comparable. Still the clearest signal this week that carbonaceous particles carry the cerebrovascular risk. Relevance: argues for adding absorption (BC) channels to low-cost nodes, not just scattering mass.}
{10.3389/fpubh.2026.1909901}

\paperentry{Sun et al. 2026}
{When air gets heavy: joint PM(2.5) and ambient humidity exposure, frailty, and stroke.}
{Front Public Health}
{CHARLS (n=17,197) plus a single-centre hospital sample (n=782), with annual PM$_{2.5}$ and relative humidity dichotomised at database medians. High PM$_{2.5}$ gave HR 1.184 (95\% CI 1.083-1.295) for incident stroke in CHARLS and OR 1.846 (1.247-2.733) in the hospital sample; high humidity HR 1.112; joint high-high exposure HR 1.361 (1.171-1.582). Frailty mediated 18.1\% of the PM$_{2.5}$ association. Median dichotomisation discards the exposure-response shape, the hospital arm is cross-sectional, and humidity at annual resolution is largely a climate-zone proxy confounded with region. Relevance: humidity is also the dominant bias term in optical PM sensors - an epidemiologic humidity effect estimated from sensor-derived PM would be partly an artefact, so correction must precede the health model.}
{10.3389/fpubh.2026.1883147}

\paperentry{Yang et al. 2026}
{Mitochondrial DNA methylation as a bridge: Linking indoor air purification to alleviated cardiovascular impairments in young adults.}
{Environ Res}
{A randomised double-blind crossover of true versus sham air purification in 38 healthy young adults. Purification cut indoor PM$_{2.5}$ by 47.45\%. Each 10 $\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$ rise in personal PM$_{2.5}$ at lag 24-48 h was associated with mtATP6 hypomethylation, and mtDNA hypomethylation mediated 14.87\% (p=0.04) of the PM$_{2.5}$ association with D-dimer at lag 0 h, among 16 cardiovascular indicators tested. The mediation analyses run across many CpG sites, lags and outcomes with no stated multiplicity correction, and the headline p=0.04 would not survive one. Short crossover periods in young adults cannot show clinical benefit. Relevance: personal PM$_{2.5}$ was the analysed exposure, so the intervention's value rests on personal-monitor accuracy - the device and its calibration are not reported in the abstract.}
{10.1016/j.envres.2026.125744}

\paperentry{Pellegrino et al. 2026}
{A possible mediating role of SASP in the association between environmental air pollution and incident atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease}
{Sci Total Environ}

\paperentry{Naka et al. 2026}
{Impact of particulate matter 2.5 exposure on severity of pediatric chronic rhinitis: A prospective cohort study.}
{Pediatr Allergy Immunol}

\band{Reproductive \& developmental\hfill 1 record}{Sage}

\paperentry{Zhang et al. 2026}
{Association between ambient PM(2.5) exposure during pregnancy and risk of preterm birth: a retrospective birth cohort study in Chengdu, Southwest China.}
{Front Public Health}
{35,136 singleton births at one Chengdu hospital, 2015-2024; 2,622 (7.46\%) preterm. Mean whole-pregnancy PM$_{2.5}$ was 49.1 $\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$ (satellite) and 51.9 $\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$ (monitors). Per 10 $\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$, whole-pregnancy ORs were 1.089 (95\% CI 1.010-1.174) satellite and 1.091 (0.996-1.195) monitor, with first- and third-trimester signals and landmark ORs 1.06-1.10 for weeks 32-35; associations were confined to late, not earlier, preterm birth. Single-hospital sampling, district-level fixed effects and residence at delivery limit the exposure contrast. The methodological result is the useful one: the two exposure products gave near-identical point estimates, with satellite giving complete coverage and narrower intervals. Relevance: a direct, if coarse, benchmark for when a sparse monitor network is adequate - the question dense sensor networks exist to answer at finer scale.}
{10.3389/fpubh.2026.1936547}

\band{Mechanistic toxicology\hfill 5 records}{Violet}

\paperentry{Lin et al. 2026}
{Air Pollution Triggers Metabolomic Signature of Mitochondrial Dysfunction in Mice and Humans: Implications for Cardiovascular Disease Development.}
{Arterioscler Thromb Vasc Biol}
{A cross-species design: ApoE-/- mice given two weeks of diesel exhaust, and 26 healthy adults who moved from Los Angeles (PM$_{2.5}$ 14.4 $\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$) to Beijing (67.6 $\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$) for ten weeks. Multi-platform metabolomics and lipidomics converged on long-chain dicarboxylic acids and medium-to-long-chain acylcarnitines rising in both species, consistent with impaired fatty-acid oxidation; Seahorse respirometry confirmed hepatic mitochondrial dysfunction in the mice, and in humans the shifts tracked 12-/15-LOX lipid peroxidation products and PAH exposure biomarkers. Tryptophan, pyrimidine and lysine signatures did not replicate across species. n=26 with a city-level exposure contrast that also changes diet, time zone and stress - no personal monitoring is reported. Relevance: a 4.7-fold ambient contrast is exactly where calibrated personal sensors would sharpen the dose axis.}
{10.1161/atvbaha.125.324277}

\paperentry{Monge et al. 2026}
{Heart Function Changes During Environmentally Relevant Combined Exposure to Heat, Ultrafine Carbon Black, and Ozone: Role of NOD-like Receptor X1.}
{Cells}
