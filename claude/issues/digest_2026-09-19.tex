\section*{\sffamily\bfseries\color{Deep}Signal of the day}
\vspace{-1.5mm}{\color{Amber}\rule{\linewidth}{1.1pt}}\vspace{2.5mm}

\begin{keybox}
{\sffamily\bfseries\small\color{Deep}PM$_{2.5}$ oxidative potential saturates rather than
adds --- so a toxicity-weighted particle metric cannot be a linear function of composition.}\\[1.4mm]
\footnotesize
Chen et al.\ (\textit{Environ Sci Technol}) propose a \textbf{quasi-Michaelis--Menten}
mechanism for the dithiothreitol assay, in which DTT, transition metals and oxygen form a
saturating catalytic cycle rather than a set of independent additive contributions. The
derived copper and manganese equations reproduce the nonlinear single-metal
concentration--response \textit{and} the \textbf{antagonism of Cu--Mn mixtures}, which a
linear model cannot represent at all. On \textbf{62 ambient PM$_{2.5}$ samples} collected
over one year in Beijing, a three-term model --- water-soluble Cu, water-soluble Mn, and
WSOC entered linearly --- accounted for \textbf{98.6\% of measured OP$_{\mathrm{DTT}}$}.

The number to distrust is the 98.6\%. It is an in-sample fit on 62 filters from a single
city, with no held-out set and no second site, so those coefficients are a calibration and
not a validation; metal-poor or biomass-dominated aerosol elsewhere may well need a
different parameterisation. The \textit{mechanism}, though, does not depend on the fit
quality, and it is the part that bites for distributed sensing. Oxidative potential is the
leading candidate for a health-weighted alternative to particle mass, and the entire
appeal of inferring it from a dense low-cost network rests on being able to map an optical
or mass channel onto toxicity. This result says that mapping is \textbf{saturating and
non-additive in the metals}: a linear surrogate trained where Cu and Mn are low will
overstate hazard where they are high, and will not reproduce mixture antagonism at any
concentration. Any OP proxy built on a mass-only node needs a nonlinear composition term
it currently has no way to measure.
\end{keybox}

\section*{\sffamily\bfseries\color{Deep}What changed today}
\vspace{-1.5mm}{\color{Amber}\rule{\linewidth}{1.1pt}}\vspace{2.5mm}

\footnotesize
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{The largest venous-thrombosis synthesis to date, and its PM$_{2.5}$ estimate
barely clears the null.} Fang et al.\ (\textit{J Thromb Haemost}) pooled \textbf{23 studies
and more than 1.4 million venous thrombotic events}. Long-term particle exposure was
associated with VTE --- \textbf{RR 1.0804 (95\% CI 1.0015--1.1656)} per
10\,$\mu$g/m$^3$ PM$_{2.5}$ and \textbf{1.0369 (1.0260--1.0479)} per 10\,$\mu$g/m$^3$
PM$_{10}$ --- while \textbf{short-term particle exposure and every gaseous metric were
null}. A lower bound of 1.0015 is a hair's breadth from unity; PM$_{10}$, the cruder
metric, is here the better-supported one. The long-versus-short contrast is the part worth
carrying: the risk sits in the chronic annual mean, not in the daily excursion a dense
network is best at resolving.
\item \textbf{Two mixture analyses on the same day, and the methods review that indicts
both.} Huang et al.\ (\textit{Zhonghua Liu Xing Bing Xue Za Zhi}) report an elastic-net
mixture score across PM$_{2.5}$, PM$_{10}$, NO$_2$ and warm-season O$_3$ against MASLD in
\textbf{23,806 China Kadoorie Biobank} participants; Liu et al.\ attribute frailty to
\textbf{OC, EC, Ca and Cu} components in 1,875 adults. Han et al.\ (\textit{China CDC
Wkly}), reviewing \textbf{97 mixture studies}, find a median sample size of \textbf{729}
against a median \textbf{9 correlated exposures}, with 80--90\% of papers running post-hoc
variable-importance, interaction and nonlinearity tests and most failing to document
preprocessing or justify method choice. The component-ranking claims in this issue are
exactly the genre that review says is under-reported.
\item \textbf{A 24.8-million-facility-day panel finds PM effects where regulation does not
look.} Kim et al.\ (\textit{J Am Geriatr Soc}) link Medicare--Medicaid claims from
\textbf{24,760 nursing facilities} across 47 states with facility fixed effects. Elevated
PM$_{2.5}$ days raised ED visits \textbf{0.020 (95\% CI 0.013--0.027)} and hospitalisations
\textbf{0.017 (0.012--0.022)} per 1,000 resident-days. The absolute magnitudes are tiny
and the exposure is a county-level outdoor value dichotomised at 9\,$\mu$g/m$^3$ applied
to a population that is almost never outdoors --- the HVAC infiltration the authors name
as the mechanism is nowhere measured. The policy asymmetry is the finding:
\textbf{nursing facilities are regulated on indoor temperature and not at all on indoor
particles}.
\item \textbf{Heat and ozone are strongly synergistic, which is a warning about every
main-effect pollutant coefficient.} Li et al.\ (59 counties, warm seasons 2013--18) show
heat-associated circulatory mortality rising from \textbf{9.93\% (2.52--17.89)} at
65\,$\mu$g/m$^3$ O$_3$ to \textbf{27.17\% (20.48--34.23)} at 214\,$\mu$g/m$^3$, with the
O$_3$ coefficient reaching significance only above roughly the 89th temperature
percentile. Ozone is not PM, but the structural lesson transfers directly: a pollutant
coefficient estimated as a main effect is an average over a temperature distribution and
is wrong at both tails.
\item \textbf{Every extractable effect estimate carries a confidence interval --- a first
in this archive.} All \textbf{12} estimates in the forest plot report bounds, against 3 of
7 in the previous issue. That is a property of today's record mix (clinical epidemiology
and meta-analysis, rather than the modelling and instrumentation papers that usually
report point values) and not a change in reporting practice, but it makes this the first
issue in which precision can be read off the figure without caveat.
\item \textbf{Five of twenty records assert no finding, and one of them is the only
instrumentation paper in the window.} The abstract-free same-day deposit problem landed on
\textbf{J Aerosol Sci 10.1016/j.jaerosci.2026.106898}, a sub-10\,nm unipolar aerosol
charger --- the single most relevant record of the day for distributed sizing and the one
that cannot be summarised. Semantic Scholar returns 404 for all five DOIs (not yet
indexed) and the OpenAlex per-work route remains dead, as logged on 18 September. All five
are flagged for pickup rather than paraphrased.
\end{itemize}

\band{Cardiovascular \& metabolic\hfill 3 records}{Teal}

\paperentry{Fang et al. 2026}
{Venous Thrombosis and Air Pollution: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of 1.4 Million Adult Patients.}
{J Thromb Haemost}
{23 studies, >1.4 million venous thrombotic events, searched to 1 July 2025. Long-term particle exposure was associated with VTE - RR 1.0804 (95\% CI 1.0015-1.1656) per 10 $\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$ PM$_{2.5}$ and 1.0369 (1.0260-1.0479) per 10 $\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$ PM$_{10}$ - while short-term particle exposure and every gaseous metric were null. The PM$_{2.5}$ lower bound sits at 1.0015, so the headline association is barely separated from the null and rests on a handful of long-term cohorts; PM$_{10}$, with the far tighter interval, is the more defensible estimate despite being the cruder metric. The projected 98,233 events averted by meeting the 5 $\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$ WHO guideline is a linear no-threshold extrapolation well below the exposure range of the contributing studies and should be read as illustrative. The long-short contrast is the useful signal: chronic annual mean, not the daily excursion a dense sensor network resolves best, is what carries the thrombotic risk here.}
{10.1016/j.jtha.2026.09.022}

\paperentry{Huang et al. 2026}
{Association between air pollution exposure and metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease in adults in China.}
{Zhonghua Liu Xing Bing Xue Za Zhi}
{23,806 adults, China Kadoorie Biobank third resurvey, mean age 65.3 (SD 9.1); 13,428 with MASLD of whom 2,457 had significant fibrosis. Per 1 SD: PM$_{2.5}$ OR 1.27 (95\% CI 1.05-1.53), PM$_{10}$ 1.77 (1.36-2.31), NO$_2$ 1.20 (1.08-1.33), warm-season O$_3$ 1.53 (1.20-1.95). One unit of the mixture score gave 40\% higher MASLD odds (18-65\%) and 42\% higher fibrosis odds (1-100\%) - the fibrosis interval touching unity makes that second estimate uninformative. PM$_{2.5}$ was non-linear (P=0.007), flat at low concentration and steepening above it. The design is cross-sectional, so reverse causation from residential sorting by health status is unaddressed, and the reported PAFs of 25.4-66.4\% are causal-language attached to a single-timepoint contrast and should not be quoted as avoidable burden.}
{10.3760/cma.j.cn112338-20251103-00783}

\paperentry{Li et al. 2026}
{Joint effects of short term exposure to heat and ozone on circulatory disease mortality in Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei region and surrounding areas.}
{Zhonghua Liu Xing Bing Xue Za Zhi}
{59 counties, warm seasons 2013-18, median 4 circulatory deaths per county-day. Independently, each 10 $\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$ of O$_3$ raised circulatory mortality 0.16\% (95\% CI 0.02-0.31\%) and a P75-to-P99 temperature rise raised it 21.43\% (16.32-26.76\%). The interaction is the finding: heat-associated mortality rose from 9.93\% (2.52-17.89\%) at 65 $\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$ O$_3$ to 27.17\% (20.48-34.23\%) at 214 $\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$, and the O$_3$ coefficient only became significant above roughly the 89th temperature percentile, peaking at 0.92\% circulatory and 1.26\% cerebrovascular at P99. Ozone is not PM, but the structure transfers directly: a pollutant coefficient estimated as a main effect is an average over a temperature distribution and will be wrong at both tails. Four deaths per county-day is thin, and the counties are not independent - regional synoptic episodes drive heat and O$_3$ together, so the product term partly encodes shared meteorology rather than biological synergy.}
{10.3760/cma.j.cn112338-20260118-00051}

\band{Respiratory \& allergic\hfill 1 record}{Sky}

\paperentry{Tang et al. 2026}
{Analysis on epidemiology trends of chronic obstructive pulmonary disease in residents aged 40 years and above in Hubei Province, 2014-2024.}
{Zhonghua Liu Xing Bing Xue Za Zhi}
{China COPD surveillance, Hubei, waves 2014-15, 2019-20 and 2024, complex weighted sampling. Prevalence in adults 40+ fell 12.5\% (95\% CI 10.1-14.8) to 10.7\% (7.8-13.6) to 8.1\% (5.8-10.5), but the trend test was null (t=-2.06, P=0.079) - the point estimates drop by a third and the study still cannot reject a flat line, which is what three waves and intervals this wide buy you. Occupational dust exposure, household coal heating and respiratory symptom rates all fell significantly over the same decade while protective-equipment use rose, so the particulate exposure story is consistent even where the outcome trend is not. Adjusted risks: rural 1.38 (1.06-1.81), male 3.45 (1.80-6.61), age 70+ 8.43 (4.51-15.73), current smoker 2.48 (1.75-3.52). The protective BMI gradient (obese OR 0.07) is almost certainly reverse causation from advanced disease, not a benefit of adiposity.}
{10.3760/cma.j.cn112338-20251218-00906}

\band{Reproductive \& developmental\hfill 1 record}{Sage}

\paperentry{Shriyan et al. 2026}
{Ambient and Indoor Air Pollution in Pregnancy and the risk of Low birth weight and Ensuing Effects in Infants (APPLE): A cohort study in Bangalore, South India.}
{Wellcome Open Res}
{516 pregnant women recruited before 18 weeks in Bangalore urban slums, infants followed to age one. This is a protocol, not a result - no effect estimate is reported, and it is a fourth-version revision of a design first posted years ago, so its appearance in this window is a deposit event rather than new evidence. Carried because the measurement plan is the relevant part: paired indoor and ambient PM$_{10}$, PM$_{2.5}$ and CO in solid-fuel slum households is exactly the setting where a fixed regulatory monitor is furthest from personal exposure, and where a low-cost indoor node earns its keep. {10.12688/wellcomeopenres.14830.4}

\band{Mechanistic toxicology\hfill 1 record}{Violet}

\paperentry{Chen et al. 2026}
{A Quasi-Michaelis-Menten Mechanism Explains the Nonlinear Relationship between Transition Metals and PM$_{2.5}$ Oxidative Potential Measured by the Dithiothreitol (DTT) Assay.}
{Environ Sci Technol}
{62 ambient PM$_{2.5}$ samples, Beijing, one year, plus laboratory standards. The authors derive a quasi-Michaelis-Menten scheme for DTT, transition metals and oxygen, and the resulting Cu and Mn equations reproduce both the saturating single-metal response and the antagonism seen in Cu-Mn mixtures. A three-term model on water-soluble Cu, Mn and WSOC - WSOC entered linearly - accounts for 98.6\% of measured OP-DTT. The weakness is that 98.6\% is an in-sample fit on 62 filters from one city with no held-out set and no cross-site test, so the coefficients are calibration, not validation. Still, this is the most directly useful result of the day for sensing: it says OP-DTT is a saturating, not additive, function of composition, so any attempt to infer toxicity-weighted PM from a low-cost mass or optical channel must carry a nonlinear composition term or it will systematically overstate hazard in metal-rich air.}
{10.1021/acs.est.6c09293}

\band{Exposure assessment \& modelling\hfill 4 records}{Clay}

\paperentry{Melssen Pfahl et al. 2026}
{Renoxification via Reduction of Inorganic Nitrate by Photoacids in Aerosols.}
{Environ Sci Technol}
{Chamber study, no human subjects. Catechol and related phenols directly reduce particulate nitrate to HONO and NO under UV-visible light; nebulised gallic acid with nitrate produced 23 times more NOy than nitrate photolysis alone. HONO formation persisted at pH 8, above the pKa of HONO, implicating the phenols as photoacids rather than as bulk acidifiers. The limitation is the usual one for chamber aerosol chemistry - gallic acid at nebuliser concentrations is a proxy for a biomass-burning phenol pool that is far more heterogeneous, and no ambient measurement anchors the 23-fold enhancement. Relevance to distributed sensing is indirect but real: a smoke plume that renoxifies this efficiently generates ozone downwind, so a PM-only network mis-reports the health burden of the same plume hours later and tens of kilometres away.}
{10.1021/acs.est.6c04742}

\paperentry{Han et al. 2026}
{Environmental Mixture-Health Associations: Current Analytical Practice and Reporting Recommendations.}
{China CDC Wkly}
{97 original mixture-exposure studies in four high-impact journals, 2021-25. Median sample size 729 (IQR 396-1,992) and median 9 exposures (IQR 6-15); BKMR in 80.4\%, WQS 36.1\%, quantile g-computation 30.9\%, with 42.3\% running at least two methods. 88.7\% used internal (biomarker) exposures and only 67.4\% of those stated a detection-rate threshold; feature reduction was reported in 25.8\%. Post-hoc variable-importance, interaction and nonlinearity testing appeared in 80-90\% of papers, which with median n=729 and 9 correlated exposures is a multiple-comparison problem the field is not reporting. The review is descriptive and the four-journal frame is not the literature, so prevalence figures are upper bounds on best practice. The checklist matters here because PM component mixtures are the archetypal case and, as the frailty and MASLD records in this same issue show, component-level claims are being made routinely without the preprocessing documentation this paper finds missing.}
{10.46234/ccdcw2026.179}

\paperentry{Ma et al. 2026}
{Association between combined occupational exposure to coal dust and noise and high-frequency hearing loss among workers: a cross-sectional study.}
