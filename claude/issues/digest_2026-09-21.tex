\section*{\sffamily\bfseries\color{Deep}Signal of the day}
\vspace{-1.5mm}{\color{Amber}\rule{\linewidth}{1.1pt}}\vspace{2.5mm}

\begin{keybox}
{\sffamily\bfseries\small\color{Deep}A consumer wearable, three sampling days per
pregnancy, and a 0.43\,SD deficit in femur length: the exposure instrument that made this
study possible is the same one whose calibration error is not reported.}\\[1.4mm]
\footnotesize
Tamire et al.\ (\textit{J Expo Sci Environ Epidemiol}) followed pregnant women in
\textbf{rural Butajira, Ethiopia} from the early second trimester, measuring personal
PM$_{2.5}$ over \textbf{24 hours with an Atmotube Pro} at roughly 20, 30 and 36 weeks and
tracking fetal growth by ultrasound at the same visits. An \textbf{IQR increase of
27.0\,$\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$} was associated with a \textbf{0.43\,SD decrease in femur length}
($\beta$ --0.43, 95\,\% CI --0.51 to --0.35) and a \textbf{0.17\,SD decrease in abdominal
circumference} (--0.17, --0.21 to --0.13), with \textbf{no significant association for head
circumference or biparietal diameter} under either concurrent or lagged exposure.
\textbf{The dissociation is the result, not the magnitude.} Skeletal and soft-tissue growth
respond while cranial growth is spared --- the brain-sparing pattern --- and that pattern is
hard to produce with a nutritional or socioeconomic confounder acting on growth as a whole.
Three limitations bite, and the first is the one that will be ignored. \textbf{The exposure
contrast is enormous by construction}: an IQR of 27\,$\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$ describes a
biomass-cooking population whose range does not overlap the cohorts these coefficients will
inevitably be compared against, so the $\beta$ is not transportable even if it is correct.
\textbf{Three 24-hour samples cannot represent a 16-week gestational window} --- but that
error is non-differential and biases toward the null, so it does not manufacture the femur
result. And the abstract does not report $n$.
\textbf{What makes this the signal of the day for a sensing programme is the instrument.}
This design --- repeated personal exposure across pregnancy in a rural low-income setting
--- was not affordable with gravimetric personal sampling, and it is now being run on
consumer-grade optical wearables. \textbf{The paper reports no sensor calibration, no
co-location against a reference, and no humidity correction.} Biomass smoke is the single
worst case for a nephelometric low-cost sensor: high relative humidity near a cookfire, a
fresh combustion aerosol whose size distribution and refractive index differ sharply from
the factory calibration aerosol. Any multiplicative bias in that instrument maps directly
onto the IQR and therefore onto $\beta$. The finding is plausible and the direction is
almost certainly right; the coefficient carries an uncertainty the paper does not quantify
and could have, with one co-location.\par
\end{keybox}

\section*{\sffamily\bfseries\color{Deep}What changed today}
\vspace{-1.5mm}{\color{Amber}\rule{\linewidth}{1.1pt}}\vspace{2.5mm}

\footnotesize
\begin{itemize}

\item \textbf{Where you work is dirtier than
where you live, and the gap is socioeconomically patterned in the direction nobody assumes.}
A whole-population linkage of the \textbf{2022 Irish Census} to EPA pollutant surfaces
(\textit{Health Place}) assigned every person both a residential and a work/school address.
Mean annual PM$_{2.5}$, PM$_{10}$ and --- most sharply --- NO$_2$ are \textbf{higher at the
workplace than at home}, with O$_3$ running the other way, the expected NO$_x$-titration
signature. The contrast is \textbf{larger for more advantaged workers}, who commute from
cleaner suburbs into denser cores. Residence-only exposure assignment therefore does not
merely add noise; \textbf{it adds noise correlated with socioeconomic position}, which is
the one property an exposure proxy must not have in a study that adjusts for it. For
network design this is the strongest argument in this archive that \textbf{siting should
follow daytime population, not residential population}.

\item \textbf{HEPA filtration reliably cuts
indoor PM$_{2.5}$ and unreliably helps patients.} A synthesis of randomised trials in
asthma and COPD (\textit{Expert Rev Respir Med}) reports consistent, substantial exposure
reduction alongside \textbf{heterogeneous clinical effect} --- symptom, peak-flow and
quality-of-life gains concentrated in highly symptomatic or high-exposure subgroups, and
\textbf{most spirometric endpoints unmoved}. The authors' list of why is the contribution:
short follow-up, almost no adult studies, \textbf{device specifications unreported and
adherence unmeasured}. Those last two are measurement failures, not design failures, and a
logging sensor inside the intervention arm would close both.

\item \textbf{The instrumentation axis stayed
dark for a second day, but the deposits improved.} The \textbf{PubMed sensing query
returned zero again}, OpenAlex returned HTTP 429 and arXiv HTTP 406, so the instrumentation
legs rested entirely on Crossref-by-ISSN for the second consecutive issue. Unlike the 20th,
\textbf{the Copernicus (\textit{ACP}) and ACS (\textit{EST}) deposits carried full
abstracts}, which is why \textbf{metadata-only fell from 5 of 12 (42\,\%) to 5 of 18
(28\,\%)} without any change on the retrieval side. The five that remain are Elsevier and
Taylor \& Francis deposits, and they include the most directly relevant instrumentation
title of the window --- a method for measuring \textbf{penetration efficiency of low-cutoff
cyclones}, i.e.\ the calibration that defines what a size-selective inlet actually admits.
\textbf{The retry list now stands at 28 records.}

\item \textbf{The forest plot had a latent
ratio-scale assumption, and this issue is the first to trip it.} Every effect estimate this
watch has carried since July has been a ratio measure, so three assumptions were
load-bearing and invisible: a logarithmic axis, \textbf{a null line hardcoded at 1.0}, and a
geometric-mean span floor. The two Butajira estimates are \textbf{SD-scale regression
coefficients and both are negative}, and \texttt{(lo\,$\times$\,hi)$^{0.5}$} duly returned a
\textbf{complex number}, which killed the build in a comparison rather than at the axis.
Estimates are now \textbf{partitioned by scale}, each panel drawing its own axis, null and
tick ladder; a ratio metric whose lower bound is $\leq 0$ is routed to the difference panel
defensively. Worth recording that the colour map has had a \texttt{beta} entry since the
palette was written --- \textbf{the colour was anticipated, the axis was not.}

\item \textbf{Two upwind results argue the same
thing from opposite ends of India.} At \textbf{Sonipat}, the upwind receptor for Delhi-NCR
(\textit{ACP}), composition-based PM$_{2.5}$ exceeded \textbf{300\,$\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$} in two
post-monsoon haze episodes with organic aerosol at 65\,\% of non-refractory mass and
\textbf{57--60\,\% of it already oxygenated} --- the haze is aged secondary mass before it
reaches the city. In \textbf{Delhi} itself, replacing globally prescribed refractive indices
with local ones inside an operational forecast (\textit{ACP}, AIRWISE) cut surface
shortwave by up to \textbf{80\,W\,m$^{-2}$}, lowered boundary-layer height by
\textbf{200\,m} and \textbf{raised} PM$_{2.5}$. The feedback runs the wrong way for
forecasting: \textbf{getting the optics right makes the episode worse}, so a network
assimilating into a model with generic refractive indices inherits a low bias during exactly
the episodes that matter.

\item \textbf{A corpus defect was fixed ahead of
the monthly it would have corrupted.} \texttt{state/corpus/2026-08-03.json} carried a
\textbf{six-element} \texttt{LIFECOURSE} where the schema has five --- a trailing ``not
life-course specific'' overflow bucket holding 19 records. Because
\texttt{\_lifecourse\_range} sizes its accumulator from \textbf{the first issue it meets in
the range}, that record could silently widen every pooled life-course figure whose window
opened on it. The overflow bucket has been dropped and all 57 archived issues now conform.
One gap remains and is not a corruption: \texttt{2026-07-27} carries no
\texttt{LIFECOURSE} at all and is skipped cleanly by the rollup.

\end{itemize}

\section*{\sffamily\bfseries\color{Deep}Paper-level digest}
\vspace{-1.5mm}{\color{Amber}\rule{\linewidth}{1.1pt}}\vspace{1mm}

{\fontsize{7.4}{9}\selectfont\color{Slate}Ordered by cluster. Each entry gives the
methodological core and the finding that matters, not an abstract paraphrase. Records
marked \textit{metadata only} carry no recoverable abstract and assert no finding.\par}

\band{Neuro / mental health\hfill 1 record}{Deep}

\paperentry{Hong Kong momentary stress 2026}
{An Explainable Multi-Modal Deep Learning Framework for Investigating the Influences of Dynamic Environmental Exposure on Momentary Stress.}
{Environ Sci Technol}
{4,342 momentary stress reports against 125,440 minute-level environmental exposure records; the model reaches 78.26\% accuracy and 0.828 AUC-ROC. Two results are worth extracting from the architecture. First, exposure in the preceding 25 minutes dominates prediction, which is a direct empirical argument for minute-resolution mobile monitoring over daily or residential-annual assignment for acute endpoints. Second, the explainability analysis recovers thresholds rather than slopes - 25 $\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$ for PM$_{2.5}$, 55 dBA noise, 32 C, 40-70\% humidity, 25\% greenery visibility - consistent with nonlinearity that a linear exposure-response term would average away. The weaknesses are structural and serious: stress is self-reported into the same phone that carries the sensors, so reporting and exposure share a device and a context; the design is correlational with no within-person randomisation of exposure; the thresholds are read off a gradient-based attribution of a black-box model, not estimated with uncertainty, so they should be treated as hypotheses; and PM$_{2.5}$ co-varies with noise and temperature in ways a deep model can blend invisibly. Still the clearest current demonstration of what wearable-grade PM sensing buys over address-based assignment.}
{10.1021/acs.est.6c09466}

\band{Respiratory \& allergic\hfill 1 record}{Sky}

\paperentry{HEPA RCT synthesis 2026}
{HEPA air filtration for asthma and COPD: lessons from randomized controlled trials.}
{Expert Rev Respir Med}
{The consistent finding across trials is the one that should worry anyone who equates exposure reduction with clinical benefit: portable HEPA reliably and substantially lowers indoor PM$_{2.5}$, but clinical effects are heterogeneous. Several asthma trials improved symptoms, peak flow or quality of life - concentrated in highly symptomatic or higher-exposure subgroups - while most spirometric endpoints moved little or not at all. COPD evidence is thin and only suggestive. The authors attribute the gap to trial design rather than to absent efficacy, and list the specific failures: short follow-up, almost no adult studies, device specifications unreported, adherence unmeasured, no economic evaluation, and near-total absence of low-resource settings. That list is itself the contribution. Being a narrative rather than systematic synthesis with no pooled estimate and no formal risk-of-bias assessment, it cannot bound the effect, and the subgroup pattern it highlights is exactly the pattern selective reporting produces. For a sensing programme the actionable point is that unmeasured adherence and unreported device spec are measurement problems a logging sensor inside the intervention arm would largely solve.}
{10.1080/17476348.2026.2735502}

\band{Reproductive \& developmental\hfill 1 record}{Sage}

\paperentry{Tamire et al. 2026}
{Prenatal fine particulate matter (PM$_{2.5}$) exposure differentially impairs fetal skeletal and soft-tissue growth: a longitudinal study in rural Butajira, Ethiopia.}
{J Expo Sci Environ Epidemiol}
{Pregnant women recruited at or before 24 weeks, followed over three visits, March-June 2025. An IQR rise of 27.0 $\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$ in personal PM$_{2.5}$ was associated with a 0.43 SD fall in femur length (beta -0.43, 95\% CI -0.51 to -0.35) and a 0.17 SD fall in abdominal circumference (-0.17, -0.21 to -0.13); head circumference and biparietal diameter showed no significant association under either concurrent or lagged exposure. The dissociation is the finding - skeletal and soft-tissue growth respond while cranial growth is spared, which is the brain-sparing pattern and argues against a purely nutritional confounder. Three weaknesses bite. The exposure contrast is enormous by design (an IQR of 27 $\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$ implies a biomass-cooking population far outside the range of the cohorts these coefficients will be compared against), the sample is small and the paper does not report n in the abstract, and three 24-h samples cannot represent a 16-week gestational window - classical measurement error that, being non-differential, biases toward null and so does not explain the femur result. Directly relevant to distributed sensing: the exposure instrument is a consumer-grade optical wearable, and effect sizes this large resting on three wearable-days per pregnancy is exactly the design low-cost sensing makes newly affordable in LMIC settings - and exactly where sensor humidity and biomass-smoke calibration error, unreported here, would propagate straight into beta.}
{10.1038/s41370-026-00966-4}

\band{Mechanistic toxicology\hfill 1 record}{Violet}

\paperentry{CSE HASMC isoforms 2026}
{A comprehensive full-length transcriptome landscape of cigarette smoke-exposed HASMCs reveals extensive remodeling of mRNA isoforms and regulatory networks.}
{PLoS One}
{Oxford Nanopore long-read sequencing of human aortic smooth muscle cells at 0.6\% cigarette smoke extract, the midpoint of a biphasic viability curve (0.2\% mildly proliferative, 0.4-0.8\% progressively cytotoxic). 11,222 novel transcripts; 1,866 transcripts up in ferroptosis and cell division pathways, 493 down in oxidative phosphorylation and glutathione metabolism, 1,753 alternative polyadenylation events remodelling 3'UTRs, and 269 alternative splicing events hitting cell-cycle regulators and transcription factors including CREM, FOSL1 and HBP1. The claim is that high-dose cytotoxicity is executed post-transcriptionally rather than through differential gene expression alone, and the isoform resolution is what makes that visible - a short-read study would have reported the pathway shifts and missed the 3'UTR remodelling entirely. Admitted at tier C with an explicit caveat: cigarette smoke extract is not ambient PM, the cell line is not a vessel, a single 0.6\% dose is not a dose-response for the mechanism claimed, and no particle mass or size metric is reported, so nothing here is transferable to an ambient exposure-response coefficient. Carried for the ferroptosis and glutathione-depletion axis, which recurs in ambient PM vascular toxicology and is worth tracking.}
{10.1371/journal.pone.0354604}

\band{Exposure assessment \& modelling\hfill 5 records}{Clay}

\paperentry{Ireland Census linkage 2026}
{Ambient air pollution at home, work and school: Evidence from Ireland.}
{Health Place}
{Whole-population census microdata linked to Irish EPA pollutant surfaces at two addresses per person. Mean annual PM$_{2.5}$, PM$_{10}$ and - most sharply - NO$_2$ are higher at the work, school or college address than at the residence, while O$_3$ runs the other way, the expected NOx-titration signature of the same urban gradient. The socioeconomic result inverts the usual assumption: among workers the residential-to-workplace contrast is *larger* for more advantaged groups, because advantaged workers commute into dense urban cores from cleaner suburbs. Residence-only assignment therefore does not simply add noise, it adds noise correlated with socioeconomic position, which is the one thing an exposure proxy must not do in a study adjusting for it. Limits: both endpoints are modelled ambient surfaces at an address, so nothing here captures infiltration, indoor sources or time-activity weighting, and the paper measures the size of a bias without estimating its effect on any health coefficient. For network design this is the strongest argument yet that siting density should follow daytime population rather than residential population.}
{10.1016/j.healthplace.2026.103732}

\paperentry{Sonipat ACSM campaign 2026}
{Chemical characterization and source apportionment of carbonaceous aerosols during post-monsoon biomass burning and Diwali at an upwind site of Delhi.}
{Atmos Chem Phys}
{25 October to 15 November 2023, spanning two severe haze episodes and Diwali. Composition-based PM$_{2.5}$ exceeded 300 $\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$ in both episodes; organic aerosol was 65\% of non-refractory PM$_{2.5}$ with a daily mean near 140 $\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$, and oxygenated OA was 57-60\% of total OA, reaching 42.8 $\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$ of more-oxidised OOA at peak haze - so the haze is substantially aged secondary mass, not fresh plume. Biomass- or solid-fuel eBC was \textasciitilde{}78\% of campaign-integrated eBC. The authors are honest that this split is sensitive to the assumed absorption Angstrom exponent pair, which is the paper's real limitation: the headline 78\% is a function of a prior, and the sensitivity range is not propagated into the PMF factors. Single site, single season, three weeks. The result that matters upstream of a sensor network is that the mass is already loaded before the air reaches Delhi, so an urban-only network measures an urban increment on a large regional background and will misattribute regional transport to local sources unless it has an upwind anchor.}
{10.5194/acp-26-13227-2026}

\paperentry{W. India BC-dust 2026}
{Seasonal Variability and Interactions of Black Carbon and Mineral Dust over Semi-Arid Western India: Satellite and Ground-Based Observations.}
{Environ Sci Atmos}
{Only the opening of the abstract was deposited, so the design is legible but the numbers are not: the study characterises seasonal co-variation of anthropogenic black carbon and natural mineral dust in a semi-arid region where both are large, using satellite retrievals alongside ground-based observations. No effect sizes, retrieval-validation statistics or station counts are recoverable from the deposit, and nothing is claimed here beyond scope. Flagged for retry rather than summarised further. The reason to carry it at all is the mixing problem it addresses: BC and dust are the two components that most degrade low-cost optical PM sensing in arid regions, BC because it absorbs rather than scatters and dust because its coarse mode falls outside a nephelometric sensor's effective cut, and a seasonal climatology of their interaction is the calibration prior a regional network would need.}
{10.1039/d6ea00109b}

{Atmos Pollut Res}

{Atmos Res}

\paperentry{AIRWISE Delhi 2026}
{Improving aerosol-radiation interactions in the operational forecasting system - AIRWISE.}
{Atmos Chem Phys}
{Replacing globally prescribed refractive indices with Delhi-specific values inside the operational AIRWISE system cut surface downwelling shortwave by up to \textasciitilde{}80 W/m2 instantaneously (\textasciitilde{}43 W/m2 diurnally) during a severe post-monsoon episode, and monthly mean SWDOWN by 25-37 W/m2, a quarter to a third of total clear-sky aerosol-radiation interaction. The meteorological response is coherent and the right sign: surface temperature -0.2 C, near-surface wind -0.4 m/s, boundary layer height -200 m, daytime humidity +3-4\%, and consequently higher PM$_{2.5}$ - the shallower, calmer boundary layer traps the aerosol that caused the dimming. Validated against WiFEX surface radiation at Delhi airport with 62\% (December) and 35\% (January) bias reduction. Limits: one city, one season, refractive indices tuned where they are evaluated, and radiation bias reduction does not demonstrate PM$_{2.5}$ forecast skill improvement, which is the operational claim a reader will assume. Relevant because the feedback runs the wrong way for forecasting: getting optics right makes the pollution episode worse, so a network assimilating into a model with generic refractive indices inherits a systematic low bias during exactly the episodes that matter.}
