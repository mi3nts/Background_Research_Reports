\section*{\sffamily\bfseries\color{Deep}Signal of the day}
\vspace{-1.5mm}{\color{Amber}\rule{\linewidth}{1.1pt}}\vspace{2.5mm}

\begin{keybox}
{\sffamily\bfseries\small\color{Deep}Eight million people, intervals three decimal places
wide --- and the number that decides what the study means is the one nobody
measured.}\\[1.4mm]
\footnotesize
Lee et al.\ (\textit{Allergy}) linked 8{,}252{,}498 Korean National Health Insurance
enrollees to 1\,km long-term PM estimates and got incident-asthma hazard ratios of
\textbf{1.33 (95\% CI 1.32--1.34)} per 10\,$\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$ of PM$_{2.5}$ and
\textbf{1.38 (1.37--1.38)} for PM$_{10}$. Stratifying on age, Medical Aid status and
urbanicity produced a clean monotone gradient from 1.16 (1.14--1.18) in younger rural
adults without Medical Aid to \textbf{1.80 (1.66--1.94)} in older urban Medical Aid
recipients, interaction $p<0.001$. At this sample size the confidence intervals stop being
the interesting uncertainty. Incident asthma here is a diagnosis code, and the same
gradient that defines the exposure strata --- urban, poor, old --- is the gradient of
healthcare contact, so a portion of the 1.16-to-1.80 spread is ascertainment rather than
susceptibility, and the paper offers no lever to separate them. The second problem is
ours. The modifier being tested is \emph{urbanicity}, a within-city contrast, and the
exposure surface resolving it is 1\,km. Intra-urban PM$_{2.5}$ gradients run at hundreds of
metres near roads; a 1\,km surface averages the Medical Aid recipient beside the arterial
together with the resident four blocks back. That kind of misclassification does not
manufacture a gradient, it compresses one --- so 1.80 is probably a floor, and the study's
own design is an argument for finer exposure surfaces.
\end{keybox}

\section*{\sffamily\bfseries\color{Deep}What changed today}
\vspace{-1.5mm}{\color{Amber}\rule{\linewidth}{1.1pt}}\vspace{2.5mm}

\footnotesize
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{A controlled human exposure asks whether blood can stand in for a
bronchoscope.} Thirty-two participants, double-blind crossover, two hours of diesel exhaust
against filtered air, paired-end RNA-seq on both blood and bronchial brushings
(\textit{Environ Res}). Airway differential expression at FDR $<$ 10\% picks up GPX2 and
NQO1 --- the NRF2 antioxidant arm --- and hallmark mapping gives interferon-$\alpha$,
interferon-$\gamma$, inflammatory and ROS activity in \emph{both} compartments. The
concordance between them was not visible when either compartment was analysed alone. The
authors call it exploratory and it is: one dose, one duration, no exposure--response. But
the question is the right one for panel studies of traffic particles, where bronchoscopy
is the reason sample sizes stay at 32.

\item \textbf{Personal monitoring plus multi-omics narrows PM$_{2.5}$ to four
constituents.} A 313-measurement longitudinal panel in older adults
(\textit{Environ Sci Technol}) screened constituents with a mixed model and three
machine-learning methods and landed on chloride, nickel, barium and selenium. Per IQR,
chloride went with a \textbf{3.48\% (95\% CI 1.50--5.46)} rise in diastolic blood pressure
and nickel with a \textbf{0.09\% (0.05--0.12)} fall in FEV$_1$/FVC, with 212 CpG sites
mediating both cardiovascular and pulmonary endpoints. The constituents are collinear, so
the four winners are correlates and not isolated causes --- but the exposure is
\emph{personal}, which removes the spatial misalignment that usually decides
constituent studies before the statistics start.

\item \textbf{A source that is trivial by mass and dominant by tracer.} Wood-fired pizza
ovens in Milan (\textit{preprint}) contribute 0.085--3.34\,$\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$ of primary
PM$_{10}$ and almost no NO$_x$ --- but \textbf{19.7--34.3\% of levoglucosan} and
\textbf{35.0--37.5\% of benzo(a)pyrene}. Any apportionment that reads levoglucosan as
residential wood burning will mis-assign a third of it in a restaurant-dense city centre.
The ranges are model spreads, not measured intervals, and it is one city.

\item \textbf{The cooking-aerosol record reports its negative result rather than burying
it.} FIGAERO-CIMS on oxidised corn-oil emissions (\textit{Atmos Chem Phys}) gives a
molar-mass-dependent partitioning scheme --- gas-favoured below 120\,g\,mol$^{-1}$,
particle-favoured above 220 --- and then states that a substantial gap remains between
FIGAERO-derived effective volatility and equilibrium thermodynamics, attributing it to
combined measurement and model uncertainty rather than resolving it. Non-equilibrium
partitioning in a source that is a rising share of urban organic aerosol is a direct
problem for any mass-based instrument calibrated against an ambient mix.

\item \textbf{The connector's apparent additions were mostly an artefact, and the DOI gate
caught it.} Of the three connector PMIDs the harvester's fresh set lacked, only one was
genuinely new; the other two had shipped on 8 and 9 September via Crossref carrying
\emph{no PMID}, so a PMID-keyed check missed them. \texttt{check\_dois.py} failed the build
with two \textsc{duplicate-doi} errors. Connector records must be matched on DOI.

\end{itemize}

\band{Neuro / mental health\hfill 1 record}{Deep}

\paperentry{Older-adult cognition review 2026}
{Air pollution and cognitive outcomes in older adults: a systematic review}
{Rev Environ Health}
{PubMed searched to February 2025 for observational studies from 2000 onward on ambient air pollution and cognition in adults 50+; 36 studies included. PM$_{2.5}$ is the most-studied pollutant, and most studies report worse global or domain-specific cognition, faster decline, or higher impairment and dementia risk with higher PM$_{2.5}$. Other pollutants trend adverse; ozone is the least consistent. Heterogeneity is attributed to exposure-assessment method, cognitive instrument, design and confounding control. Two limitations bound what this can support: a single-database search with no quantitative pooling and no formal risk-of-bias tool, so the conclusion is a vote count, not a weighted synthesis, and publication bias in a literature this size is unaddressed. The exposure-assessment heterogeneity it names is the actionable part}
{10.1515/reveh-2026-0022}

\band{Cardiovascular \& metabolic\hfill 1 record}{Teal}

\paperentry{Constituent multiomics cardiopulmonary 2026}
{PM$_{2.5}$ Constituents and Cardiopulmonary Dysfunctions in Older Adults: A Multiomics Approach Unveiling Molecular Pathways}
{Environ Sci Technol}
{Longitudinal panel, 313 repeated measurements in older adults, personal rather than ambient PM$_{2.5}$ with constituent speciation, screened by a linear mixed model plus three machine-learning methods. Chloride, nickel, barium and selenium emerged as the key constituents. Per IQR, chloride was associated with a 3.48\% (95\% CI 1.50-5.46) rise in diastolic blood pressure and nickel with a 0.09\% (0.05-0.12) fall in FEV1/FVC. Mediation implicated 212 CpG sites acting on both cardiovascular and pulmonary endpoints and 7 genes spanning more than two constituent-function pairs, with 22 CpGs linked by eQTM to 14 coding genes including CERS6 and LRCH3. Limitations: 313 measurements is a small panel for a screen over this many constituents and CpGs, the constituent set is collinear so the four winners are correlates rather than isolated causes, and the mediation is cross-sectional within visit. Personal monitoring is the strength - it removes the spatial misalignment that defeats constituent studies built on central-site speciation}
{10.1021/acs.est.6c04206}

\band{Respiratory \& allergic\hfill 1 record}{Sky}

\paperentry{Korea 8.25M asthma cohort 2026}
{Cumulative Vulnerability to Particulate Matter and Incident Asthma: Joint Effects of Urbanicity, Poverty, and Age in a Nationwide Cohort}
{Allergy}
{8,252,498 adults in the Korean National Health Insurance Service database linked to 1-km long-term PM estimates, Cox models for incident asthma. Per 10 $\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$, PM$_{2.5}$ gave HR 1.33 (95\% CI 1.32-1.34) and PM$_{10}$ HR 1.38 (1.37-1.38). Stratification by age, Medical Aid status and urbanicity produced a monotone gradient from HR 1.16 (1.14-1.18) in younger rural adults without Medical Aid to 1.80 (1.66-1.94) in older urban Medical Aid recipients, p for interaction < 0.001. Limitation: incident asthma in claims data is diagnosis-coded, and urban Medical Aid recipients have higher healthcare contact, so part of the 1.16-to-1.80 gradient is plausibly ascertainment rather than susceptibility - the confidence intervals are narrow enough to make that distinction matter. Exposure contrast at 1 km is coarse relative to the urbanicity modifier being tested, which is the resolution argument for dense low-cost networks}
{10.1111/all.70502}

\paperentry{Korean birth-cohort growth mixture 2026}
{Prenatal bisphenols and ambient air pollutants are differentially associated with early childhood length and weight from birth to 36 months: a multi-domain mixture analysis in a Korean birth cohort}
{Environ Pollut}
{\textsc{metadata only.} No abstract was available on the entry date from Crossref per-DOI, Europe PMC, PubMed efetch or Semantic Scholar. \band{Mechanistic toxicology\hfill 1 record}{Violet}

\paperentry{Vancouver DE blood-lung concordance 2026}
{Concordant transcriptomics analysis of the lungs and blood after exposure to diesel exhaust: a randomized, controlled human exposure study}
{Environ Res}
{Double-blind crossover controlled human exposure, 32 participants, two hours of diesel exhaust and of filtered air on separate occasions, with paired-end RNA-seq on blood and bronchial brushings. Airway DEGs at FDR < 10\% include upregulated GPX2 and NQO1, i.e. the NRF2 antioxidant arm. Hallmark pathway mapping gives IFN-alpha, IFN-gamma, inflammatory response and ROS activity in both compartments, and a concordant analysis finds correlated blood-airway expression that neither compartment showed alone. The authors are explicit that this is exploratory. n = 32 at a single two-hour dose, no exposure-response, and the concordance is correlational - but it is the right design question: whether a blood transcriptomic readout can stand in for bronchoscopy in panel studies of traffic-related particles}
{10.1016/j.envres.2026.125625}

\band{Exposure assessment \& modelling\hfill 1 record}{Clay}

\paperentry{Milan pizza-oven dispersion 2026}
{Assessment of Air Quality Impacts from Wood-Fired Pizza Ovens: Comparison with Major Urban Emission Sources}
{Preprints.org}
{PREPRINT. Milan. Measured wood-fired-oven emission factors feed a dispersion model, with road traffic and domestic heating simulated on the same framework and all three compared against ARPA Lombardia monitors. Oven contributions concentrate in the city centre: 0.085-3.34 $\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$ of primary PM$_{10}$, only 0.0044-1.75 $\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$ of NOx, but 19.70-34.28\% of levoglucosan and 34.95-37.47\% of benzo(a)pyrene. That split is the finding - a source that is minor by mass and major by tracer, which is exactly the pattern that breaks levoglucosan-based residential-wood-burning apportionment in a restaurant-dense centre. Limitations: preprint, single city, emission factors from a small appliance sample, and the ranges are model spreads rather than measured confidence intervals}
{10.20944/preprints202609.0792.v1}

\band{Sensing, forecasting \& instrumentation\hfill 2 records}{Amber}

\paperentry{Cooking aerosol FIGAERO partitioning 2026}
{Unravelling gas-particle partitioning dynamics in cooking aerosol oxidation through FIGAERO-CIMS analysis}
{Atmos Chem Phys}
{Heated corn oil emissions aged in a Go:PAM oxidation flow reactor, with FIGAERO-I-CIMS and a 2-D VBS parameterisation tracking composition, volatility and partitioning. Kinetic calculations give molar-mass-dependent partitioning with the surface process rate-limiting. Primary cooking aerosol sits biased toward the gas phase relative to equilibrium; in secondary aerosol the crossover is near 120-220 g\,mol$^{-1}$, below which compounds favour gas and above which they favour particle. The honest result is the negative one: a substantial unresolved gap remains between FIGAERO-derived effective volatility and equilibrium thermodynamic estimates, which the authors attribute to combined measurement and model uncertainty rather than resolving. Cooking is a rising share of urban OA as combustion sources are controlled, and non-equilibrium partitioning is a direct problem for any mass-based sensor calibrated on ambient mix}
{10.5194/acp-26-12793-2026}

\paperentry{FY-4B/AGRI AOD evaluation 2026}
{Evaluation of the FY-4B/AGRI Land Aerosol Optical Depth Product and Comparison with Geostationary Satellite Products over Asia}
{Atmos Environ}
{\textsc{metadata only.} No abstract was available on the entry date from any of the four sources queried - the same same-day Elsevier deposit lag logged repeatedly since 2026-09-08. Title and journal establish a validation of the FY-4B/AGRI land AOD product against other geostationary retrievals over Asia. No validation statistics, no reference network and no spatial or temporal coverage are available, so nothing is asserted here and the record is tier C. Worth re-reading when the abstract deposits: geostationary AOD is the only route to sub-daily satellite-surface PM fusion, and product-versus-product agreement is the term that dominates the error budget of any such fusion}
{10.1016/j.atmosenv.2026.122354}

{COVID-19 Pandemic as an Environmental Health Indicator: Lessons Learned from Air Pollution Changes and Climate Implications}
{Preprints.org}

Logged for completeness of the burden-and-policy subtopic; carries no estimate this archive should propagate}
{10.20944/preprints202609.0712.v1}
