\section*{\sffamily\bfseries\color{Deep}Signal of the day}
\vspace{-1.5mm}{\color{Amber}\rule{\linewidth}{1.1pt}}\vspace{2.5mm}

\begin{keybox}
{\sffamily\bfseries\small\color{Deep}Two papers, two countries, one result: the two
quantities a PM instrument actually measures --- scattered light and gravimetric mass ---
both decouple from the fraction that does the damage, and both fail in the direction that
under-reports it.}\\[1.4mm]
\footnotesize
Zeng et al.\ (\textit{J.\ Hazard.\ Mater.}) put volume-normalised oxidative potential and
light extinction on a common quadrant plane at two urban sites in southern Taiwan and
isolate an \textbf{``invisible killer'' regime: high redox activity, low optical impact}.
The mediator is thermodynamic, not chemical --- \textbf{high aerosol liquid water content}
drives hygroscopic growth into optics-dominant states, while \textbf{low ALWC} corresponds
to the highest OP-to-extinction ratios; secondary nitrate loads the extinction side
specifically. Independently, Zhao C et al.\ (\textit{Environ.\ Pollut.}) tracked 16 priority
particle-bound PAHs across a real air-quality improvement in Kunming. Mass fell
\textbf{32.7\%} and BaP-equivalent burden \textbf{31.4\%}, but the
\textbf{particle-normalised toxicity, BaP$_{\mathrm{eq}}$/PM$_{2.5}$, did not move
($-$7.6\%, $p$=0.274)}; 4--6-ring species stayed above \textbf{81\%} of $\Sigma$PAH, and
\textbf{40.3\%} of simulated adult lifetime cancer risks still exceeded $10^{-6}$.
Put together: a nephelometric sensor reports the wrong thing when the air is dry, a
gravimetric filter reports the wrong thing when the air is cleaner, and the error is the
same sign in both cases. A network built to certify compliance against a mass threshold
will read a genuine improvement while the per-microgram hazard is unchanged. Neither paper
is a sensor study, and that is the point --- this is the calibration target problem stated
from the chemistry side.
\end{keybox}

ragged: \textit{Measurement campaign} (7) leads, then \textit{Review / synthesis} and

\item \textbf{The no-endpoint share fell hard, and that is a composition change rather than
a trend.} \textbf{12 of 27 (44\%)} carry no human health endpoint, against 61\%, 60\% and

cohort epidemiology held, so the ratio moved because the \textit{ES\&T} by-ISSN deposit
that inflated 3 September did not repeat.

\item \textbf{Two UK Biobank cardiovascular papers, and their intervals are not on the same
evidential footing.} Chen N et al.\ report per-IQR incident-VTE hazard ratios of
\textbf{1.052 (1.005--1.101)} for elemental carbon and \textbf{1.090 (1.010--1.176)} for
ammonium on 9{,}988 events --- both lower bounds sit within 0.01 of the null, which is the
honest width for a time-invariant residential exposure. The same paper's heatwave hazard
ratios run \textbf{1.149 (1.137--1.161)} to \textbf{1.407 (1.396--1.417)}: intervals
roughly \textbf{three times narrower} on the same events, because a daily time-varying
exposure carries far more information than an annual surface. Both belong in the paper;
they should not be read off one forest panel as comparable quantities, and the mediation
claim (elemental carbon and ammonium partly mediating the greenness effect) inherits the
weaker exposure.

\item \textbf{Two prenatal-PM cohorts with exactly inverted strengths.} Mbishi et al.\ have
\textbf{119{,}110} Flemish children and municipality-scale kriged PM, and report a
head-circumference deficit at 12 months of \textbf{$-$0.065 $z$ per IQR PM$_{2.5}$} --- about
one-fifteenth of a standard deviation, unambiguous at that $n$ and, as the authors
themselves write, small. Wei Q et al.\ have \textbf{2{,}680} pairs and weekly
component-resolved exposure, and can name windows the first study cannot see: femur length
most sensitive to black carbon at \textbf{gestational weeks 12--15}, head circumference to
nitrate at \textbf{weeks 5--12 and 22--24}. Power and exposure resolution are being traded
against each other, and no record today has both.

\item \textbf{The widest source spread on the forest panel since August.} 11 estimates from
\textbf{five} sources, tying 27 August; only the 26--27 July back-scan and 6 August have had
more. One source still supplies 6 of the 11, so the panel is more diverse than it is
balanced.

\item \textbf{Geography narrowed.} China 8 of 27 (30\%), \textit{Global / multi-region} 7
(of which four are genuine multi-region reviews, one is a laboratory record and two are
metadata-only records with no stated setting), East Asia ex-China 4. \textbf{Sub-Saharan
Africa, South Asia, the Middle East and Oceania are all absent}, three days after
Sub-Saharan Africa tied its watch record at three. Latin America returns with a single
Merida aerosol-chemistry record.

\item \textbf{One new interventional trial registration, and it is a registry-hygiene
warning.} NCT07805824 is a cluster RCT of a workplace heat and air-pollution mitigation
package in \textbf{20 restaurants / 200 oven workers} in Najaf, Iraq, with
\textbf{personal PM$_{2.5}$ by gravimetric and real-time monitoring} as its sole secondary
endpoint --- directly relevant to occupational personal sensing. But it is filed as
\textbf{Phase 4} with no drug, its start and primary-completion dates are both 2026-01-01
while its status is already \textsc{completed}, and the public record still carries the
submitter's own pre-submission checklist asking them to ``confirm the exact first-participant
enrollment date''. Treat the endpoint design as usable and the timeline fields as not.

\item \textbf{Nil legs, one of them now verified rather than inspected.} Consensus is nil
for the \textbf{sixth} consecutive run, but this time by DOI-level date check: its three
low-cost-sensor calibration hits are all real and all out of window (Crossref

recalibration-cadence paper --- \emph{is already in this corpus} from an earlier issue. The
leg finds the right literature; only the date filter is missing.
\textbf{OpenAlex} HTTP 429, unchanged since 28 July. \textbf{arXiv} answered cleanly for the
second consecutive run, newest submission still \textbf{2026-08-20}, contribution again zero.
\end{itemize}

\band{Sensing, forecasting \& instrumentation\hfill 6 records}{Amber}

\paperentry{Zeng et al.\ 2026d}
{Oxidative potential and light extinction decouple, and aerosol water is the switch}
{J Hazard Mater}
{Paired oxidative-potential and optical measurements at two urban sites in southern
Taiwan, arranged into a quadrant framework on \emph{volume-normalised} OP against light
extinction. Under coupling, mass-normalised OP and mass extinction efficiency share their
drivers, principally \textbf{water-soluble organic carbon}. Under decoupling, \textbf{high
aerosol liquid water content} pushes the aerosol into an optics-dominant state through
hygroscopic growth, while \textbf{low ALWC} yields the highest OP-to-extinction ratios ---
the ``invisible killer'' quadrant. Secondary nitrate loads extinction specifically; aerosol
pH is a weaker secondary term. The direct consequence for distributed sensing: every
nephelometric low-cost sensor estimates mass from scattering, so its error against the
redox-active fraction is not random but conditioned on humidity, in the same direction as
its already-known RH bias. Weakness: OP is one assay on one aerosol population at two
sites, sampling duration is not stated in the abstract, and no causal chain from OP to a
clinical endpoint is tested here.}
{10.1016/j.jhazmat.2026.143459}

\paperentry{Ichikawa et al.\ 2026}
{Drone sampling agrees with a flux tower to within 30\%, and that is the useful number}
{Atmos Chem Phys}
{Multi-year warm-season BVOC measurements at multiple heights on a 30\,m flux tower in a
\textit{Quercus serrata}-dominated suburban Tokyo forest, with supplemental drone sampling
and comparison against MEGAN. Isoprene is \textbf{over 90\% of measured BVOC composition}
at peak summer, monoterpenes stay low with weak vertical gradients, and daily average
isoprene emission fluxes span \textbf{$-$0.05 to 15.30\,mg\,m$^{-2}$\,h$^{-1}$}. The
methodological result is the one worth carrying: \textbf{drone-measured horizontal
variability is 10--30\% within 30\,m of the tower}, and \textbf{tower- and drone-derived
flux estimates differ by about 30\%}. That is a direct, published estimate of the
representativeness error a mobile aerial platform introduces relative to a fixed mast ---
the same quantity a mobile PM platform needs and rarely reports. Limits: BVOCs are
SOA precursors, not particles; intermittent campaigns over multiple years confound
season with instrument configuration.}
{10.5194/acp-26-12521-2026}

\paperentry{Kim C-h et al.\ 2026}
{Block-missing monitoring data, five imputation models, and an honest sensitivity test}
{Atmosphere}
{Eleven months of 20-minute measurements of 22 designated odour compounds at a livestock
farm, a wastewater plant and an organic-waste plant in Eumseong, South Korea, with
\textbf{multi-week to multi-month block gaps} --- the failure mode that actually afflicts
long-running low-cost networks, as opposed to the scattered single-point dropout most
imputation papers assume. Compound-specific performance was assessed by \textbf{block
holdout} against BiLSTM, BiGRU, TCN, Transformer and HistGradientBoosting. Odour activity
values were computed \emph{primarily from observed concentrations}, with the fully imputed
series used only as a sensitivity comparison to quantify interpolation-induced bias, which
is the correct discipline and is rarely followed. Ammonia was excluded outright for a low
valid-observation rate. Odour contribution was dominated by volatile fatty acids and
trimethylamine rather than hydrogen sulfide. Transferable to PM networks in method, not in
analyte.}
{10.3390/atmos17090868}

\paperentry{Cai J et al.\ 2026}
{Subway air microbiome is homogeneous at network scale and heterogeneous at platform scale}
{Environ Sci Technol}
{Stratified cluster sampling of \textbf{43 underground stations across 13 lines} of the
Shanghai subway, collecting total suspended particles and PM$_{2.5}$ \emph{simultaneously}
at entrances, halls and platforms, then integrating taxonomy, transcriptional potential,
PM$_{2.5}$ chemistry, microclimate and network topology. Bacteria dominate both size
fractions and most transcriptional signal; eukaryotes are strongly enriched in TSP,
\textbf{a clean demonstration of particle-size filtering of the biological load}. Dominant
taxa (\textit{Staphylococcus}, \textit{Bacillus}) show weak abundance--transcription
coupling, so abundance is a poor proxy for activity. Diversity and composition are
essentially uniform across the network --- consistent with passenger mixing --- while
finer-scale structure appears within stations. For exposure work this means a single
station is a reasonable network sentinel for community composition and a poor one for
microenvironment. Weakness: one city, one season not stated, no human outcome.}
{10.1021/acs.est.6c00174}

\paperentry{Rosas et al.\ 2026}
{The PM$_{2.5}$/PM$_{10}$ ratio separates two aerosol regimes that mass alone cannot}
{Environ Sci Pollut Res Int}
{Continuous PM$_{2.5}$, PM$_{10}$ and particle-bound PAH at a near-coastal urban site in
Merida, Mexico, joined to FIRMS fire detections and HYSPLIT back-trajectories, contrasting
the March--May regional biomass-burning season against the June--August African dust
season. Concentrations are the wrong discriminator: \textbf{PM$_{2.5}$ and PM$_{10}$ rise
only modestly during dust}, but the \textbf{PM$_{2.5}$/PM$_{10}$ ratio falls}, marking the
coarse mineral contribution, while biomass burning loads the fine mode and the combustion
tracers. A network reporting PM$_{2.5}$ mass alone would score the dust season as the
milder of the two and miss the toxicologically distinct composition entirely. Part I of a
two-part study, so the health framing in the title is anticipatory rather than delivered
here. Single site, single urban domain.}
{10.1007/s11356-026-38193-x}

\paperentry{Seong \& Hoque 2026}
{Attachment fraction ranks metal $>$ wood $>$ glass, and carpet is two microenvironments}
{Front Pediatr}
{Batch attachment and centrifugal detachment of \textit{Corynebacterium} sp.\ on carpet,
wood, metal and glass, with near-surface velocity fields resolved by particle image
velocimetry and surfaces characterised for roughness, contact angle and porosity.
Attachment fraction rose with exposure time on all materials, reaching \textbf{$\sim$0.59
on metal, $\sim$0.48 on wood, $\sim$0.36 on glass}. The useful observation is
morphological: \textbf{carpet generates two distinct microenvironments} --- a
higher-velocity outer-fibre region and a sheltered low-velocity inter-fibre region --- so a
single resuspension coefficient for ``carpet'' is a category error. Wood produced the most
spatially heterogeneous velocity field, glass the most uniform. Relevance to PM is
indirect but real: resuspension source terms in indoor exposure models are usually fitted
per material with no flow-field justification. One organism, no PM measurement, replicate
count not stated.}
{10.3389/fped.2026.1895305}

\band{Exposure assessment \& modelling\hfill 4 records}{Amber}

\paperentry{Ullah et al.\ 2026}
{Transboundary smoke moves almost nobody across China's standard and almost everybody
across WHO's}
{Environ Pollut}
{Daily PM$_{2.5}$ from \textbf{51 cities} in Yunnan, Guangxi and Guangdong for
March--April 2015--2019, gridded onto WorldPop, with the Indo-China Peninsula fire
contribution isolated by paired GEOS-Chem runs with and without the fires. The
population-weighted share of days above the \textbf{WHO 2021 24\,h guideline
(15\,$\mu$g/m$^3$) reached 78--96\% every year}; above \textbf{China's Grade II standard
(75\,$\mu$g/m$^3$) it was 1--6\%}, an average daily exposed population of about
\textbf{6 million}, under 3\% of the corridor's residents. The two benchmarks describe
different worlds from identical data, which is the paper's real contribution to monitoring
policy. Principal weakness: the attribution runs at \textbf{2$^\circ$ $\times$
2.5$^\circ$}, roughly 200\,km, so the fire contribution is a regional average imposed on
city-scale observations and cannot support any within-province ranking.}
{10.1016/j.envpol.2026.129081}

\paperentry{Arunachalam et al.\ 2026}
{Building-sector combustion mortality is three times prior estimates, and it is mostly NO$_2$}
{Research Square (preprint)}
{CMAQ Decoupled Direct Method sensitivities of NO$_2$, O$_3$ and PM$_{2.5}$ to
commercial and residential building-sector precursor emissions for the contiguous US
(2021), propagated through BenMAPR. Net burden \textbf{$\approx$14{,}000 premature deaths
per year (95\% CI 7{,}900--19{,}000)}: \textbf{12{,}000 from NO$_2$}, \textbf{3{,}100 from
PM$_{2.5}$}, and \textbf{1{,}000 deaths avoided} through O$_3$ reductions. Residential gas
alone accounts for \textbf{$\approx$7{,}900}. The headline ``three times higher than
previously reported'' is carried almost entirely by the NO$_2$ term, so the result is a
statement about which NO$_2$ concentration--response function you accept, not about
building emissions being larger than thought. Preprint, not peer reviewed. The negative
O$_3$ term is the sign of a titration effect and is correctly reported rather than
suppressed, which is a point in the paper's favour.}
{10.21203/rs.3.rs-10758198/v1}

\paperentry{Zhao C et al.\ 2026}
{Mass fell a third; toxicity per microgram did not move}
{Environ Pollut}
{Sixteen priority PM$_{2.5}$-bound PAHs in Kunming, compared between a higher- and a
lower-PM$_{2.5}$ stage. Raw declines: \textbf{PM$_{2.5}$ $-$32.7\%,
$\Sigma$PAHs $-$27.4\%, BaP$_{\mathrm{eq}}$ $-$31.4\%}; covariate-adjusted,
\textbf{$-$28.8\%} and \textbf{$-$33.5\%}. But the particle-normalised intensity
\textbf{BaP$_{\mathrm{eq}}$/PM$_{2.5}$ changed by $-$7.6\% ($p$=0.274)} and
BaP$_{\mathrm{eq}}$/$\Sigma$PAH by only $-$6.5\% ($p$=0.026). Four-to-six-ring species held
above \textbf{81\%} of $\Sigma$PAH and BaP plus dibenzo[a,h]anthracene supplied over
\textbf{82\%} of BaP$_{\mathrm{eq}}$ in both stages; \textbf{40.3\%} of simulated adult
incremental lifetime cancer risks still exceeded $10^{-6}$. PMF patterns are read
conservatively as source-\emph{related} rather than as resolved sources, which is the
right caution. The inference is threatened by the two-stage design: ``stage'' bundles
season, meteorology and policy, and no counterfactual separates them.}
{10.1016/j.envpol.2026.129094}

\paperentry{Leung et al.\ 2026}
{Abandoned wells, indoor VOCs, and a pilot honest enough to report its own limits}
{Indoor Air}
{Passive indoor sampling for 13 VOCs in \textbf{17 households (October 2024)} and
\textbf{15 households (February--March 2025)} in southwestern Ontario, with abandoned-well
exposure estimated by inverse-distance-squared weighting over wells within 3\,km and
self-reported acute symptoms over the previous six months and during sampling. This is
gas-phase, not particulate --- carried here because the exposure-assignment design is the
one a distributed indoor PM study would use and because the MAPSH project measures both.
At $n$$<$20 per round with Spearman correlations and no personal monitoring, the study is
powered to describe indoor concentrations against Canadian guidelines and not to detect a
well-proximity association; the six-month symptom recall window compounds that. Read as a
feasibility and concentration-range report.}
{10.1155/ina/8041546}

\band{Occupational \& indoor\hfill 4 records}{Clay}

\paperentry{Ibrahim 2026b}
{The case for routine indoor PM monitoring, argued without data}
{Qeios (preprint)}
{A commentary arguing that Malaysia's ICOP IAQ 2010 framework, though it contains
preventive elements, is operated as a complaint-triggered investigation regime, and that
\textbf{particulate matter and outdoor-to-indoor pollutant transport} should join CO$_2$ as
routinely monitored parameters in building management. The specific climatic argument ---
that hot, humid conditions couple ventilation, condensation and biological growth in ways a
temperate-derived code handles badly --- is sound and underexploited in the LCS literature.
Everything else is assertion: single author, non-peer-reviewed platform, no primary data,
and the Malaysian office, hospital and school studies it cites are described rather than
synthesised. Included as an argument about monitoring design, not as evidence.}
{10.32388/b506md}

\paperentry{Wei J et al.\ 2026}
{Welding fume immunology, with the exposure-assessment gap named explicitly}
{Front Immunol}
{Mini review of metal-rich fine and ultrafine welding-fume particles as a stimulus to
airway epithelium, alveolar macrophages, neutrophils, monocytes and circulating immune
cells. Consistent signals across human exposure studies, controlled inhalation, cell models
and workplace investigations: acute-phase proteins, cytokines, chemokines and neutrophilic
responses, with some evidence of altered T-helper activity and blunted responsiveness to
bacterial stimuli. TRPA1/TRPV1 sensory signalling is proposed as a rapid route to cough and
airway reflex, and the review is careful to say that direct welding-fume-specific human
evidence for that pathway is limited. The stated obstacle to synthesis is the one that
matters here: \textbf{fume composition and particle metric vary so much between studies
that biomarker results cannot be pooled}. No quantitative estimate, no pooling, no
systematic search protocol reported.}
