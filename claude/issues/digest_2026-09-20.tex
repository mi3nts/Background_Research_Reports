\section*{\sffamily\bfseries\color{Deep}Signal of the day}
\vspace{-1.5mm}{\color{Amber}\rule{\linewidth}{1.1pt}}\vspace{2.5mm}

\begin{keybox}
{\sffamily\bfseries\small\color{Deep}Twenty years of western US speciation: the part of
PM$_{2.5}$ that is disappearing is the part optical sensors see, and the part that is
growing is the part they miss.}\\[1.4mm]
\footnotesize
Kaur et al.\ (\textit{Atmos Pollut Res}) reconcile Positive Matrix Factorization with
Chemical Mass Balance across \textbf{12 monitoring sites in five western states over
2000--2020}, resolving five to eight source factors per site. PM$_{2.5}$ declined
significantly at ten of the twelve (\textbf{--0.38 to --0.037\,$\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$ per
year}; Boise and Commerce City are the exceptions). The decline is not uniform across
composition. It is carried by \textbf{secondary ammonium nitrate, secondary ammonium
sulfate, and the two elemental-carbon-rich factors} --- the direct product of NO$_x$,
SO$_2$ and mobile-source regulation. EC-rich fell \textit{despite} rising vehicle miles
travelled, which isolates per-vehicle emission control rather than reduced activity as
the mechanism. Winter OC-rich, the residential-wood-combustion proxy, showed the largest
seasonal decline. \textbf{Dust went the other way and rose at most sites}, which the
authors attribute to regional aridity and unpaved-surface activity.

Take that compositional divergence to a low-cost network and it becomes a slow,
systematic drift in network bias. A nephelometric or optical-scattering node responds
efficiently to submicron secondary and combustion aerosol --- accumulation-mode particles
near the peak of its scattering response --- and poorly to coarse mineral dust, which is
both larger than the effective upper cut of most low-cost inlets and a weaker scatterer
per unit mass. A sensor calibrated against a gravimetric reference in 2005 was calibrated
against an aerosol dominated by the fraction it detects well. \textbf{The same sensor,
same calibration, measuring the 2025 aerosol, is reading a mixture in which the
undetected fraction has grown} --- so the negative bias against true mass widens with
time, and does so fastest in exactly the arid western sites where dust is rising. The
correction is not a better fit; it needs a composition or size term the node has no
channel for.

The number to distrust is any individual factor's 20-year trajectory. CSN speciation
methods changed within the record, and while reconciling two apportionment techniques
constrains the artefact it does not remove it: part of what a factor does over two
decades is what the instrument did.
\end{keybox}

\section*{\sffamily\bfseries\color{Deep}What changed today}
\vspace{-1.5mm}{\color{Amber}\rule{\linewidth}{1.1pt}}\vspace{2.5mm}

\footnotesize
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{A transboundary instrument for PM$_{2.5}$, and a design worth stealing.}
Kwon and Kim (\textit{BMC Public Health}) instrument South Korean PM$_{2.5}$ with
\textbf{wind direction crossed with upwind Chinese monitor levels}, breaking the
endogeneity between local pollution and local economic activity that contaminates
ordinary time-series estimates. A \textbf{1\,$\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$ rise raises 65+ mortality
0.17\% and respiratory hospitalisations 5.1\%}; the observed 5.58\,$\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$
national decline over 2015--22 is valued at \textbf{USD 14.14 billion} on avoided
life-years alone. The exclusion restriction is not defended against the obvious threat
--- the synoptic conditions that import Chinese aerosol are themselves cold, dry and
independently hazardous --- and no confidence intervals are reported, so nothing from
this paper is plotted. The structure still transfers: for any dense network sited in a
downwind receptor region, transport is a ready-made exposure instrument.
\item \textbf{A 4,441-patient cancer-survival cohort where the cause-specific effect
exceeds the all-cause effect.} Zhu et al.\ (\textit{Environ Res}) followed stage I--III
gastric cancer resections in Zhejiang for a median 3.83 years. Per 1\,SD of 36-month
pre-surgery PM$_{2.5}$, all-cause mortality \textbf{HR 1.15 (95\% CI 1.06--1.25)}; the
\textbf{gastric-cancer-specific subdistribution HR was 1.31 (1.20--1.44)} under Fine--Gray
with non-gastric deaths as competing events, which is the correct handling and is
routinely skipped in this literature. A cause-specific effect larger than the all-cause
effect is internally coherent but unusual, and wants replication outside one province
before it is read as tumour biology rather than as a competing-risks artefact.
\item \textbf{The instrumentation axis went dark, and the reason is infrastructural, not
seasonal.} \textbf{The PubMed sensing query returned zero}, OpenAlex returned HTTP 429 and
arXiv HTTP 406 --- so the entire instrumentation and atmospheric-measurement side of this
window rests on the Crossref-by-ISSN leg, which deposits titles without abstracts. Of the
twelve admitted records, \textbf{five assert no finding at all}, and among them are the two
most operationally relevant titles of the day: a spatiotemporal characterisation of
pollutants \textbf{within subway stations} and a process-resolved characterisation of
\textbf{airborne mixtures in plastic-button manufacturing}. Both are on the deferred
re-query list. With OpenAlex per-work and Semantic Scholar both established as dead
recovery routes (18 and 19 September respectively), waiting is now the only route there is.
\item \textbf{Secondhand smoke COPD burden halved in rate and rose in count --- the
divergence that makes rate-only reporting misleading.} Huang et al.\ (\textit{Tob Induc
Dis}) report age-standardised mortality falling \textbf{11.36 (95\% UI 4.52--18.55) to
5.87 (2.29--9.72)} per 100,000 across 1990--2021 while absolute deaths rose, with China
carrying the largest absolute burden (\textbf{117,388}) and an SDI inflection at 0.42. The
binding limitation is inherited from GBD: secondhand-smoke exposure is modelled from
survey-reported household and workplace smoking, never from measured particulate or
cotinine, so every country contrast partly encodes survey quality rather than exposure.
\item \textbf{An eighty-study microplastics review that cannot be connected to an air
concentration.} Messina et al.\ (\textit{Front Toxicol}) find apoptosis across ten organ
systems with converging mechanisms --- oxidative stress, mitochondrial depolarisation, ER
stress, caspase activation. The included work is overwhelmingly \textbf{polystyrene
spheres at mass doses far above any modelled human airway deposition}, and the search
closed in September 2024. It establishes that these pathways \textit{can} be engaged, not
that ambient airborne microplastic does engage them. \textbf{No dose-response anchor to a
measured air concentration appears anywhere in the synthesis} --- which is the gap the
monitoring side, not the toxicology side, would have to fill.
\item \textbf{A record returns from rejection because a DOI was registered.} The toxic
metals in cannabinoid-vaping aerosols white paper (\textit{J Test Eval}) was excluded from
the 19 September issue --- on topic but with no DOI, the first rejection ever made on that
ground. The DOI has since been deposited and the record is admitted here. Its finding is a
sampling one and generalises past vaping: \textbf{substrate blank, not detector
sensitivity, sets the floor} for trace-metal PM measurement, and several otherwise-clean
trapping media impose enough airflow resistance that the device never activates.
\end{itemize}

\band{Respiratory \& allergic\hfill 1 record}{Sky}

\paperentry{Huang et al. 2026}
{Global, regional, and 204 countries burden of disease attributed to secondhand smoke-related COPD, 1990-2021: A secondary data analysis of the Global Burden of Disease Study 2021.}
{Tob Induc Dis}
{GBD 2021 secondary analysis, 1990-2021, with an ARIMA projection to 2031. Age-standardised mortality for secondhand-smoke-attributable COPD roughly halved, 11.36 (95\% UI 4.52-18.55) to 5.87 (2.29-9.72) per 100,000, and ASDR fell 234.01 (93.58-380.47) to 120.94 (47.2-197.93) - yet absolute deaths rose, the familiar ageing-and-population-growth divergence that makes rate-only reporting misleading. China carries the largest absolute mortality (117,388; 47,776-195,915), Papua New Guinea the highest rates. The SDI relationship is non-linear with an inflection at SDI 0.42. The binding limitation is inherited, not introduced: GBD secondhand-smoke exposure is modelled from survey-reported household and workplace smoking, not measured particulate or cotinine, so the uncertainty intervals understate exposure misclassification and every country contrast partly reflects differing survey quality. An ARIMA extrapolation on a modelled series compounds that. Relevant here because secondhand smoke is the one fine-particulate exposure with an intervention that works, and it anchors the indoor end of the PM dose-response.}
{10.18332/tid/230940}

\band{Reproductive \& developmental\hfill 1 record}{Sage}

\paperentry{Eick et al. 2026}
{Prenatal exposure to aromatic amines and associations with infant cognition in the ECHO.CA.IL cohorts.}
{J Expo Sci Environ Epidemiol}
{N=250 across two pooled prospective birth cohorts (IKIDS and Chemicals in Our Bodies). Six aromatic amines were detected in over half of second-trimester urine samples; infant visual recognition memory was measured by infrared eye tracking at 7.5 months, with log-transformed individual analytes and a molar sum entered in linear regressions and child sex examined as a modifier. The relevance to a PM watch is indirect and worth stating plainly: aromatic amines are combustion and tobacco-smoke products, cotinine is measured alongside them, so urinary AAs partly index particulate-bound combustion exposure rather than only consumer-product contact - but the study makes no air measurement and cannot separate the two routes. At N=250 with six correlated analytes and a sex-interaction analysis, this is hypothesis-generating; any single-analyte association at this sample size is underpowered for the multiplicity involved. Carried at tier C for the exposure biomarker panel, not for the cognitive estimate.}
{10.1038/s41370-026-00905-3}

\band{Mechanistic toxicology\hfill 1 record}{Violet}

\paperentry{Messina et al. 2026}
{Organ-specific apoptotic pathways induced by micro- and nanoplastics: a systematic review of in vitro toxicological evidence.}
{Front Toxicol}
{Eighty in vitro studies, PECOS framing, PRISMA reporting, search run 6 September 2024. Apoptosis recurs across gastrointestinal, hepatic, pulmonary, renal, reproductive, placental, neural, cardiovascular, dermal and haematological models, and the mechanisms converge: oxidative stress, mitochondrial depolarisation, ER stress, calcium dysregulation, MAPK and PI3K/AKT signalling, Bcl-2 family shifts and caspase activation, with ferroptotic and autophagic crosstalk. Particle size and surface chemistry modulate potency. Two limitations bound the inference hard. First, the search predates a fast-moving literature by two years. Second - and this is the one that matters for inhalation - the included work is overwhelmingly polystyrene spheres at mass doses far above any modelled human airway deposition, so the review establishes that these pathways can be engaged, not that they are engaged at ambient airborne microplastic levels. No dose-response anchor to a measured air concentration appears anywhere in the synthesis, which is the gap the monitoring side would have to fill.}
{10.3389/ftox.2026.1908508}

\band{Exposure assessment \& modelling\hfill 1 record}{Clay}

\paperentry{Kaur et al. 2025}
{Long-term PM$_{2.5}$ source apportionment at 12 sites in the western United States from 2000-2020.}
{Atmos Pollut Res}
{Twelve monitoring sites, twenty years, five to eight resolved factors per site. PM$_{2.5}$ fell at ten of twelve sites (-0.38 to -0.037 $\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$ per year; no decline at Boise or Commerce City), and the decline is carried by secondary ammonium nitrate, ammonium sulfate and the two EC-rich factors - exactly the sources NOx, SO$_2$ and mobile-source rules targeted. EC-rich fell despite rising vehicle miles travelled, isolating per-vehicle emission control rather than reduced activity. Winter OC-rich, the biomass-burning proxy, showed the largest seasonal decline. Dust rose at most sites. The weakness is the one the authors set out to address and only partly do: CSN speciation methods changed within the record, so a factor's 20-year trajectory is partly an instrument trajectory, and reconciling PMF with CMB constrains but does not eliminate that. For distributed sensing the finding that matters is compositional: the shrinking part of western PM$_{2.5}$ is the secondary and combustion mass an optical sensor sees reasonably well, while the growing part is dust, which nephelometric low-cost sensors under-report because coarse mineral particles scatter poorly at their sampling cut. Network bias against dust therefore worsens over time.}
{10.1016/j.apr.2025.102779}

\band{Sensing, forecasting \& instrumentation\hfill 1 record}{Amber}

\paperentry{Thomas et al. 2026}
{Procedural Challenges in the Analysis of Toxic Metals in Electronic Cannabinoid Delivery System Aerosols Produced for Vaping Cannabinoids.}
{J Test Eval}
{No subjects; a white paper reviewing ten years of trapping approaches. The central problem is that metal constituents sit in the $\mu$g/g range, so the trap itself and the sample-preparation consumables contribute more metal than the aerosol does unless materials are chosen against a metals blank - media validated for organic constituents are simply wrong here. The second constraint is mechanical: several otherwise-clean media impose enough airflow resistance that the device will not puff-activate, so the aerosol never forms. No quantitative inter-method comparison is offered, which is the paper's limitation - it maps the failure modes without ranking the survivors. The transferable point for distributed sensing is that sampling-substrate blank, not detector sensitivity, sets the floor for any trace-metal PM measurement, which is the same constraint that governs filter-based reference sampling behind a low-cost network. Note: this record was rejected from the 19 September issue for carrying no DOI; the DOI has since been registered.}
{10.1520/jte20250320}
