\section*{\sffamily\bfseries\color{Deep}Signal of the day}
\vspace{-1.5mm}{\color{Amber}\rule{\linewidth}{1.1pt}}\vspace{2.5mm}

\begin{keybox}
{\sffamily\bfseries\small\color{Deep}What people \emph{smell} moves protective behaviour
roughly five times more than what the network \emph{measures}. The last metre of a
monitoring system is perceptual, and it is currently unengineered.}\\[1.4mm]
\footnotesize
Slavik et al.\ (\textit{Environ Challenges}) linked survey responses from
\textbf{2{,}086 parents} in wildfire-prone western North America to three and a half
years of smoke-monitoring data (2020--2023). Parents who \emph{perceived} smoke exposure
took \textbf{1.11 more protective actions} (95\% CI 0.92--1.30) than those who did not,
whereas each \textbf{10\,$\mu$g/m$^3$ of measured PM$_{2.5}$} on smoke days bought only
\textbf{0.23 additional actions} (0.06--0.40) --- a $\sim$4.8-fold gap in favour of the
subjective channel. The measured-exposure response is also non-monotonic: actions rise,
plateau at moderate concentrations, then rise again. Since sub-visible smoke is
demonstrably harmful, a network that reports accurate concentrations into a population
that acts on sight and smell is delivering a fraction of its achievable health benefit.
Read against the two records in today's corpus that show \emph{mass} is the wrong
currency for the hazard --- Yadav et al.'s Agra year-long series, where PM$_{10}$ mass and
endotoxin are effectively uncorrelated ($r_s=0.10$, $p=0.75$) across an 8-fold seasonal
endotoxin swing, and Lei et al.'s eight-year speciated PM$_1$ record in Dublin --- the
day's through-line is that the two ends of the measurement chain are both weakly coupled
to the thing that matters. Composition is not mass, and communicated risk is not
measured risk.
\end{keybox}

\section*{\sffamily\bfseries\color{Deep}What changed today}
\vspace{-1.5mm}{\color{Amber}\rule{\linewidth}{1.1pt}}\vspace{2.5mm}

\footnotesize
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{The confidence-interval drought broke.} Jung et al.\ (\textit{Ann Rheum
Dis}) report the first peer-reviewed PM ratio estimate with an interval this watch has
seen in five issues: \textbf{OR 1.113 (95\% CI 1.017--1.218)} for rheumatoid arthritis
flare per monthly mean PM$_{2.5}$, across 12{,}583 outpatient visits from 1{,}070
patients, with a within-patient case-crossover sensitivity arm that reproduces the
association at $>$2 weeks of cumulative exposure. PM$_{2.5}$ was the only one of six
pollutants to survive. Six effect estimates carry intervals today against one on 5
August.

\item \textbf{Two well-powered nulls, and both are informative rather than empty.}
Sewor et al.\ found \emph{no} association between 48-hour personal PM$_{2.5}$ and blood
pressure in 622 rural Rwandan children --- at a \textbf{median exposure of
192.9\,$\mu$g/m$^3$}, roughly 40$\times$ the WHO annual guideline. The authors' own
reading is the right one: the exposure--response may simply be flat that far up the
global continuum, which is a statement about where monitoring effort buys epidemiological
information. Separately, Lees et al.\ (preprint) report a PM$_{2.5}$-component mixture
IRR of \textbf{1.31 (0.99--1.73, $p=0.06$)} for hospitalisation in 2{,}511 CKD patients
with \textbf{93\% of the WQS weight on nitrate alone} --- a null whose weight vector is
more interesting than its point estimate.

\item \textbf{Instrumentation side: three papers about the limits of what an instrument
can be asked to mean.} Liu et al.\ (\textit{ACP}) show that inferring the aerosol
mixing-state index $\chi$ from HTDMA $\kappa$ distributions works until externally mixed
\emph{intermediate}-hygroscopicity species violate the method's binary assumption, and
publish size-resolved error bounds so the retrieval can be used honestly. Tian et al.\
(\textit{AMT}) do the analogue for lidar-retrieved CCN profiles with a heterogeneity index
that flags when vertical layering has invalidated the homogeneous-composition assumption.
Yee et al.\ (\textit{AMT}) release UCB-GLOBES, $\sim$27{,}000 GC/EI-MS spectra --- and
report \textbf{$\sim$70\% of them still match nothing}, in their own library or in NIST.

\item \textbf{Imputation gets an honest negative.} Somnugpong's similarity-weighted
seasonal-anchor Stineman method improves on plain Stineman for the UCI dataset under MCAR
and MAR but \emph{does not} beat it on Beijing PM$_{2.5}$. The paper is framed as a
boundary condition rather than a method launch, which is the correct framing and a rare
one --- relevant to anyone reconstructing gappy low-cost sensor series.

\item \textbf{Preclinical cardiac remodelling is now measurable at IQR-scale exposure
contrasts.} Leiser et al.\ (\textit{JACC Adv}) find per-IQR PM$_{2.5}$ associated with
+0.52\% global longitudinal strain (0.20--0.83), $-$1.05\% LVEF per year
($-$1.73 to $-$0.37) and $-$1.39 left atrial volume index ($-$2.25 to $-$0.54) over a
median 4.3 years of serial echocardiography in 1{,}630 people. Note the sign pattern is
internally awkward --- GLS and LAVI move in directions that do not obviously cohere --- and
the paper does not resolve it.

\item \textbf{Sub-Saharan Africa contributed three records, the most in any window so
far} --- Mushore et al.\ (road-edge gradient \emph{reverses sign} between sites),
Makanjuola (563 street sweepers) and Sewor above, all three using handheld or wearable
optical instruments as the primary exposure measurement.
\end{itemize}

\section*{\sffamily\bfseries\color{Deep}Paper-level digest}
\vspace{-1.5mm}{\color{Amber}\rule{\linewidth}{1.1pt}}\vspace{1mm}

{\fontsize{7.4}{9}\selectfont\color{Slate}Ordered by cluster. Each entry gives the
methodological core and the finding that matters, not an abstract paraphrase.\par}

\band{Sensing, forecasting \& instrumentation\hfill 7 records}{Amber}

\paperentry{Somnugpong 2026}
{Similarity-weighted seasonal-anchor Stineman interpolation for gappy air-quality series}
{Environ Monit Assess}
{SW-SAST keeps a local Stineman anchor and selectively borrows cross-day information when
nearby daily analogues are compatible with the local estimate. Evaluated on UCI Air
Quality and Beijing PM$_{2.5}$ under MCAR, MAR and MNAR at 10--50\% missingness, 30
repeated runs per condition, against a univariate LSTM baseline and with contiguous
gap-length experiments. \textbf{It improves on plain Stineman for UCI, chiefly under MCAR
and MAR, and fails to beat it on Beijing.} The contribution is explicitly a boundary
condition on when seasonal borrowing helps, not a replacement interpolator --- which is
the right claim and an unusually disciplined one. Directly applicable to reconstructing
low-cost-sensor series where dropouts are neither random nor uniform.}
{10.1007/s10661-026-15770-8}

\paperentry{Lei et al.\ 2026}
{Eight-year speciated PM$_1$ record in Dublin: heating emissions versus transboundary transport}
{Atmos Chem Phys}
{Continuous chemically speciated PM$_1$, 2016--2023, resolving two distinct pollution
regimes: short intense events (hours, PM$_1>$100\,$\mu$g/m$^3$) driven by local solid-fuel
home heating, and moderate multi-day events ($<$60\,$\mu$g/m$^3$) from continental
transport. Annual PM$_1$ fell from 6.5 to $<$5.0\,$\mu$g/m$^3$, led by nitrate and
ammonium ($-$0.11 and $-$0.09\,$\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$\,yr$^{-1}$). \textbf{Transported
oxygenated organic aerosol rose (+0.34\,$\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$\,yr$^{-1}$)} while locally formed
OOA barely fell, so the composition shifted even as mass declined --- and 2023 showed a
heating-driven rebound. A demonstration that a mass-trend headline can be exactly wrong
about what a network is now measuring.}
{10.5194/acp-26-10927-2026}

\paperentry{Liu et al.\ 2026}
{From $\kappa$ to $\chi$: particle-resolved evaluation of hygroscopicity-based mixing-state retrieval}
{Atmos Chem Phys}
{PartMC-MOSAIC urban-plume ensembles spanning emissions, ageing and meteorology were used
to compare the particle-resolved reference mixing-state index against the Yuan--Zhao
retrieval from HTDMA $\kappa$ distributions, per diameter over 50--250\,nm. The retrieval
performs well for many populations but \textbf{systematically overestimates $\chi$ when
externally mixed intermediate-hygroscopicity components break its binary
more-/less-hygroscopic assumption}. The useful output is the size-resolved error
distribution, which the authors then apply as uncertainty bounds to long-term HTDMA
records at urban, continental and coastal sites. Model-derived error bars on an
observational retrieval --- the validation is only as good as PartMC's own composition
realism, which the paper does not independently test.}
{10.5194/acp-26-10965-2026}

\paperentry{Yee et al.\ 2026}
{UCB-GLOBES: open mass-spectral library for identified \emph{and} unidentified organic aerosol}
{Atmos Meas Tech}
{$\sim$27{,}000 GC/EI-MS spectra of trimethylsilyl derivatives of semi-volatile organic
aerosol from US and Central Amazon ambient samples plus laboratory SOA, catalogued
\emph{without regard to known chemical identity} and annotated with sample provenance and
predicted properties. Reanalysis of prior ambient data gave an 11\% net increase in
MS-based source categorisation for the southeastern US and lifted Central Amazon matches
from 136 to 375 of 1{,}513 analytes. \textbf{$\sim$70\% of UCB-GLOBES spectra match
nothing} in the library or in NIST --- the honest headline is the size of the unidentified
fraction, not the size of the library.}
{10.5194/amt-19-5027-2026}

\paperentry{Zhao et al.\ 2026}
{WHA air-LSTM: composite morbidity-driven pollution index for next-day outpatient forecasting}
{Biomed Environ Sci}
{A five-level composite index is built \emph{per city} from city-specific
exposure--response relationships across $>$223.7 million hospital visits, then fed with
temperature, humidity and visit history into an LSTM predicting next-day respiratory
outpatient load. Beijing $R^2=0.963$, RMSE 53.5; removing the index degrades RMSE by
44.1\%; performance holds on held-out cities. The index is fitted on morbidity and then
used to predict morbidity, so the incremental-value claim is partly circular --- the
external validation is what carries it. Attractive template for turning a dense sensor
network into an operational early-warning product.}
{10.3967/bes2026.062}

\paperentry{Li et al.\ 2026}
{Interpretable ML for 90-day paediatric allergic-rhinitis exacerbation from clinical and ambient data}
{Front Pediatr}
{Development cohort $n=2{,}000$ linking NHANES (2005--06, 2011--18) to EPA AQS and NOAA
data; external cohort $n=50$ from Wuhan. L2-regularised logistic regression was
prespecified and beat random forest, XGBoost, LightGBM and SVM under nested 5-fold CV.
Internal AUC 0.81 (0.74--0.88), external 0.71 (0.50--0.92). Baseline symptom score,
same-day PM$_{2.5}$ and total IgE dominate SHAP; \textbf{PM$_{2.5}>$75 vs
$<$35\,$\mu$g/m$^3$ gives OR 5.08 (3.45--7.47)}. The external cohort is 50 children on a
different continent --- the authors say plainly this is preliminary transportability, not
external validity, and they are right.}
{10.3389/fped.2026.1865399}

\paperentry{Tian et al.\ 2026}
{A heterogeneity index for when lidar-retrieved CCN vertical profiles stop being trustworthy}
{Atmos Meas Tech}
{ARM ground-based Raman lidar plus surface CCN measurements retrieve vertically resolved
CCN number concentration on the assumption of vertically homogeneous composition. A
$k$-nearest-neighbours classifier on lidar ratio and linear depolarisation ratio infers
dominant aerosol type by altitude, and a derived heterogeneity index quantifies vertical
complexity. Validated against HI-SCALE aircraft in situ data: \textbf{retrieval closure
degrades monotonically as the index rises}, so the metric functions as a self-diagnostic
flag on the retrieval. Single campaign, so the index thresholds are not yet portable.}
{10.5194/amt-19-5051-2026}

\band{Exposure assessment \& modelling\hfill 3 records}{Sky}

\paperentry{Mushore et al.\ 2026}
{Daily outdoor PM$_{2.5}$ and the road-edge effect across The Gambia, Kenya and Mozambique}
{Atmos Environ X}
{Multi-temporal personal exposure measurements (2022--23) interpolated to continuous
surfaces by inverse-distance weighting, validated leave-one-out at $R^2>$0.55, MAE
$<$4\,$\mu$g/m$^3$, RMSE $<$10\,$\mu$g/m$^3$; multi-distance buffers at 10\,m intervals to
1000\,m from major roads. Most sites exceed WHO guidance, The Gambia highest across all
land-cover classes. \textbf{The road-edge gradient reverses sign between sites} ---
PM$_{2.5}$ falls with distance in Rabai and Mozambique and \emph{rises} elsewhere,
implicating wetland, fire and industrial sources that a traffic-proximity exposure proxy
would misattribute entirely. IDW over sparse personal measurements is a weak spatial
model; the buffer result is the transferable part.}
{10.1016/j.aeaoa.2026.100463}

\paperentry{Helbich et al.\ 2026}
{Socioeconomic patterning of the urban external exposome depends on how the exposome is defined}
{Environ Int}
{4{,}640 adults across Bulgaria's five largest cities, with residential NO$_2$, PM, traffic
noise, green space, blue-space distance, impervious surface, walkability and night-time
light. City-specific latent profile analysis was compared against composite indices, then
both were regressed on individual- and area-level SES. High-risk profiles bundle low
green space, high air pollution, noise and imperviousness \emph{with} higher walkability.
\textbf{Higher area-level SES was positively associated with high-risk profiles in most
cities} --- the inverse of the standard environmental-justice expectation --- and composite
indices only partially reproduced the latent-profile findings. The methodological warning
is the finding: operationalisation choice changes the sign of the inequality conclusion.}
{10.1016/j.envint.2026.110419}

\paperentry{Yadav et al.\ 2026}
{Airborne endotoxin on PM$_{10}$ in Agra: an 8-fold seasonal swing that PM mass does not track}
{Research Square (preprint)}
{Full calendar year (Jan--Dec 2022) of ambient endotoxin by chromogenic LAL assay with
concurrent bacterial and fungal bioaerosol, PM$_{10}$ and meteorology in the semi-arid
Indo-Gangetic Basin. Monthly means run 0.92$\pm$0.35 (June) to 7.35$\pm$1.48\,EU\,m$^{-3}$
(December) on winter stagnation. Endotoxin correlates strongly with bacterial
concentration ($r_s=0.93$) and \textbf{not at all with PM$_{10}$ mass ($r_s=0.10$,
$p=0.75$)} --- mineral dust is not the carrier here. USEPA-model hazard quotients stay
well below unity (max 0.0198, children, December). Preprint, single city, LAL assays are
notoriously matrix-sensitive; the decoupling from mass is the durable point.}
{10.21203/rs.3.rs-10523351/v1}

\band{Cardiovascular \& metabolic\hfill 3 records}{Teal}

\paperentry{Leiser et al.\ 2026}
{Ambient PM$_{2.5}$ and preclinical change in cardiac structure and function}
{JACC Adv}
{1{,}630 participants with echocardiography at two visits (2008--11, 2015--18), median 4.3
years apart; residential PM$_{2.5}$ and NO$_2$ from spatio-temporal models as annual means
preceding each visit, adjusted for baseline and time-varying covariates. Per IQR of
PM$_{2.5}$: \textbf{global longitudinal strain +0.52\% (0.20--0.83), LVEF
$-$1.05\%/year ($-$1.73 to $-$0.37), relative wall thickness +0.12 unit (0.05--0.19),
left atrial volume index $-$1.39 ($-$2.25 to $-$0.54)}. NO$_2$ moves the same direction,
smaller. The LAVI direction sits awkwardly with a concentric-remodelling reading and the
paper does not reconcile it; treat the strain and RWT results as the load-bearing ones.}
{10.1016/j.jacadv.2026.103104}

\paperentry{Sewor et al.\ 2026}
{Personal household-air-pollution exposure and blood pressure in rural Rwandan children}
{Environ Res Health}
{622 children aged 8--15 from 626 biomass-cooking households, 48\,h personal PM$_{2.5}$
and black carbon by Ultrasonic Personal Aerosol Sampler, then standardised BP measurement.
Median 48\,h PM$_{2.5}$ \textbf{192.9\,$\mu$g/m$^3$} (IQR 119.7--336.0), BC
9.85\,$\mu$g/m$^3$. \textbf{No association with SBP or DBP} (PM$_{2.5}$--SBP 0.51\,mmHg
per 211.7\,$\mu$g/m$^3$ IQR, 95\% CI $-$0.46 to 1.49) and no clear effect modification by
age or sex. Yet 24\% of the children were hypertensive or had elevated BP against US
reference percentiles. Cross-sectional, and the exposure range sits entirely above the
region of the global exposure--response curve where slope is expected --- a null that
constrains where measurement effort is epidemiologically productive.}
{10.1088/2752-5309/ae8ff9}

\paperentry{Liu et al.\ 2026}
{Long-term ozone and cardiovascular disease: exposure windows, sex and region}
{China CDC Wkly}
{Meta-analysis of cohort studies from PubMed, Web of Science and Embase, stratified in the
way most prior reviews were not. Per 10\,$\mu$g/m$^3$ ozone: CVD mortality
RR 1.0105 (1.0027--1.0185), morbidity RR 1.0578 (1.0289--1.0874). The exposure-window
split is the result: \textbf{warm-season ozone RR 1.0186 (1.0119--1.0253) versus an
annual-average RR of 0.9971 (0.9781--1.0164)} --- annual averaging destroys the signal.
Female RR 1.1135 (1.0057--1.2328) versus male 1.0707 (0.9855--1.1633); Asia estimates are
imprecise. Co-pollutant record; no PM estimate is reported. The averaging-window lesson
transfers directly to PM network design.}
{10.46234/ccdcw2026.161}

\band{Reproductive \& developmental\hfill 1 record}{Coral}

\paperentry{Li et al.\ 2026}
{Prenatal NO$_2$ and stillbirth in 3.98 million Iranian mother--baby pairs}
{Environ Pollut}
{3{,}982{,}304 pairs, 2013--2018, pregnancy-average NO$_2$ from satellite-based gridded
estimates averaged within 10\,km of the delivery hospital, analysed under both the Iranian
($\geq$22 weeks) and WHO ($\geq$28 weeks) stillbirth definitions. Per 10\,$\mu$g/m$^3$:
\textbf{OR 1.206 (1.192--1.220)} and \textbf{1.106 (1.088--1.125)} respectively,
essentially unchanged on adjustment for PM$_{2.5}$ and ozone. The exposure--response is
monotonic with \emph{steeper slope at low concentrations}. A 10\,$\mu$g/m$^3$ target would
avert 7{,}686 (7{,}242--8{,}127) stillbirths, an attributable fraction of 25.8\%.
Exposure assigned at the delivery hospital rather than residence is a substantial
misclassification channel in a country with heavy referral flows, and the effect size is
large enough to warrant scepticism. Co-pollutant, not PM.}
{10.1016/j.envpol.2026.128908}

\band{Occupational \& indoor exposure\hfill 2 records}{Clay}

\paperentry{Makanjuola 2026}
{Personal PM exposure and spirometry among 563 Southwest Nigerian street sweepers}
{Research Square (preprint)}
{Cross-sectional, non-smoking sweepers aged 18--60 from Ado-Ekiti, Ibadan and Ikeja;
personal PM$_{2.5}$ and PM$_{10}$ by handheld PMS5003 at breathing height; spirometry to
ATS standard. Mean PM$_{2.5}$ \textbf{103.55$\pm$72.03\,$\mu$g/m$^3$} and PM$_{10}$
105.09$\pm$73.95, roughly 20$\times$ and 7$\times$ the WHO 2021 annual guideline.
PM$_{2.5}$ correlates inversely with FEV$_1$/FVC ($r=-0.14$, $p=0.001$) but
\textbf{the association is fully attenuated in the adjusted model ($\beta=-0.0001$,
$p=0.286$)}. A bare PMS5003 without RH correction at these loadings is a real
measurement-error problem the paper does not address, and PM$_{2.5}\approx$PM$_{10}$ is
itself a red flag for the instrument. Reported for the exposure characterisation, not the
health inference.}
{10.21203/rs.3.rs-9928829/v1}

\paperentry{Piedra et al.\ 2026}
{Informal employment versus cantonal satellite pollution as respiratory-symptom predictors}
{Front Public Health}
{1{,}807 Ecuadorian workers across four sectors, ECRHS symptom instrument cross-referenced
with Sentinel-5P retrievals; optimal spatial/temporal concentration percentile selected
per outcome by AIC; Firth penalised logistic regression. \textbf{Informal employment
dominates}: asthma aOR 12.56 (5.09--32.87), wheeze 6.78 (1.83--27.89), chronic cough 4.14
