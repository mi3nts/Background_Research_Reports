\section*{\sffamily\bfseries\color{Deep}Signal of the day}
\vspace{-1.5mm}{\color{Amber}\rule{\linewidth}{1.1pt}}\vspace{2.5mm}

\begin{keybox}
{\sffamily\bfseries\small\color{Deep}Run PurpleAir offline in the cold and the winter record goes missing: with SD-card logging and no WiFi, pilot co-locations recovered 100\,\% of data in Arizona but 57\,\% in Oklahoma and 10\,\% in South Dakota, with logging failing at mean (SD) ambient temperatures of 4.4 (3.2) and 7.7 (2.0)\,$^\circ$C.}\\[1.4mm]
\footnotesize
Columbia University, \textit{Sensors}; Strong Heart Air Study, ten PurpleAir monitors co-located per site in three American Indian communities before a cardiovascular cohort deployment. SD logging was chosen to keep data under community control rather than in the vendor cloud. Outdoor tests suggested a WiFi connection protects logging at low temperature; 4\,$^\circ$C cold-room runs reproduced the failure without WiFi.
\textbf{Where it is weak:} a pilot completeness finding, not an accuracy evaluation; firmware and card models are not in the abstract, and the mechanism (power, card or firmware) is not isolated.
\textbf{Why it matters here:} the missingness is not at random --- it concentrates in cold, stagnant, wood-smoke weeks, so any exposure--response analysis built on offline PurpleAir data in cold climates is biased toward the clean season unless the gap is modelled.\par
\end{keybox}

\section*{\sffamily\bfseries\color{Deep}What changed today}
\vspace{-1.5mm}{\color{Amber}\rule{\linewidth}{1.1pt}}\vspace{2.5mm}

\footnotesize
\begin{itemize}

\item \textbf{Sensirion over 19 months.} Two SEN54 and two SPS30 against a Fidas 200S (Czech Hydrometeorological Institute): raw PM$_{2.5}$ R$^2$ 0.87 (RMSE 5.53\,$\mu$g/m$^3$), PM$_{10}$ only 0.50 (13.70); a MARS correction on dew point deficit lifted them to 0.92 (3.29) and 0.68 (8.25). Mineral-dust PM$_{10}$ episodes stayed invisible.

\item \textbf{Calibrate one, deploy many --- fails for PM$_{10}$.} TU Delft, five multi-modal devices in Brussels: tree ensembles win on the source unit but lose their advantage over the \emph{uncalibrated} sensor for PM$_{10}$ once transferred; a cross-sensor residual monitor catches injected drift in reconstructable channels. Separately, an Upper Silesia SDS011 study emulates a heated inlet in software (R$^2$ 94\,\%, RMSE 3.69--3.79\,$\mu$g/m$^3$ against the heated twin) --- a low-cost target, not a reference.

\item \textbf{Telomeres: significant until clustered.} Northern Thailand, 179 rural adults: relative LTL +0.050 T/S (0.012--0.089) per IQR of 0--30-day PM$_{2.5}$ and +0.079 (0.035--0.123) at 0--60 days --- but the authors' wild cluster bootstrap over eight site--date groups leaves all five windows non-significant (p 0.25--0.79).

\item \textbf{Randomised stove, no PM$_{2.5}$ gradient.} HAPIN Peru follow-up, 101 children aged 3--5: oscillometry did not differ between LPG and biomass arms; no association with prenatal or postnatal personal PM$_{2.5}$; postnatal CO tracked worse reactance (AX7 +0.041 z per ppm, 0.003--0.078).

\item \textbf{Exposure basis (x1).} Five of today's 27 records rest on low-cost sensors, all on the measurement side, none producing a health estimate. Of the four forest estimates, only the HAPIN contrast rests on measured (personal) exposure; the Geneva interaction uses a modelled residential surface and the Thai telomere paper does not state its PM$_{2.5}$ source. \emph{No effect estimate today comes from sensor-measured exposure.}

\end{itemize}

{Eur J Public Health}
{Population sample of 1,006 children (2-9 y) and 1,154 adolescents (10-17 y) in Geneva. \paperentry{Sichuan CDC seven sentinel hospitals DLNM multi-pathogen ARI positivity humidity RSV PM$_{2.5}$ H. influenzae FDR}
{Lagged environmental effects on multi-pathogen respiratory positivity: a distributed-lag nonlinear analysis of seven Sichuan sentinel hospitals, China}
{Research Square}
{Preprint. Weekly panel of 23,014 acute respiratory infection cases from seven Sichuan sentinel hospitals (2024-2026); quasi-Poisson DLNMs (6-week lag) gave cumulative RRs for the 90th versus 10th percentile of each exposure with within-pathogen FDR control. \paperentry{HAPIN Peru LPG vs biomass RCT follow-up oscillometry 3-5 year olds R7 X7 AX7 PM$_{2.5}$ CO}
{Effects of Cooking With Liquefied Petroleum Gas or Biomass on Lung Function Among Pre-School Aged Children in Peru: A Prospective Follow-Up of a Randomized Trial.}
{Pediatr Pulmonol}
{Follow-up of 101 children aged 3-5 whose mothers were randomised to LPG stoves or usual biomass during pregnancy and infancy (HAPIN Peru). \paperentry{Wuhan PLWH vitamin D RCT PM$_{1}$ PM$_{2.5}$ PM$_{10}$ CD4 CD19 DNA methylation mediation 2023}
{Vitamin D and Particulate Matter-Associated Immune Responses in People Living with HIV: A Randomized Trial with Exploratory DNA Methylation Analyses.}
{Nutrients}
{Double-blind placebo-controlled trial of daily vitamin D in 80 people living with HIV (75 analysed, 271 follow-ups, March-July 2023), PM averaged over the 72 h before each 28-day visit. Supplementation attenuated the negative PM-cell count associations: the PM-associated change in CD4+ T cells differed from placebo by 3.37\%, 2.21\% and 1.96\% per 10 $\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$ PM$_{1}$, PM$_{2.5}$ and PM$_{10}$, and in CD19+ B cells by 4.71\%, 2.98\% and 2.65\%. 173 vitamin D-related CpG sites changed; 25 mediated 28-79\% of effects on TLR4, IL-6 and TNF-alpha. {10.3390/nu18213710}

\paperentry{Chiang Mai rural adults biomass burning PM$_{2.5}$ lag 0-90 d leukocyte telomere length 8-OHdG wild cluster bootstrap}
{Association Between Ambient PM$_{2.5}$ Exposure and Leukocyte Telomere Length Among Rural Adults: Cross-Sectional Study in Northern Thailand.}
{Int J Mol Sci}
{179 rural adults in biomass-burning northern Thailand; PM$_{2.5}$ averaged over five windows up to the blood draw. Positive associations with telomere length appeared only for the 0-30, 0-60 and 0-90 day windows: per window-specific IQR (8.13 and 7.25 $\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$) relative LTL was 0.050 T/S higher (95\% CI 0.012-0.089) at 0-30 days and 0.079 (0.035-0.123) at 0-60 days. {10.3390/ijms27219869}

\paperentry{Warsaw Fischer 344 rats 7-day whole-body biodiesel exhaust B7 SHB20 DPF colon oxidative stress}
{Colon Effects of 7-Day Whole-Body Inhalation Exposure of Fischer 344 Rats to Emissions from First- and Second-Generation Biodiesel Fuels with and Without a Particle Filter.}
{Int J Mol Sci}
{Male Fischer 344 rats breathed whole-body diesel exhaust from first-generation B7 or second-generation SHB20 (FAME plus hydrotreated vegetable oil) blends for 7 days, 6 h/day, at measured PM of 2.19 and 2.13 mg/m3 without a particle filter and 0.17 and 0.19 mg/m3 with one. Doses are two to three orders above ambient, exposure is the whole mixture (NOx and VOCs included), and the abstract gives no effect sizes or group sizes. Relevance: indirect - the DPF arm cuts PM about 12-fold without abolishing effects, pointing at the gas phase or ultrafines that mass-based sensors do not see.}
{10.3390/ijms27219724}

\paperentry{Chiang Mai veterinary review PM$_{2.5}$ lung pathology short vs long lifespan animal models One Health sentinel}
{Comparative Susceptibility to PM$_{2.5}$-Induced Pulmonary Pathology: A Lifespan-Based Perspective on Animal Models.}
{Biology (Basel)}
{Review contrasting PM$_{2.5}$ lung disease in short-lifespan laboratory rodents, typically dosed high for a small fraction of life, with long-lived species breathing ambient air for decades. Rodents show acute, often reversible inflammation; long-lived animals show cumulative particle bioaccumulation, airway remodelling and spontaneous neoplasia. The authors concede lifespan is confounded with exposure intensity and duration and cannot be separated from them, and propose veterinary sentinel cohorts, cross-species dosimetric scaling and in-tissue particle burden measurement. Relevance: indirect - sentinel-animal cohorts would need long, spatially resolved ambient exposure records, the kind dense low-cost networks can supply.}
{10.3390/biology15212042}

Not peer reviewed; dose, route detail, group sizes and effect sizes are absent from the abstract, and crystalline silica is an occupational particle with toxicity specific to its surface chemistry, not ambient PM. Relevance: indirect - mechanistic link from particle lung injury to systemic autoimmunity; no measurement content.}
{10.21203/rs.3.rs-11403486/v1}

\paperentry{Korea Institute of Toxicology yellow dust BALB/c MH-S alveolar macrophage AKT Nrf2 mitochondrial ROS}

\band{Sensing, forecasting \& instrumentation\hfill 7 records}{Amber}

\paperentry{TU Delft Brussels multi-modal low-cost sensor calibration transfer DANN drift detection residual monitor}
{Data-Driven Calibration and Reference-Free Drift Detection for Multi-Modal Sensor Systems.}
{Sensors (Basel)}
{Five custom multi-modal devices at two Brussels sites (9-68 days each), reference data for only a subset. MLR, Elastic Net, random forest, gradient boosting and a domain-adversarial network (DANN) were benchmarked for O$_3$, NO$_2$, PM$_{2.5}$ and PM$_{10}$ on the source unit and transferred to other units at the same and the other site, with block-bootstrap intervals. A cross-sensor residual monitor flagged injected gradual drift in reconstructable channels (PM$_{1}$, UV, T, RH, CO). Five devices and short records; drift was injected, not real ageing, and the monitor cannot localise the faulty channel. {10.3390/s26217016}

\paperentry{Strong Heart Air Study PurpleAir SD card logging failure cold temperature WiFi Oklahoma South Dakota Arizona}
{Temperature-Associated SD Card Logging Failures in PurpleAir Monitors: Findings from the Strong Heart Air Study.}
{Sensors (Basel)}
{Pilot co-locations of ten PurpleAir monitors at each of three American Indian community sites in a cardiovascular cohort, logging to SD card without WiFi to keep data under community control. Data recovery was 100\% in Arizona but 57\% in Oklahoma and 10\% in South Dakota; logging failed when ambient temperature fell to a mean (SD) of 4.4 (3.2) C and 7.7 (2.0) C.
