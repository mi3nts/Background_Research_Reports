\section*{\sffamily\bfseries\color{Deep}Signal of the day}
\vspace{-1.5mm}{\color{Amber}\rule{\linewidth}{1.1pt}}\vspace{2.5mm}

\begin{keybox}
{\sffamily\bfseries\small\color{Deep}Assimilating 33 rural low-cost PM$_{2.5}$ nodes into WRF-Chem cut the Haryana mean fractional bias from $-$116.7\,\% to $-$56.8\,\% and recovered the Severe AQI category in \textasciitilde95\,\% of Delhi cases, where the control run called none.}\\[1.4mm]
\footnotesize
CUPI-G network, November 2024 stubble-burning season, twice-daily observations, 3D-Var. The control run predicted \emph{no} Severe case at Delhi, Punjab or Haryana; with assimilation the category was right in about 95\,\% of Delhi cases and 100\,\% of Punjab and Haryana cases. A second experiment using a 1.5\,$\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$ observation error --- the reference-monitor value rather than the 10\,\% assumed for the sensors --- improved further.
\textbf{Where it is weak:} a preprint; one month of one burning season; and no co-location statistics for the CUPI-G nodes, so the 10\,\% observation error is asserted, not demonstrated. That the tighter error helped more is as consistent with the nodes being better than assumed as with them being worse.
\textbf{Why it matters here:} the case for source-region low-cost deployment is usually made on spatial coverage. This makes it on forecast skill for the one thing an air-quality warning exists to call --- the extreme episode --- against a control that misses it entirely.\par
\end{keybox}

\section*{\sffamily\bfseries\color{Deep}What changed today}
\vspace{-1.5mm}{\color{Amber}\rule{\linewidth}{1.1pt}}\vspace{2.5mm}

\footnotesize
\begin{itemize}

\item \textbf{Brake wear decouples number from mass.} Wheel-end sampling on a plug-in hybrid: 36\,\% of decelerations carried 80.3--92.7\,\% of the PM$_{10}$ mass, while sustained heating raised sub-100\,nm number 48.3--98.1-fold and kept it elevated through cooling. Single-scattering albedo 0.606--0.766 and mass absorption efficiency 0.364--0.747\,m$^2$\,g$^{-1}$, evolving by pad formulation. A hundredfold ultrafine excursion a mass sensor cannot see, with pad-dependent optics inside the 630\,nm band nephelometers use.

\item \textbf{Two low-cost networks carrying health inferences, neither with a co-location statistic.} Kinshasa, 424 households, locally corrected PurpleAir PA-II-FLEX: asthma prevalence 15.8\,\%, adjusted OR 1.210 for high PM$_{2.5}$ (\emph{p}$<$0.001, no CI), and \emph{no} association with major-road proximity (OR 0.939), read as near-homogeneous traffic pollution across a dense road network. Attica, one IoT smart bench standing for a district, feeding WHO AirQ$+$. \textsc{backfill} for the first, entry 12 March.

\item \textbf{Cancer signals only in subgroups of a null.} NIH-AARP, 140,457 women, 3,088 endometrial and 1,582 ovarian cases: nothing overall, then PM$_{2.5}$ and endometrial cancer in Atlanta (HR 1.70, 1.16--2.48) and Pennsylvania (HR 1.15, 1.02--1.29) per 5\,$\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$. State-stratified subgroups of a null result; the authors call them suggestive and attribute the geography to composition. \textsc{backfill}, entry 15 June.

\item \textbf{A measured decay timescale for smoke optics.} FIREX-AQ airborne actinic flux: $k$(387\,nm) 0.02--0.03, absorption Angstrom exponent 3--5, and half-lives of 13$\pm$3\,h ($k$), 16$\pm$4\,h (volume absorption cross section) and 17$\pm$4\,h (co-albedo) with plume age, published as a parameterization. This is the quantity that makes an optical sensor's smoke response depend on distance from the fire.

\item \textbf{Composition keeps outrunning mass.} California: 23 PM$_{2.5}$ metals over 11 sectors, inversion cutting mean fractional error 0.835 $\rightarrow$ 0.511; miscellaneous sources 9.1\,\% of emissions but 57.7\,\% of the hazard index, cadmium-driven. Plus a karst-city rare-earth record and a MERRA-2/CAMS component intercomparison, both metadata-only.

\item \textbf{Pedestrian scale, measured not assumed.} PM$_{2.5}$ at 2-second resolution across 90 cells of 120$\times$150\,m in a dense historic district, with CFD at 1.5\,m: short-term crowd mobility and economic activity associated with concentrations on top of building and road form, synergies varying by season. No effect sizes, and the instrument class behind 2-second logging is not named.

\item \textbf{A limit below background.} Nine authors contest a 4\,$\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$ 8-h TWA guidance value for biodurable nanomaterials, partly on the ground that it sits at or below typical ambient PM$_{2.5}$ and current measurement capability. Whatever the toxicology, the metrological objection stands on its own. The letter's industry affiliations are not stated in the record.

\item \textbf{Exposure basis (x1).} Of 28 records, 4 rest on a low-cost sensor or network --- the highest count this watch has logged in a single day --- and 2 on reference monitors. Health side with a recoverable basis (n=7): 86\,\% measured, 14\,\% modelled, 0\,\% proxy, but that measured share is three low-cost networks and one personal monitor, none reporting a co-location statistic in its abstract.

\end{itemize}
