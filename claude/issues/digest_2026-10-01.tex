\section*{\sffamily\bfseries\color{Deep}Signal of the day}
\vspace{-1.5mm}{\color{Amber}\rule{\linewidth}{1.1pt}}\vspace{2.5mm}

\begin{keybox}
{\sffamily\bfseries\small\color{Deep}Bulk PM$_{2.5}$ oxidative potential understates the oxidative
burden of its own size fractions --- metal--organic complexation makes PM$_1$ and PM$_{1-2.5}$ antagonistic
when mixed.}\\[1.4mm]
\footnotesize
Liu and colleagues (\textit{ES\&T}, tier A) tested roadside PM$_1$ and PM$_{1-2.5}$ separately and mixed. Every
endpoint was sub-additive: interaction factor \textbf{0.65--0.81} for ascorbate OP, \textbf{0.80--0.93} for DTT
OP, \textbf{0.10--0.88} for cellular ROS and \textbf{0.43--0.78} for mitochondrial superoxide. Metal-free
biomass-burning PM was strictly additive; Cu$^{2+}$--humic-acid mixtures reproduced the antagonism (IF
0.22--0.95), and EPR showed complexed Cu forming non-linearly with the HA:Cu ratio --- which explains why
earlier studies disagree on whether size fractions add. \textbf{Caveats:} one roadside site, extract assays,
one model complex; whether lung lining fluid reproduces the complexation is untested. \textbf{Why it matters
for sensing:} OP is being proposed as the toxicity-weighted complement to mass. If bulk PM$_{2.5}$ OP is
not the sum of its parts, OP measured on a PM$_{2.5}$ filter cannot be read as dose; a size cut at 1\,$\mu$m
is the cheaper fix, and an optical counter's size bins are already there to stratify by.\par
\end{keybox}

\section*{\sffamily\bfseries\color{Deep}What changed today}
\vspace{-1.5mm}{\color{Amber}\rule{\linewidth}{1.1pt}}\vspace{2.5mm}

\footnotesize
\begin{itemize}

\item \textbf{Coal retirements delayed, deaths priced} (medRxiv, tier A): 20 of 50 US coal plants due to retire
before 2035 now retire later or not at all; plant-attributable PM$_{2.5}$ over 2026--2035 adds
\textbf{1,256} Medicare deaths (1,136--1,376), \textbf{3.1$\times$} the original burden. Eighteen of the
20 sit in balancing authorities with hyperscale data-centre demand. The interval carries
concentration--response uncertainty only.

\item \textbf{Metastatic prostate cancer} (33,384 men, 11 US registries): cardiovascular mortality HR
\textbf{1.04} (1.02--1.07) per IQR PM$_{2.5}$; \textbf{1.19} (1.05--1.34) high vs low PM$_{2.5}$ in
ADT-treated men. \textbf{ELSA} (12,235 adults 50+): PM$_{2.5}$ and PM$_{10}$ exceedance-day counts
\textbf{null} for 14-year cognitive decline once the annual mean is adjusted; NO$_2$ exceedance days were not.
\textbf{Crime, China} (483,718 events): every one of six pollutants positive on the day --- the pattern
shared confounding produces.

\item \textbf{Urban greening is a weak PM sink.} Direct leaf sampling in Delhi puts urban-forest removal at
\textbf{527} t PM$_{2.5}$ a year, \textbf{1.08\,\%} of emissions, and at \textbf{$\sim$24\,\%} of what the
deposition-velocity method predicts ($\sim$4.6\,\% for PM$_{10}$), with leaf loading saturating in winter.

\item \textbf{Hazard decoupled from mass.} PM$_{2.5}$-bound PFASs in Jinan (163 filters) were numerically
higher on clean than on slightly polluted days in summer and autumn; in Beijing, haze, firework and dust
events rank differently by hazard index, carcinogenic risk and OP. Neither ordering is recoverable from a
mass sensor.

\item \textbf{Six of 30 records are metadata-only (20\,\%)}, down from 32\,\% on 30 Sep: three Elsevier, two
RSC (\textit{Environ Sci: Atmos}, teaser only) and one \textit{KRCP}. The most watch-relevant is an
\textbf{indoor air study in low-income Indianapolis housing}. Retry list grows by six.

\end{itemize}

\band{Neuro / mental health\hfill 2 records}{Deep}

\paperentry{Han et al. 2026}
{Air pollution and criminal behavior: a nationwide case-crossover analysis in China}
{Int J Epidemiol}
{Time-stratified case-crossover of 483,718 crime events in China (2013-2019) with conditional logistic regression per pollutant and weighted-quantile-sum for the mixture. Per IQR on the event day, crime rose 1.66\% for PM$_{2.5}$, 1.88\% PM$_{10}$, 4.03\% NO$_2$, 2.03\% CO, 2.81\% SO$_2$ and 1.30\% O$_3$; WQS index +3.22\% per unit. Associations were stronger in the cold season, the north and lower-SES regions, and for violent crime and theft. Limitation: every pollutant is 'positive', which is what shared confounding by weather, daylight and outdoor activity patterns looks like; exposure is assigned at a coarse scale to events of unknown offender residence; no CIs in the abstract. Relevance: low - a behavioural endpoint with acute timing, where personal or hyperlocal exposure would separate PM from co-pollutants.}
{10.1093/ije/dyag214}

\paperentry{Bloomberg et al. 2026}
{Daily Exceedance of World Health Organization Air Pollutant Guidelines and Cognitive Aging}
{medRxiv (preprint)}
{English Longitudinal Study of Ageing, 12,235 adults 50+ (waves 1-10, 2002-2023). Exposure is the number of days above WHO daily guideline values in the year before baseline, modelled at the residence, adjusted for the annual mean. NO$_2$ exceedance days (75th percentile vs median) predicted faster 14-year decline: memory -0.09 SD (-0.17 to -0.01), fluency -0.10 SD (-0.19 to 0.00). PM$_{2.5}$ and PM$_{10}$ exceedance days were not associated with decline or baseline cognition. Limitation: exceedance count and annual mean are highly collinear, so the 'peak beyond mean' effect is fragile; one exposure year; attrition in ageing cohorts is cognition-dependent; preprint. Relevance: moderate - a null for PM peak frequency is exactly what high-time-resolution networks are positioned to retest.}
{10.64898/2026.09.23.26363787}

\paperentry{Cosenza et al. 2026}
{Associations of Outdoor Air Pollution With Cause-specific Mortality in Metastatic Prostate Cancer: A cohort study}
{medRxiv (preprint)}

{10.64898/2026.09.29.26364034}

\band{Mechanistic toxicology\hfill 5 records}{Violet}

\paperentry{Liu et al. 2026c}
{Bulk PM$_{2.5}$ Masks Oxidative Potential and Reactive Oxygen Species Responses by Metal-Organic Complexation in PM$_{1}$ and PM$_{1}$-2.5 Mixtures}
{Environ Sci Technol}
{Roadside PM$_{1}$ and PM$_{1}$-2.5 were tested separately and mixed. Mixing is antagonistic on every endpoint - interaction factor 0.65-0.81 for ascorbate OP, 0.80-0.93 for DTT OP, 0.10-0.88 for cellular ROS and 0.43-0.78 for mitochondrial superoxide - whereas metal-free biomass-burning PM is strictly additive. Limitation: one roadside site, extract-based assays, and Cu-HA as the single model complex; whether in vivo lining fluid reproduces the complexation is untested. Relevance: high - bulk-PM$_{2.5}$ OP, the metric proposed as a toxicity-weighted complement to mass, understates the oxidative burden of its size fractions; size-resolved sampling is needed to interpret it.}
{10.1021/acs.est.6c09837}

\paperentry{Jin et al. 2026}
{ANKRD22 contributes to NF-kB-associated inflammatory responses in alveolar type II epithelial cells during PM$_{2.5}$-triggered airway inflammation}
{Front Immunol}
{PM$_{2.5}$ raised ANKRD22 in alveolar type II cells in a mouse airway-inflammation model and in A549 cells. AT-II-specific ANKRD22 knockout mice had less BALF leukocytosis and neutrophilia, histological inflammation and IL-6/CXCL1; siRNA knockdown in A549 lowered IL6, IL8, IL1B, CXCL1 and CXCL2. PM$_{2.5}$ increased p50, phospho-IKKb, IkBa and RELA, and the non-canonical p52/RELB arm, all attenuated by ANKRD22 loss. Limitation: particle source, dose and dosimetry are not given in the abstract, A549 is a poor AT-II surrogate, and the knockout readouts are acute. Relevance: low - mechanism only; no exposure metric that a monitoring network could map onto.}
{10.3389/fimmu.2026.1894350}

\paperentry{Kim et al. 2026}
{Acorus gramineus Solander Preserves Corneal Epithelial Barrier Integrity against PM$_{2.5}$-Induced EMT-Like Remodeling}
{J Pharmacopuncture}
{Human corneal epithelial cells grown as 3D spheroids were exposed to PM$_{2.5}$ with or without an Acorus gramineus root extract. PM$_{2.5}$ destabilised spheroids without marked cytotoxicity, lowered transepithelial resistance, disrupted ZO-1, ZO-3 and occludin, induced EMT-like markers (N-cadherin, vimentin, MMPs) and cytokines, with TGF-b/Smad activation; the extract reversed these. Limitation: particle source and concentration are not stated in the abstract, no in vivo or tear-film model, and the therapeutic arm is a crude herbal extract. Relevance: low - the ocular surface is an underexamined direct-contact target, but nothing here quantifies dose.}
{10.3831/kpi.2026.29.3.314}

\paperentry{Onoda et al. 2026}
{Gene-environment interaction in neurodevelopment: Setd5 haploinsufficiency and prenatal carbon nanoparticle exposure synergistically induce ASD-like phenotypes}
{Part Fibre Toxicol}
{Pregnant C57BL/6N dams received intranasal carbon black nanoparticles (95 ug/kg) or water on GD5 and GD9; offspring were genotyped wild-type or Setd5+/- and analysed by sex. Only male Setd5+/- offspring exposed to CBNP showed a higher proportion with ASD-like behaviour (reduced social-novelty exploration, fixation on a familiar mouse) with selective loss of parvalbumin interneurons; either factor alone had minimal effect. Limitation: a single dose by intranasal bolus, a model particle without the metals or organics of ambient PM, and 'proportion of individuals' outcomes in small groups; group sizes are not in the abstract. Relevance: low-moderate - a gene-by-exposure synergy implies population risk is concentrated in susceptible genotypes that a mean exposure-response hides.}
{10.1186/s12989-026-00705-5}

\paperentry{Liu et al. 2026d}
{Metabolite-Associated Inflammation during Combined Exposure to Cooking Oil Fumes and Atopic Dermatitis: A Multi-Organ Spatial Metabolomics Study}
{Environ Sci Technol}
{A mouse model of atopic dermatitis with cooking-oil-fume co-exposure, profiled by untargeted and spatial metabolomics with in situ immunofluorescence across lung, blood, heart and kidney. AD+COF gave the largest rises in IL-6 and TNF-a and the largest metabolite shifts: succinate accumulation, xanthine and uric-acid depletion, glucose perturbation and spatial lipid remodelling, correlated across organs. Limitation, stated by the authors: no tracing, receptor blockade or mitochondrial assays, so inter-organ signalling is associative, and the high-concentration regimen limits environmental extrapolation; COF particle size and mass are not given in the abstract. Relevance: low - indoor cooking PM is a large exposure class, but this design cannot inform a dose metric.}
{10.1021/acs.est.6c11745}

\paperentry{Munteanu et al. 2026}

\paperentry{Liu et al. 2026a}
{Persistent cold air pools and associated sustained haze pollution in Taiyuan Basin}
{J Environ Sci (China)}

Events carry stronger surface inversions, larger valley heat deficit and suppressed ventilation; PM$_{2.5}$ peaks lag heat-deficit intensification by 12 h (R = 0.55). Limitation: R = 0.55 on a single basin, with emissions held implicit, so the 12-h lag is descriptive. Relevance: moderate - a 12-h lead from a sounding-derived metric is a usable forecasting feature, and basin networks must site nodes at more than one elevation to see the pool.}
{10.1016/j.jes.2026.03.027}

\paperentry{Wang et al. 2026a}
{Organosulfate-driven amplification of secondary organic aerosol formation from isoprene oxidation intermediates: Coupled effects of SO$_2$ and relative humidity}

\paperentry{Zheng et al. 2026}
{Comparative analysis of multiple health risks from different air pollution events}
{J Environ Sci (China)}

\paperentry{Mao et al. 2026}
