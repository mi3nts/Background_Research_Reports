\section*{\sffamily\bfseries\color{Deep}Signal of the day}
\vspace{-1.5mm}{\color{Amber}\rule{\linewidth}{1.1pt}}\vspace{2.5mm}

\begin{keybox}
{\sffamily\bfseries\small\color{Deep}A community-priced particle counter matched a
research-grade CPC at $R^2>0.99$ across three units --- and in the same issue, a year of
GPS-tracked personal sensors shows that knowing where a child actually went buys almost
nothing over knowing which of two rooms they were in.}\\[1.4mm]
\footnotesize
Wang et al.\ (\textit{Aerosol Sci.\ Technol.}) collocated three Community Condensation
Particle Counters, three optical particle counters and one Sensirion SPS30 (in a TSI
BlueSky package) at an urban regulatory station for roughly four months at
$>$98\% data recovery. Against a research-grade reference CPC the cCPCs returned
$R^2>0.99$ with concentration ratios near unity and a \textbf{3\% coefficient of variation}
across the three units --- reference-comparable 0.005--2.5\,$\mu$m \emph{number}
concentration at a price that permits a network, which is the measurement the low-cost
optical family structurally cannot make. The OPCs tracked reference PM$_{2.5}$ and
PM$_{10-2.5}$ volumes but \emph{on different regression slopes}, so no single mass
conversion serves both fractions; the SPS30 tracked PM$_{2.5}$ and failed outright on
coarse mass. One site, one season, no drift or humidity-regime generalisation.\par
\vspace{1.2mm}
Set against it, Hsu et al.\ (\textit{Environ Pollut}) ran 918 person-days of GPS-equipped
personal PM$_{2.5}$ sensors on northern Taiwanese schoolchildren over a year, BAM-1020
calibrated to $R^2=0.996$, and built six time-weighted microenvironment models. The fully
GPS-resolved model reached $R^2=0.619$; a model using \emph{only} GPS-identified home and
school reached \textbf{0.616}. Essentially the entire gain comes from correctly identifying
two microenvironments, and resolving every visited location adds nothing measurable. Read
together the two records bound a network design from both ends: the instrument side has
more headroom than the siting side, and a deployment that spends its budget on trajectory
resolution rather than on sensor class is optimising the term that does not move. Hsu's
validation is internal against concurrent personal measurement, and whether the models
reduce exposure--health misclassification is explicitly not tested.
\end{keybox}

\section*{\sffamily\bfseries\color{Deep}What changed today}

\footnotesize
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{PubMed indexed 26 records all day. The issue was carried by the other two
legs.} A no-term control on \texttt{[Date - Entry]} 2026/09/12 returns \textbf{26 records
registry-wide}, against 1{,}135 on 8 September. The health axis returned one hit and the
sensing axis none; the PubMed connector, run independently, returned exactly the same one
and zero. Weekend indexing is near-absent, which is a property of the source and not of
the field. Europe PMC (17) and Crossref-by-ISSN (28) supplied 20 of the 21 records.

\item \textbf{Half a million adults, and the mediator explains almost none of it.}
The China Kadoorie Biobank (\textit{J Hazard Mater}) followed 490{,}172 participants to
15{,}614 incident fractures over a median 12.0 years, with calcaneal quantitative-ultrasound
BMD in 32{,}545. PM$_{2.5}$ gave HR \textbf{1.09 (95\% CI 1.05--1.13)} per
10\,$\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$ and contributed 70\% of the mixture effect by quantile g-computation.
BMD fell 0.26--0.31\,SD per pollutant principal-component increment but mediated only
\textbf{5.8--8.6\%} of the fracture association --- so whatever the pathway is, it is mostly
not bone density.

\item \textbf{A well-powered null on wildfire smoke and fetal growth, reported as a null.}
20{,}034 ECHO births across 30 sites (\textit{Environ Epidemiol}) with daily
census-tract wildfire-specific PM$_{2.5}$: no association with term birth weight,
birth-weight-for-gestational-age $z$-score or small-for-gestational-age, and no consistent
sensitive window in distributed-lag or trimester models. The one positive is
large-for-gestational-age, OR \textbf{1.015 per smoke day (1.001--1.029)}, borderline and
biologically unexplained. Tract-level assignment of a plume-scale exposure attenuates
toward the null --- the standing argument for denser measurement.

\item \textbf{Two records point the same way on composition.} Source-apportioned PM$_{2.5}$
metals at 71 northern-Taiwan sites (\textit{preprint}) give a mean mass of only
19.33\,$\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$ but a combined lifetime incremental cancer risk of
$2.60\times10^{-4}$ and a children's hazard index of 4.65 against 0.69 in adults, driven by
As and Cr from industry and non-exhaust traffic. A Delhi PM$_{2.5}$ water-soluble extract
(\textit{Apoptosis}) drives proliferation, ROS and multinucleated giant cells in MCF7 cells.
Both say mass concentration misranks the bioactive fraction.

\end{itemize}

\band{Neuro / mental health\hfill 1 record}{Deep}

\paperentry{WTC responder exposure mixture 2026}
{Urban exposures, frailty, and mental illness in World Trade Center Health Program responders}
{Nat Commun}
{18,734 WTC Health Program general responders, later-life residential exposures. A decile increment in the joint PM$_{2.5}$/temperature/greenness mixture raises frailty index by 0.56\% (95\% CI 0.14-0.98) and PTSD score by 0.31\% (0.07-0.55); effects are larger in responders with higher original WTC exposure, read as acquired biological vulnerability. The estimates are percentage differences on continuous scores, not ratio metrics, so they are small in absolute terms and the mixture decile has no physical unit - a decile shift is not a concentration. Residential assignment at annual resolution cannot separate PM$_{2.5}$ from the temperature and greenness terms it co-varies with. For distributed sensing the relevant point is that the mixture framing needs co-located PM, thermal and greenness data at a resolution annual products cannot give.}
{10.1038/s41467-026-77401-7}

\band{Cardiovascular \& metabolic\hfill 1 record}{Teal}

\paperentry{California PM$_{2.5}$ diabetes mortality 2026}
{Associations of PM$_{2.5}$ exposure with diabetes-related mortality for California residents}
{Front Public Health}
{60,824 diabetes-related deaths (ICD-10 E11, primary or contributory) and 119,053 matched controls from California vital records, 2010-2021. Per 2.65 $\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$ IQR increase in the one-year rolling mean PM$_{2.5}$, conditional logistic regression gave OR 1.18 (95\% CI 1.15-1.22), rising to 1.21 (1.17-1.25) after NO$_2$ adjustment - the association strengthening on co-pollutant adjustment is itself a signal that NO$_2$ is acting as a negative confounder here. The design's weakness is structural: controls are drawn from the same mortality file and are people who had not yet died, so both arms are decedents and the contrast is timing of death, not death versus survival. The \$31.2 million per 100,000 avoided-loss figure rests on that design.}
{10.3389/fpubh.2026.1903823}

\band{Respiratory \& allergic\hfill 1 record}{Sky}

\paperentry{PM$_{2.5}$ lung cancer never-smokers review 2026}
{Breathing carcinogens: PM$_{2.5}$ air pollution and lung cancer in never-smokers, a narrative review}
{J Thorac Dis}
{Narrative review of PM$_{2.5}$ and lung cancer in never-smokers, PubMed/MEDLINE 2000 to May 2026 plus reference-list snowballing. The central claim is mechanistic rather than quantitative: PM$_{2.5}$ acts predominantly as a tumour promoter rather than a direct mutagen, operating through inflammatory signalling, metabolic reprogramming and epigenetic change, and is associated with more advanced stage at diagnosis and worse survival after resection. Lung cancer in never-smokers is described as molecularly and immunologically distinct, with screening and precision-oncology implications. No pooled estimate, no risk-of-bias assessment and no search-yield count are reported, so it carries no quantitative weight and the selection is author-curated by design. Useful as a mechanism map, not as evidence.}
{10.21037/jtd-2026-1264}

\band{Reproductive \& developmental\hfill 1 record}{Sage}

\paperentry{ECHO prenatal wildfire PM$_{2.5}$ 2026}
{Prenatal exposure to wildfire fine particulate matter and fetal growth outcomes in the United States: Findings from the ECHO Cohort}
{Environ Epidemiol}
{20,034 births (2006-2020) across 30 ECHO sites with daily census-tract wildfire PM$_{2.5}$ and smoke-day counts. Mean pregnancy wildfire PM$_{2.5}$ and smoke days were not associated with term birth weight, birth-weight-for-gestational-age z-score or small-for-gestational-age, and distributed-lag and trimester models showed no consistent sensitive window. The single positive signal is large-for-gestational-age, OR 1.015 per smoke day (95\% CI 1.001-1.029), which is borderline and biologically unexplained. This is a well-powered null and should be read as one rather than as a failure; the binding limitation is tract-level assignment of a plume-scale exposure, which attenuates any true effect toward the null. Exactly the misclassification that dense low-cost networks exist to reduce.}
{10.1097/ee9.0000000000000526}

\band{Mechanistic toxicology\hfill 3 records}{Violet}

\paperentry{Berberine PM$_{2.5}$ breast cancer 2026}
{Berberine shows potential in mitigating PM$_{2.5}$-induced breast cancer progression by inducing DNA damage and inhibiting error-prone DNA repair pathways}
{Apoptosis}
{Water-soluble Delhi PM$_{2.5}$ applied to MCF7 breast cancer cells increased proliferation, migration and ROS and produced multinucleated giant cells, i.e. genomic instability. Transcriptomic profiling gave a PM$_{2.5}$ carcinogenic signature enriched in MAPK, ROS, metabolic, lysosomal and ribosomal pathways, with BIRC5, WSB1 and RCC1 dysregulated in patient tumours and reversed by berberine. The inference is weak on two counts: only the water-soluble fraction was used, which discards the insoluble carbonaceous and metal-bound mass that carries much of PM$_{2.5}$ oxidative potential, and no dose-response or in vivo confirmation is reported. Relevant to sensing only as a reminder that mass concentration is a poor proxy for the bioactive fraction.}
{10.1007/s10495-026-02423-x}

\paperentry{AS-IV vs PM vascular senescence 2026}
{Replicative and Air Pollution-Induced Senescence: Telomere Dysfunction, Mitochondrial Stress, and Modulatory Effects of Astragaloside IV in Endothelial and Vascular Smooth Muscle Cells}
{Cardiovasc Toxicol}
{HUVECs and human coronary smooth muscle cells under replicative senescence (passages 6-7, 10-11, 17-20) and fine-dust-induced premature senescence (48 h, 50 and 100 ug/mL certified urban PM$_{10}$-like reference material), then Astragaloside IV at 50 uM for 24 h. Both cell types showed SA-beta-gal positivity, telomere shortening, p16INK4a/p21Cip1 activation and mitochondrial dysfunction; fine dust drove premature senescence, telomere shortening, TERRA upregulation and acute mitochondrial impairment mainly in endothelial cells, with smooth-muscle cells relatively resistant. AS-IV partially rescued endothelial but not smooth-muscle damage. The dose is the problem: 50-100 ug/mL in culture is orders of magnitude above any inhaled ambient mass dose, so the mechanism is plausible and the magnitude is not transferable.}
{10.1007/s12012-026-10173-0}

\paperentry{Nasal PM$_{2.5}$ testicular pyroptosis 2026}
{Nasal Exposure to Environmental PM$_{2.5}$ Drives Testicular Pyroptosis and Tissue Damage: Evidence from Inhibitor Intervention and Multi-Source Data}
{Res Sq (preprint)}
{24 male Sprague-Dawley rats in four arms of six: saline, Caspase-1 inhibitor Z-YVAD-FMK, intranasal PM$_{2.5}$ 10 mg/kg, and both, for four weeks. PM$_{2.5}$ reduced sperm count and motility and raised abnormality rate, with elevated ASC, Caspase-1 and gasdermin D in testis, all reversed by the inhibitor - a coherent NLRP3/Caspase-1/GSDMD pyroptosis chain. Reanalysis of GEO GSE189187 (GC-2spd cells, 3 vs 3) gave 217 DEGs enriched in interferon/antiviral processes, with Hmox1 up and Stat1 down. n=6 per arm and a 3-vs-3 public dataset cannot support hub-gene claims, and intranasal instillation at 10 mg/kg bears no quantitative relation to ambient inhalation. Preprint, no peer review.}
{10.21203/rs.3.rs-8701799/v1}

\band{Exposure assessment \& modelling\hfill 4 records}{Clay}

\paperentry{Benin PM$_{2.5}$-water grid screening 2026}
{Grid-based screening of fine particulate matter and water-optical conditions in Southern Benin: the Cotonou, Lake Nokoue, and Porto-Novo Corridor}
{Environ Monit Assess}
{A 1 km screening framework over 2,589 grid cells in the Cotonou-Lake Nokoue-Porto-Novo corridor, 2001-2022. Mean PM$_{2.5}$ was 32.17 $\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$ for 2001-2022 and 32.70 $\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$ for 2018-2022, roughly six times the WHO annual guideline, with no corridor-wide trend surviving false-discovery-rate correction. Sentinel-2 NDTI tracked field turbidity (Spearman rho 0.488, 95\% CI 0.369-0.590) and a red-edge ratio tracked chlorophyll-a (0.432, 0.295-0.544) against 19 stations. Six cells co-flagged at the 80th percentile, but the count swings from 16 at the 75th to zero at the 90th - the threshold, not the data, sets the answer. No ground PM monitor exists in the corridor, so the PM$_{2.5}$ layer is wholly unvalidated: a concrete argument for low-cost reference-grade co-location in West Africa.}
{10.1007/s10661-026-15908-8}

\paperentry{Vilnius grey-green PM$_{2.5}$ SHAP 2026}
{Unraveling associations between grey-green spatial trade-offs and PM$_{2.5}$ concentrations using explainable machine learning}
{J Environ Manage}
{Vilnius elderships 2016-2024 under a Grey-Green Space Coordination Index with LISA clustering and XGBoost-SHAP. GGSCI is negatively associated with PM$_{2.5}$; persistent high-high PM$_{2.5}$ clusters sit on imbalanced grey-green structures. SHAP flips sign by range: NDVI above \textasciitilde{}0.3 contributes negatively in balanced classes, while population density above \textasciitilde{}2,000 km-2 and nighttime light above \textasciitilde{}18 contribute positively in imbalanced ones. The awkward result, stated but not resolved, is that the most imbalanced GGSCI classes show the lower pollution - which inverts the paper's own framing and points to confounding by peri-urban land use. SHAP attributions are model explanations, not causal effects, and the PM$_{2.5}$ field is itself modelled at a resolution the index outruns.}
{10.1016/j.jenvman.2026.130895}

\paperentry{Taiwan PM$_{2.5}$-bound metals 71 sites 2026}
{PM$_{2.5}$-Bound Metals in a High-Tech Industrial Area of Northern Taiwan: Spatial Distribution, Sources, and Health Risks}
{Res Sq (preprint)}
{71 ambient sites sampled across two campaigns (2019, 2020) in a northern Taiwan high-tech industrial region. Mean PM$_{2.5}$ was 19.33 +/- 8.97 $\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$, higher in autumn (23.51) than summer (14.57), with hotspots near industrial parks; total metals averaged 736.85 +/- 751.20 ng/m3, dominated by Zn, Ba, Cu, Pb and Mn. PMF gave fossil-fuel combustion 33.1\%, soil and road dust 29.3\%, metal industry 22.3\% and tyre/brake wear 22.3\%. Monte Carlo risk assessment returned a combined lifetime incremental cancer risk of 2.60e-4, driven by As and Cr, and a children's hazard index of 4.65 against 0.69 in adults, mostly via dust ingestion. A preprint, not peer-reviewed; source percentages sum past 100\% as printed; two short campaigns cannot support a lifetime risk integral. The usable claim is that PM$_{2.5}$ mass alone misranks risk where composition varies - the case for composition-aware monitoring.}
{10.21203/rs.3.rs-10850297/v1}

\paperentry{GPS+LCS schoolchild exposure model 2026}
{Integrating GPS and Low-Cost Sensors to Enhance Personal PM$_{2.5}$ Exposure Modeling for Schoolchildren}
{Environ Pollut}
{918 person-days from northern Taiwan schoolchildren over one year with GPS-equipped portable PM$_{2.5}$ sensors, pre-deployment calibrated against a Met One BAM-1020 (mean R2 0.996, slope 0.962). Personal exposure was dominated by within-subject variability (ICC 0.125); school was the highest-concentration microenvironment at 22.7 $\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$. The fully GPS-resolved microenvironment model reached R2 0.619 (74.0\% accuracy) and a home-plus-school-only model 0.616 (71.7\%) - so essentially all the gain comes from identifying two microenvironments, and full trajectory resolution adds nothing. That negative result is the useful one for network design: it sets a ceiling on what finer location data buys. Validation is internal against concurrent personal measurement; whether the models reduce exposure misclassification in health analysis is explicitly not tested.}
{10.1016/j.envpol.2026.129149}

\band{Sensing, forecasting \& instrumentation\hfill 6 records}{Amber}

\paperentry{Climate-stratified PM$_{2.5}$ LightGBM 2026}
{Climate-stratified assessment of PM$_{2.5}$ using machine learning: Geographic controls dominate meteorological factors}
{PLoS One}
{49,997 monthly MERRA-2 samples (2019-2023) across five latitude-band climate zones under Leave-One-Climate-Out cross-validation. Tuned LightGBM reaches a global LOCO R2 of 0.367 (MAE 6.43 $\mu$g\,m$^{-3}$), but zone predictability ranges from R2 0.604 subtropical to 0.142 tropical. SHAP puts 64\% of importance on geographic coordinates against 23\% on meteorology, and a retrain ablation confirms the coordinates are not mere spatial autocorrelation - dropping lat/lon collapses mean R2 from 0.367 to -0.099. Temporal split validation gives 0.544, so LOCO is the harder test. The honest limitation is that the target is reanalysis PM$_{2.5}$, not measured PM$_{2.5}$, so the model is benchmarked against a model; and coordinates proxying emissions means the framework cannot transfer to a region whose emission geography it never saw.}
{10.1371/journal.pone.0347591}

\paperentry{cCPC/OPC/SPS30 field evaluation 2026}
{Field Evaluation of Community Condensation Particle Counters, Optical Particle Counters, and a Low-Cost Particle Sensor}
{Aerosol Sci Technol}
{Three community CPCs, three OPCs and one TSI BlueSky (Sensirion SPS30) co-located at an urban regulatory station for about four months, data recovery above 98\%. Against a research-grade reference CPC the cCPCs gave R2 above 0.99 with concentration ratios near unity and a 3\% coefficient of variation across the three units - genuinely reference-comparable ultrafine number at community cost. OPC fine and coarse volumes correlated with reference PM$_{2.5}$ and PM$_{10}$-2.5 but on different regression slopes, so a single mass factor cannot serve both fractions. The SPS30 tracked PM$_{2.5}$ and failed on coarse mass, which is the known optical limit restated with numbers. One season at one site, so no drift or humidity-regime generalisation; this is the most directly usable record of the day for network design.}

\paperentry{Shenzhen tower vertical PM$_{2.5}$ DL 2026}
{Leveraging multi-source observations for vertical PM$_{2.5}$ estimation: A deep learning fusion model}
{J Hazard Mater}
{An AM-CNN-BiLSTM fusion model estimating PM$_{2.5}$ at 200 and 300 m on the Shenzhen Meteorological Gradient Tower, July 2022-June 2023, combining lidar aerosol extinction coefficient, microwave-radiometer thermodynamic profiles, meteorology and tower PM$_{2.5}$. Chronological test split with an expanding window gave R2 0.87 at 200 m and 0.83 at 300 m, beating baselines; SHAP, ablation and sensitivity analysis all identify extinction coefficient as the dominant constraint, with RH, temperature and wind complementary.
