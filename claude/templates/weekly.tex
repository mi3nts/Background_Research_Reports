\footnotesize
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Measurement--epidemiology coupling.} <Whether any record this week validated an
instrument against a health endpoint, or whether the two literatures stayed separate.>
\item \textbf{Ultrafine and speciated metrics.} <UFP and composition-resolved records vs.\
total-mass records; the deposition-physics mismatch.>

Africa representation against their share of global PM burden.>

deployment performance or only co-located fit.>

is a reporting-bias signal, not a clean literature.>
